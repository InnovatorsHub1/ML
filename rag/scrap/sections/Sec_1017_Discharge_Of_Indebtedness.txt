Sec. 1017. Discharge Of Indebtedness

I.R.C. § 1017(a) General Rule — 
               
               
If— 

I.R.C. § 1017(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
an amount is excluded from gross income under subsection  (a) of section 108 (relating to discharge of indebtedness), and 


I.R.C. § 1017(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
under subsection (b)(2)(E), (b)(5), or (c)(1)  of section 108, any portion of such amount  is to be applied to reduce basis, 


then such portion shall be applied in  reduction of the basis of any property held
                     by the taxpayer at the  beginning of the taxable year following the taxable year in
                     which the  discharge occurs.
I.R.C. § 1017(b) Amount And Properties Determined Under Regulations


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  amount of reduction to be applied under subsection
                     (a) (not in excess of  the portion referred to in subsection (a)), and the particular
                     properties  the bases of which are to be reduced, shall be determined under  regulations
                     prescribed by the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(2) Limitation In Title 11 Case Or Insolvency — 
                  
                  
In  the case of a discharge to which subparagraph (A)
                     or (B) of section  108(a)(1) applies, the reduction in basis under subsection (a) of this  section shall not exceed
                     the excess of— 

I.R.C. § 1017(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the aggregate of the bases of the property held by the  taxpayer immediately after
                        the discharge, over 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the aggregate of the liabilities of the taxpayer  immediately after the discharge. 


The preceding sentence shall not apply to any reduction
                        in basis by reason of an election under section 108(b)(5).



I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3) Certain Reductions May Only Be Made In The Basis Of Depreciable Property


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Any  amount which under subsection (b)(5) or (c)(1)
                        of section 108 is to be applied to reduce  basis shall be applied only to reduce the basis of depreciable
                        property  held by the taxpayer. 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(B) Depreciable Property — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this section, the term “depreciable property” means any  property
                        of a character subject to the allowance for depreciation, but  only if a basis reduction
                        under subsection
                        (a) will reduce the amount of  depreciation or amortization which otherwise would
                        be allowable for the  period immediately following such reduction. 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(C) Special Rule For Partnership Interests — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this section, any interest of a partner in a partnership shall  be
                        treated as depreciable property to the extent of such partner's  proportionate interest
                        in the depreciable property held by such  partnership. The preceding sentence shall
                        apply only if there is a  corresponding reduction in the partnership's basis in depreciable
                        property  with respect to such partner. 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(D) Special Rule In Case Of Affiliated Group — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this section, if— 

I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(D)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a corporation holds stock in another corporation  (hereinafter in this subparagraph
                           referred to as the
                           “subsidiary”), and 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(D)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
such corporations are members of the same affiliated  group which file a consolidated
                           return under section 1501 for the taxable  year in which the discharge occurs, 


then such stock shall be treated as  depreciable property to the extent that such
                           subsidiary consents to a  corresponding reduction in the basis of its depreciable
                           property.



I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(E) Election To Treat Certain Inventory As Depreciable Property


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(E)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
At  the election of the taxpayer, for purposes of this section, the term  “depreciable
                           property” includes any real property which is described in  section 1221(a)(1). 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(E)(ii) Election — 
                        
                        
An  election under clause (i) shall be made on the taxpayer's return for the  taxable
                           year in which the discharge occurs or at such other time as may be  permitted in regulations
                           prescribed by the Secretary. Such an election,  once made, may be revoked only with
                           the consent of the Secretary. 



I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(F) Special Rules For Qualified Real Property Business Indebtedness — 
                     
                     
In  the case of any amount which under section 108(c)(1) is to be applied to  reduce basis— 

I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(F)(i)  — 
                        
                        
depreciable property shall only include depreciable real  property for purposes of
                           subparagraphs (A)
                           and (C), 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(F)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
subparagraph (E) shall not apply, and 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(3)(F)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
in the case of property taken into account under section  108(c)(2)(B), the reduction with respect to such property shall be made as  of the time immediately
                           before disposition if earlier than the time under
                           subsection (a). 




I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4) Special Rules For Qualified Farm Indebtedness


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Any  amount which under subsection (b)(2)(E) of section 108 is to be applied to  reduce basis and which is attributable to an amount excluded
                        under  subsection
                        (a)(1)(C) of section 108— 

I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
shall be applied only to reduce the basis of qualified  property held by the taxpayer,
                           and 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
shall be applied to reduce the basis of qualified  property in the following order: 

I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(A)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
First the basis of qualified property which is  depreciable property. 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(A)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
Second the basis of qualified property which is land  used or held for use in the
                              trade or business of farming. 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(A)(ii)(III)  — 
                           
                           
Then the basis of other qualified property. 




I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(B) Qualified Property — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph, the term “qualified property” has the meaning  given
                        to such term by section 108(g)(3)(C). 


I.R.C. § 1017(b)(4)(C) Certain Rules Made Applicable — 
                     
                     
Rules  similar to the rules of subparagraphs (C), (D), and (E) of paragraph (3)  shall
                        apply for purposes of this paragraph and section 108(g).
I.R.C. § 1017(c) Special Rules


I.R.C. § 1017(c)(1) Reduction Not To Be Made In Exempt Property — 
                  
                  
In  the case of an amount excluded from gross income under section  108(a)(1)(A), no reduction in basis shall be made under this section in  the basis of property
                     which the debtor treats as exempt property under  section 522 of title 11 of the United
                     States Code. 


I.R.C. § 1017(c)(2) Reductions In Basis Not Treated As Dispositions — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this title, a reduction in basis under this section shall not  be
                     treated as a disposition.
I.R.C. § 1017(d) Recapture Of Reductions


I.R.C. § 1017(d)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of sections 1245 and 1250— 

I.R.C. § 1017(d)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any property the basis of which is reduced under this  section and which is neither
                        section 1245 property nor section 1250  property shall be treated as section 1245 property, and 


I.R.C. § 1017(d)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any reduction under this section shall be treated as a  deduction allowed for depreciation. 



I.R.C. § 1017(d)(2) Special Rule For Section 1250 — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of section 1250(b), the determination of what would have been the  depreciation adjustments under the
                     straight line method shall be made as  if there had been no reduction under this section.
