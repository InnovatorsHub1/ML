Sec. 1032. Exchange Of Stock For Property

I.R.C. § 1032(a) Nonrecognition Of Gain Or Loss — 
               
               
No  gain or loss shall be recognized to a corporation on the receipt of  money  or
                  other property in exchange for stock
                  (including treasury stock) of   such corporation. No gain or loss shall be recognized
                  by a corporation  with  respect to any lapse or acquisition of an option, or with
                  respect  to a securities futures contract (as defined in section 1234B), to buy or  sell its stock (including treasury stock).
I.R.C. § 1032(b) Basis — 
               
               
For  basis of property acquired by a corporation in certain exchanges for its  stock,
                  see section 362.
