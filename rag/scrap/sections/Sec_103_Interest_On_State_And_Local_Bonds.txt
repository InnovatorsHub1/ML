Sec. 103. Interest On State And Local Bonds

I.R.C. § 103(a) Exclusion — 
               
               
Except  as provided in subsection (b), gross income does not include interest on  any State or local bond.
I.R.C. § 103(b) Exceptions — 
               
               
Subsection  (a) shall not apply to— 

I.R.C. § 103(b)(1) Private Activity Bond Which Is Not A Qualified Bond — 
                  
                  
Any  private activity bond which is not a qualified bond (within the meaning of  section
                     141). 


I.R.C. § 103(b)(2) Arbitrage Bond — 
                  
                  
Any  arbitrage bond (within the meaning of section 148). 


I.R.C. § 103(b)(3) Bond Not In Registered Form, Etc. — 
                  
                  
Any  bond unless such bond meets the applicable requirements of section 149.
I.R.C. § 103(c) Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section and part IV— 

I.R.C. § 103(c)(1) State Or Local Bond — 
                  
                  
The  term “State or local bond” means an obligation of a State or political  subdivision
                     thereof. 


I.R.C. § 103(c)(2) State — 
                  
                  
The  term “State” includes the District of Columbia and any possession of the  United
                     States.
