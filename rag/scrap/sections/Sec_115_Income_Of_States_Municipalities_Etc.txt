Sec. 115. Income Of States, Municipalities, Etc.

I.R.C. § 115(1)  — 
               
               
income  derived from any public utility or the exercise of any essential  governmental
                  function and accruing to a State or any political subdivision  thereof, or the District
                  of Columbia; or
I.R.C. § 115(2)  — 
               
               
income  accruing to the government of any possession of the United States, or any
                  political subdivision thereof.
