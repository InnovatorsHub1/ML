Sec. 1211. Limitation On Capital Losses

I.R.C. § 1211(a) Corporations — 
               
               
In  the case of a corporation, losses from sales or exchanges of capital  assets shall
                  be allowed only to the extent of gains from such sales or  exchanges.
I.R.C. § 1211(b) Other Taxpayers — 
               
               
In  the case of a taxpayer other than a corporation, losses from sales or  exchanges
                  of capital assets shall be allowed only to the extent of the  gains from such sales
                  or exchanges, plus
                  (if such losses exceed such  gains) the lower of— 

I.R.C. § 1211(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
$3,000  ($1,500 in the case of a married individual filing a separate return), or 


I.R.C. § 1211(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  excess of such losses over such gains.
