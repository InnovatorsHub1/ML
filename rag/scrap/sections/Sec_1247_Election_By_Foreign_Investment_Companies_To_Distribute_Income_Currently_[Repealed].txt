Sec. 1247. Election By Foreign Investment Companies To Distribute Income Currently [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1247(a) Election By Foreign Investment Company


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                              after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                              within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




If a foreign investment company which is described in  section 1246(b)(1) elects
                        (in the manner provided in regulations  prescribed by the Secretary)
                        on or before December 31, 1962, with respect  to each taxable year beginning after
                        December 31, 1962, to—


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
distribute  to its shareholders 90 percent or more of what its taxable income would
                        be  if it were a domestic corporation; 


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
designate  in a written notice mailed to its shareholders at any time before the 
                        expiration of 45 days after the close of its taxable year the pro rata  amount of
                        the amount
                        (determined as if such corporation were a domestic  corporation) of the net capital
                        gain of the taxable year; and the portion  thereof which is being distributed; and 


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
provide  such information as the Secretary deems necessary to carry out the  purposes
                        of this section, then section 1246 shall not apply with respect
                        to the qualified shareholders of such company during any taxable year to  which such
                        election applies. 



I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2) Special Rules


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2)(A) Computation Of Taxable Income — 
                     
                     




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                                 after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                                 within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




For purposes of paragraph (1)(A), the taxable income of  the company shall be computed
                           without regard to—


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  net capital gain referred to in paragraph (1)(B), 


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
section  172 (relating to net operating losses), and 


I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
any  deduction provided by part VIII of subchapter B (other than the deduction  provided
                           by section 248, relating to organizational expenditures). 



I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2)(B) Distributions After The Close Of The Taxable Year — 
                     
                     




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                                 after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                                 within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




For purposes of paragraph (1)(A), a distribution made  after the close of the taxable
                           year and on or before the 15th day of the  third month of the next taxable year shall
                           be treated as distributed  during the taxable year to the extent elected by the company
                           (in  accordance with regulations prescribed by the Secretary) on or before the  15th
                           day of such third month.



I.R.C. § 1247(a)(2)(C) Carryover Of Capital Losses From Nonelection Years Denied — 
                     
                     




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                                 after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                                 within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




In computing the net capital gain referred to in  paragraph (1)(B), section 1212 shall not apply to losses incurred in or  with respect to taxable years before the
                           first taxable year to which the  election applies.
I.R.C. § 1247(b) Years To Which Election Applies — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                           within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




The election of any foreign investment company under  this section shall terminate
                     as of the close of the taxable year preceding  its first taxable year in which any
                     of the following occurs:


I.R.C. § 1247(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  company fails to comply with the provisions of subparagraph (A), (B), or  (C)
                     of subsection (a)(1), unless it is shown that such failure is due to  reasonable cause
                     and not due to willful neglect, 


I.R.C. § 1247(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  company is a foreign personal holding company, or 


I.R.C. § 1247(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
the  company is not a foreign investment company which is described in section  1246(b)(1).
I.R.C. § 1247(c) Qualified Shareholders — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                           within which such taxable years of foreign corporations  end.




For purposes of this section—


I.R.C. § 1247(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                              after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                              within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




The term “qualified shareholder"
                        means any shareholder  who is a United States person (as defined in section 7701(a)(30)), other  than a shareholder described in paragraph (2).



I.R.C. § 1247(c)(2) Certain United States Persons Excluded From Definition — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                              after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                              within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




A United States person shall not be treated as a  qualified shareholder for the taxable
                        year if for such taxable year (or  for any prior taxable year) he did not include,
                        in computing his long-term  capital gains in his return for such taxable year, the
                        amount designated  by such company pursuant to subsection
                        (a)(1)(B) as his share of the  undistributed capital gains of such company for its
                        taxable year ending  within or with such taxable year of the taxpayer. The preceding
                        sentence  shall not apply with respect to any failure by the taxpayer to treat an
                        amount as provided therein if the taxpayer shows that such failure was due  to reasonable
                        cause and not due to willful neglect.
I.R.C. § 1247(d) Treatment Of Distributed And Undistributed Capital Gains By A Qualified Shareholder — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States  shareholders with
                           or within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




Every qualified shareholder of a foreign investment  company for any taxable year
                     of such company with respect to which an  election pursuant to subsection (a) is in
                     effect shall include, in  computing his long-term capital gains—


I.R.C. § 1247(d)(1)  — 
                  
                  
for  his taxable year in which received, his pro rata share of the distributed  portion
                     of the net capital gain for such taxable year of such company, and 


I.R.C. § 1247(d)(2)  — 
                  
                  
for  his taxable year in which or with which the taxable year of such company  ends,
                     his pro rata share of the undistributed portion of the net capital  gain for such
                     taxable year of such company.
I.R.C. § 1247(e) Adjustments — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                           within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




Under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, proper  adjustment shall be made—


I.R.C. § 1247(e)(1)  — 
                  
                  
in  the earnings and profits of the electing foreign investment company and a  qualified
                     shareholder's ratable share thereof, and 


I.R.C. § 1247(e)(2)  — 
                  
                  
in  the adjusted basis of stock of such company held by such shareholder, to  reflect
                     such shareholder's inclusion in gross income of undistributed  capital gains.
I.R.C. § 1247(f) Election By Foreign Investment Company With Respect To Foreign Tax Credit — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                           within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




A foreign investment company with respect to which an  election pursuant to subsection
                     (a) is in effect and more than 50 percent  of the value (as defined in section 851(c)(4)) of whose total assets at  the close of the taxable year consists of stock or securities
                     in foreign  corporations may, for such taxable year, elect the application of this
                     subsection with respect to income, war profits, and excess profits taxes  described
                     in section 901(b)(1) which are paid by the foreign investment  company during such taxable year to foreign
                     countries and possessions of  the United States. If such election is made—


I.R.C. § 1247(f)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  foreign investment company— 

I.R.C. § 1247(f)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
shall  compute its taxable income, for purposes of subsection (a)(1)(A), without 
                        any deductions for income, war profits, or excess profits taxes paid to  foreign countries
                        or possessions of the United States, and 


I.R.C. § 1247(f)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
shall  treat the amount of such taxes, for purposes of subsection (a)(1)(A), as  distributed
                        to its shareholders; 



I.R.C. § 1247(f)(2)  — 
                  
                  
each  qualified shareholder of such foreign investment company— 

I.R.C. § 1247(f)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
shall  include in gross income and treat as paid by him his proportionate share  of
                        such taxes, and 


I.R.C. § 1247(f)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
shall  treat, for purposes of applying subpart A of part III of subchapter N, his
                        proportionate share of such taxes as having been paid to the country in  which the
                        foreign investment company is incorporated, and 


I.R.C. § 1247(f)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
shall  treat as gross income from sources within the country in which the foreign
                        investment company is incorporated, for purposes of applying subpart A of  part III
                        of subchapter N, the sum of his proportionate share of such taxes  and any dividend
                        paid to him by such foreign investment company.
I.R.C. § 1247(g) Notice To Shareholders — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                           within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




The amounts to be treated by qualified shareholders, for  purposes of subsection (f)(2),
                     as their proportionate share of the taxes  described in subsection (f)(1)(A) paid
                     by a foreign investment company  shall not exceed the amounts so designated by the
                     foreign investment  company in a written notice mailed to its shareholders not later
                     than 45  days after the close of its taxable year.

Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 413(a)(3), repealed Sec. 1247, effective for taxable years of foreign corporations beginning
                           after December 31, 2004, and to taxable years of United States shareholders with or
                           within which such taxable years of foreign corporations end.




If—


I.R.C. § 1247(i)(1)  — 
                  
                  
under  this section, any qualified shareholder treats any amount designated under
                     subsection (a)(1)(B) with respect to a share of stock as long-term capital  gain,
