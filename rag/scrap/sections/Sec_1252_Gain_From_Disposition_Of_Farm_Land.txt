I.R.C. § 1252(a)(2) Farm Land — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this section, the term “farm land"
                     means any land with respect  to which deductions have been allowed under section 175 (relating to soil  and water conservation expenditures).

I.R.C. § 1252(b) Special Rules — 
               
               
Under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, rules similar to the rules of  section
