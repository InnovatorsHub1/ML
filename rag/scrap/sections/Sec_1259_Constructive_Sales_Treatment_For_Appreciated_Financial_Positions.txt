Sec. 1259. Constructive Sales Treatment For Appreciated Financial Positions

I.R.C. § 1259(a) In General — 
               
               
If  there is a constructive sale of an appreciated financial position— 

I.R.C. § 1259(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  taxpayer shall recognize gain as if such position were sold, assigned, or  otherwise
                     terminated at its fair market value on the date of such  constructive sale (and any
                     gain shall be taken into account for the  taxable year which includes such date),
                     and 


I.R.C. § 1259(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
for  purposes of applying this title for periods after the constructive sale— 

I.R.C. § 1259(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
proper adjustment shall be made in the amount of any gain or loss  subsequently realized
                        with respect to such position for any gain taken  into account by reason of paragraph
                        (1), and 


I.R.C. § 1259(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  holding period of such position shall be determined as if such position  were
                        originally acquired on the date of such constructive  sale.
I.R.C. § 1259(b) Appreciated Financial Position — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1259(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Except  as provided in paragraph (2), the term “appreciated financial position”  means
                     any position with respect to any stock, debt instrument, or  partnership interest
                     if there would be gain were such position sold,  assigned, or otherwise terminated
                     at its fair market value. 


I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2) Exceptions — 
                  
                  
The  term “appreciated financial position”
                     shall not include— 

I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any  position with respect to debt if— 

I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  position unconditionally entitles the holder to receive a specified  principal
                           amount, 


I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the interest payments (or other similar amounts) with respect to such  position meet
                           the requirements of clause
                           (i) of section 860G(a)(1)(B),
                           and 


I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
such position is not convertible
                           (directly or indirectly) into stock of  the issuer or any related person, 



I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any hedge with respect to a position described in  subparagraph (A), and 


I.R.C. § 1259(b)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
any  position which is marked to market under any provision of this title or  the
                        regulations thereunder. 



I.R.C. § 1259(b)(3) Position — 
                  
                  
The  term “position” means an interest, including a futures or forward  contract,
                     short sale, or option.
I.R.C. § 1259(c) Constructive Sale — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1259(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
A  taxpayer shall be treated as having made a constructive sale of an  appreciated
                     financial position if the taxpayer (or a related person)— 

I.R.C. § 1259(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
enters into a short sale of the same or substantially identical  property, 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
enters into an offsetting notional principal contract with respect to the  same or
                        substantially identical property, 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
enters into a futures or forward contract to deliver the same or  substantially identical
                        property, 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
in  the case of an appreciated financial position that is a short sale or a  contract
                        described in subparagraph
                        (B) or (C) with respect to any  property, acquires the same or substantially identical
                        property,  or 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(1)(E)  — 
                     
                     
to  the extent prescribed by the Secretary in regulations, enters into 1 or  more
                        other transactions (or acquires 1 or more positions) that have  substantially the
                        same effect as a transaction described in any of the  preceding subparagraphs. 



I.R.C. § 1259(c)(2) Exception For Sales Of Nonpublicly Traded Property — 
                  
                  
A  taxpayer shall not be treated as having made a constructive sale solely  because
                     the taxpayer enters into a contract  for sale of any stock, debt  instrument, or partnership
                     interest which is not a marketable security (as  defined in section 453(f)) if the contract settles within 1 year after the  date such contract is entered into. 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3) Exception For Certain Closed Transactions


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
In  applying this section, there shall be disregarded any transaction (which  would
                        otherwise  cause a constructive sale
                        ) during the taxable year if— 

I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
such  transaction is closed  on or before  the 30th day after the close of such  taxable
                           year, 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the taxpayer holds the appreciated financial position throughout the  60-day period
                           beginning on the date such transaction is closed,  and 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
at no time during such 60-day period is the taxpayer's risk of loss with  respect
                           to such position reduced by reason of a circumstance which would  be described in
                           section 246(c)(4) if references to stock included  references to such position. 



I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(B) Treatment Of  Certain Closed Transactions Where Risk Of Loss On Appreciated Financial
                        Position Diminished — 
                     
                     
If— 

I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a  transaction, which would otherwise
                           cause a constructive sale  of an  appreciated financial position, is closed during
                           the taxable year or  during the 30 days thereafter, and 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
another transaction is entered into during the 60-day period beginning on  the date
                           the transaction referred to in clause (i) is closed— 

I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(B)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
which would (but for this subparagraph)
                              cause the requirement of  subparagraph (A)(iii) not to be met with respect to the
                              transaction  described in clause (i) of this subparagraph, 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(B)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
which is closed  on or  before the 30th day after the close of the taxable  year in
                              which the transaction referred to in clause (i) occurs,  and 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(3)(B)(ii)(III)  — 
                           
                           
which meets the requirements of clauses (ii) and (iii) of subparagraph  (A), 


the transaction referred to in clause
                              (ii) shall be disregarded for purposes of determining whether the
                              requirements of subparagraph (A)(iii) are met with respect to the
                              transaction described in clause  (i).





I.R.C. § 1259(c)(4) Related Person — 
                  
                  
A  person is related to another person with respect to a transaction if— 

I.R.C. § 1259(c)(4)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  relationship is described in section 267(b) or 707(b), and 


I.R.C. § 1259(c)(4)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such  transaction is entered into with a view toward avoiding the purposes of  this
                        section.
I.R.C. § 1259(d) Other Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1259(d)(1) Forward Contract — 
                  
                  
The  term “forward contract” means a contract to deliver a substantially fixed  amount
                     of property (including cash)
                     for a substantially fixed  price. 


I.R.C. § 1259(d)(2) Offsetting Notional Principal Contract — 
                  
                  
The  term “offsetting notional principal contract”
                     means, with respect to any  property, an agreement which includes— 

I.R.C. § 1259(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
a  requirement to pay (or provide credit for) all or substantially all of the  investment
                        yield (including appreciation) on such property for a specified  period, and 


I.R.C. § 1259(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
a  right to be reimbursed for (or receive credit for) all or substantially  all of
                        any decline in the value of such  property.
I.R.C. § 1259(e) Special Rules


I.R.C. § 1259(e)(1) Treatment Of Subsequent Sale Of Position Which Was Deemed Sold — 
                  
                  
If— 

I.R.C. § 1259(e)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
there is a constructive sale of any appreciated financial  position, 


I.R.C. § 1259(e)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such  position is subsequently disposed of, and 


I.R.C. § 1259(e)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
at  the time of such disposition, the transaction resulting in the  constructive sale
                        of such position is open with respect to the taxpayer or  any related person, 


solely for purposes of determining whether the taxpayer
                        has entered into a constructive sale of any other appreciated financial
                        position held by the taxpayer, the taxpayer shall be treated as entering
                        into such transaction immediately after such disposition. For purposes of  the preceding
                        sentence, an assignment or other termination shall be  treated as a disposition.



I.R.C. § 1259(e)(2) Certain Trust Instruments Treated As Stock — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this section, an interest in a trust which is actively traded  (within
                     the meaning of section 1092(d)(1)) shall be treated as stock  unless substantially all (by value) of the property held
                     by the trust is  debt described in subsection (b)(2)(A). 


I.R.C. § 1259(e)(3) Multiple Positions In Property — 
                  
                  
If  a taxpayer holds multiple positions in property, the determination of  whether
                     a specific transaction is a constructive sale and, if so, which  appreciated financial
                     position is deemed sold shall be made in the same  manner as actual sales.
I.R.C. § 1259(f) Regulations — 
               
               
The  Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary or  appropriate
                  to carry out the purposes of this section.
