Sec. 12. Cross References Relating To Tax On Corporations

I.R.C. § 12(1)  — 
               
               
For tax on the unrelated business
                  income of certain charitable and other corporations exempt from tax
                  under this chapter, see section 511.
I.R.C. § 12(2)  — 
               
               
For accumulated earnings tax and
                  personal holding company tax, see parts I and II of subchapter G (sec. 531 and following).
I.R.C. § 12(3)  — 
               
               
For doubling of tax on corporations
                  of certain foreign countries, see section 891.
I.R.C. § 12(4)  — 
               
               
For alternative tax in case of capital
                  gains, see section 1201(a).
I.R.C. § 12(5)  — 
               
               
For alternative minimum tax, see
                  section 55.
