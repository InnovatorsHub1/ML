Sec. 1313. Definitions

I.R.C. § 1313(a) Determination — 
               
               
For  purposes of this part, the term “determination"
                  means— 

I.R.C. § 1313(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
a  decision by the Tax Court or a judgment, decree, or other order by any  court of
                     competent jurisdiction, which has become final; 


I.R.C. § 1313(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
a  closing agreement made under section 7121; 


I.R.C. § 1313(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
a  final disposition by the Secretary of a claim for refund. For purposes of  this
                     part, a claim for refund shall be deemed finally disposed of by the  Secretary— 

I.R.C. § 1313(a)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
as to  items with respect to which the claim was allowed, on the date of  allowance
                        of refund or credit or on the date of mailing notice of  disallowance (by reason of
                        offsetting items) of the claim for refund, and 


I.R.C. § 1313(a)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
as to  items with respect to which the claim was disallowed, in whole or in part,
                        or as to items applied by the Secretary in reduction of the refund or  credit, on
                        expiration of the time for instituting suit with respect  thereto (unless suit is
                        instituted before the expiration of such time); or 



I.R.C. § 1313(a)(4)  — 
                  
                  
under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, an agreement for purposes of this
                     part, signed by the Secretary and by any person, relating to the liability  of such
                     person
                     (or the person for whom he acts) in respect of a tax under  this subtitle for any
                     taxable period.
I.R.C. § 1313(b) Taxpayer — 
               
               
Notwithstanding  section 7701(a)(14), the term “taxpayer"
                  means any person subject to a tax  under the applicable revenue law.
I.R.C. § 1313(c) Related Taxpayer — 
               
               
For  purposes of this part, the term “related taxpayer"
                  means a taxpayer who,  with the taxpayer with respect to whom a determination is made,
                  stood, in  the taxable year with respect to which the erroneous inclusion, exclusion,
                  omission, allowance, or disallowance was made, in one of the following  relationships: 

I.R.C. § 1313(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
husband  and wife, 


I.R.C. § 1313(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
grantor  and fiduciary, 


I.R.C. § 1313(c)(3)  — 
                  
                  
grantor  and beneficiary, 


I.R.C. § 1313(c)(4)  — 
                  
                  
fiduciary  and beneficiary, legatee, or heir, 


I.R.C. § 1313(c)(5)  — 
                  
                  
decedent  and decedent's estate, 


I.R.C. § 1313(c)(6)  — 
                  
                  
partner,  or 


I.R.C. § 1313(c)(7)  — 
                  
                  
member  of an affiliated group of corporations
                     (as defined in section 1504).
