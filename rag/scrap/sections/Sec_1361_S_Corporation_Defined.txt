Sec. 1361. S Corporation Defined

I.R.C. § 1361(a) S Corporation Defined


I.R.C. § 1361(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this title, the term “S corporation"
                     means, with respect to  any taxable year, a small business corporation for which an
                     election under  section 1362(a) is in effect for such year. 


I.R.C. § 1361(a)(2) C Corporation — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this title, the term “C corporation"
                     means, with respect to  any taxable year, a corporation which is not an S corporation
                     for such  year.
I.R.C. § 1361(b) Small Business Corporation


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subchapter, the term “small business corporation” means a  domestic
                     corporation which is not an ineligible corporation and which does  not— 

I.R.C. § 1361(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
have  more than  100  shareholders, 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
have  as a shareholder a person (other than an estate, a trust described  in  subsection
                        (c)(2), or an organization described in subsection (c)(6)) who  is not an individual, 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
have  a nonresident alien as a shareholder, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
have  more than 1 class of stock. 



I.R.C. § 1361(b)(2) Ineligible Corporation Defined — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of paragraph (1), the term “ineligible corporation” means any  corporation
                     which is— 

I.R.C. § 1361(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
a  financial institution which uses the reserve method of accounting for bad  debts
                        described in section 585, 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
an  insurance company subject to tax under subchapter L, or 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
a  DISC or former DISC. 



I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3) Treatment Of Certain Wholly Owned Subsidiaries


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in regulations prescribed by the Secretary, for purposes of  this
                        title–- 

I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a  corporation which is a qualified subchapter S subsidiary shall not be  treated
                           as a separate corporation, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
all  assets, liabilities, and items of income, deduction, and credit of a  qualified
                           subchapter S subsidiary shall be treated as assets, liabilities,  and such items (as
                           the case may be) of the S  corporation. 



I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(B) Qualified Subchapter S Subsidiary — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph, the term “qualified subchapter S subsidiary”  means
                        any domestic corporation which is not an ineligible corporation (as  defined in paragraph
                        (2)), if— 

I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
100  percent of the stock of such corporation is held by the S corporation,  and 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the  S corporation elects to treat such corporation as a qualified subchapter S  subsidiary. 



I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(C) Treatment Of Terminations Of Qualified Subchapter S Subsidiary Status


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(C)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
For  purposes of this title, if any corporation which was a qualified  subchapter
                           S subsidiary ceases to meet the requirements of subparagraph  (B), such corporation
                           shall be treated as a new corporation acquiring all  of its assets (and assuming all
                           of its liabilities)
                           immediately before  such cessation from the S corporation in exchange for its  stock. 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(C)(ii) Termination By Reason Of Sale Of Stock — 
                        
                        
If  the failure to meet the requirements of subparagraph
                           (B) is by  reason of the sale of stock of a corporation which is a qualified  subchapter
                           S subsidiary, the sale of          such stock shall be treated  as if— 

I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(C)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
the  sale were a sale of an undivided interest in the assets of such  corporation
                              (based on the percentage of the corporation's stock  sold), and 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(C)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the sale were followed by an acquisition by such corporation of all of its assets
                              (and the assumption by such corporation of all of  its liabilities) in a transaction
                              to which section 351  applies. 




I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(D) Election After Termination — 
                     
                     
If  a corporation's status as a qualified subchapter S subsidiary terminates,  such
                        corporation (and any successor corporation)
                        shall not be eligible to  make— 

I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(D)(i)  — 
                        
                        
an  election under subparagraph (B)(ii)
                           to be treated as a qualified  subchapter S subsidiary, or 


I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(D)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
an  election under section 1362(a) to be treated as an S corporation, 


before its 5th taxable year which
                           begins after the 1st taxable year for which such termination was
                           effective, unless the Secretary consents to such  election.



I.R.C. § 1361(b)(3)(E) Information Returns — 
                     
                     
Except to the extent provided by the Secretary, this paragraph  shall not apply to
                        part III of subchapter A of chapter 61 (relating to information returns).
I.R.C. § 1361(c) Special Rules For Applying Subsection (b)


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1) Members Of A Family Treated As 1 Shareholder


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)(A), there shall be treated as one  shareholder— 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a  husband and wife (and their estates), and 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
all members of a family (and their estates). 



I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B) Members Of A Family — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph— 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
The  term “members of a family” means a common ancestor, any lineal  descendant of
                           such common ancestor, and any spouse or former spouse of such common ancestor or any
                           such lineal descendant. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B)(ii) Common Ancestor — 
                        
                        
An individual shall not be considered to be a common ancestor if,  on the applicable
                           date, the individual is more than 6 generations  removed from the youngest generation
                           of shareholders who would  (but for this subparagraph) be members of the family. For
                           purposes of the preceding sentence, a spouse (or former spouse) shall be treated 
                           as being of the same generation as the individual to whom such  spouse is  (or was)
                           married. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B)(iii) Applicable Date — 
                        
                        
The  term “applicable date” means the latest of— 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B)(iii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
the  date the election under section 1362(a) is made, 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B)(iii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the earliest date that an individual described in clause (i)  holds stock in the S
                              corporation, or 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(B)(iii)(III)  — 
                           
                           
October 22, 2004. 




I.R.C. § 1361(c)(1)(C) Effect Of Adoption, Etc. — 
                     
                     
Any legally adopted child of an individual, any child who is  lawfully placed with
                        an individual for legal adoption by the
                        individual, and any eligible foster child of an individual (within the meaning of
                        section 152(f)(1)(C)), shall be treated as a child of such individual by blood. 



I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2) Certain Trusts Permitted As Shareholders


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)(B), the following trusts may be  shareholders: 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
A  trust all of which is treated (under subpart E of part I of subchapter J  of this
                           chapter) as owned by an individual who is a citizen or resident of  the United States. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
A  trust which was described in clause
                           (i) immediately before the death of  the deemed owner and which continues in existence
                           after such death, but  only for the 2-year period beginning on the day of the deemed
                           owner's  death. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
A  trust with respect to stock transferred to it pursuant to the terms of a  will,
                           but only for the 2-year period beginning on the day on which such  stock is transferred
                           to it. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
A  trust created primarily to exercise the voting power of stock transferred  to it. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A)(v)  — 
                        
                        
An  electing small business trust. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(A)(vi)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a corporation which is a bank (as defined in section  581)
                           or a depository institution holding company (as defined in section
                           3(w)(1) of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1813(w)(1)), a
                           trust which constitutes an individual retirement account under  section 408(a), including one designated as a Roth IRA under           section 408A, but only to the extent of the stock held by such trust in such bank
                           or company  as of the date of the enactment of this  clause. 


This subparagraph shall not apply to any foreign trust.



I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B) Treatment As Shareholders — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)— 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a trust described in clause (i) of subparagraph (A), the  deemed owner
                           shall be treated as the shareholder. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a trust described in clause (ii) of subparagraph (A), the  estate
                           of the deemed owner shall be treated as the shareholder. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a trust described in clause (iii) of subparagraph (A), the  estate
                           of the testator shall be treated as the shareholder. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a trust described in clause (iv) of subparagraph (A), each  beneficiary
                           of the trust shall be treated as a shareholder. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B)(v)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a trust described in clause (v) of subparagraph (A), each  potential
                           current beneficiary of such trust shall be treated as a  shareholder; except that,
                           if for any period there is no potential current  beneficiary of such trust, such trust
                           shall be treated as the shareholder  during such period. This clause shall not apply
                           for purposes of subsection (b)(1)(C). 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(2)(B)(vi)  — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a trust described in clause (vi) of subparagraph (A), the  individual
                           for whose benefit the trust was created  shall be  treated as the shareholder. 




I.R.C. § 1361(c)(3) Estate Of Individual In Bankruptcy May Be Shareholder — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)(B), the term “estate"
                     includes the estate of  an individual in a case under title 11 of the United States
                     Code. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(4) Differences In Common Stock Voting Rights Disregarded — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)(D), a corporation shall not be treated as  having
                     more than 1 class of stock solely because there are differences in  voting rights
                     among the shares of common stock. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5) Straight Debt Safe Harbor


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)(D), straight debt shall not be treated as a  second
                        class of stock. 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5)(B) Straight Debt Defined — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph, the term “straight debt” means any written  unconditional
                        promise to pay on demand or on a specified date a sum  certain in money if— 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  interest rate (and interest payment dates) are not contingent on profits,  the
                           borrower's discretion, or similar factors, 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
there  is no convertibility (directly or indirectly) into stock, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
the  creditor is an individual (other than a nonresident alien), an estate, a  trust
                           described in paragraph
                           (2), or a person which is actively and  regularly engaged in the business of lending
                           money. 



I.R.C. § 1361(c)(5)(C) Regulations — 
                     
                     
The  Secretary shall prescribe such regulations as may be necessary or  appropriate
                        to provide for the proper treatment of straight debt under  this subchapter and for
                        the coordination of such treatment with other  provisions of this title. 



I.R.C. § 1361(c)(6) Certain  Exempt Organizations Permitted As Shareholders — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of subsection (b)(1)(B), an organization which is— 

I.R.C. § 1361(c)(6)(A)  — 
                     
                     
described  in section 401(a) or 501(c)(3), and 


I.R.C. § 1361(c)(6)(B)  — 
                     
                     
exempt  from taxation under section 501(a), 


may be a shareholder in an S  corporation.
I.R.C. § 1361(d) Special Rule For Qualified Subchapter S Trust


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In  the case of a qualified subchapter S trust with respect to which a  beneficiary
                     makes an election under paragraph
                     (2)— 

I.R.C. § 1361(d)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such  trust shall be treated as a trust described in subsection (c)(2)(A)(i), 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
for  purposes of section 678(a), the beneficiary of such trust shall be treated  as the owner of that portion of
                        the trust which consists of stock in an S  corporation with respect to which the election
                        under paragraph (2) is  made, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
for  purposes of applying sections 465 and 469 to the beneficiary of the 
                        trust, the disposition of the S corporation stock by the trust shall be treated as
                        a disposition by such  beneficiary. 



I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2) Election


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
A  beneficiary of a qualified subchapter S trust (or his legal  representative) may
                        elect to have this subsection apply. 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(B) Manner And Time Of Election


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(B)(i) Separate Election With Respect To Each Corporation — 
                        
                        
An  election under this paragraph shall be made separately with respect to  each corporation
                           the stock of which is held by the trust. 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(B)(ii) Elections With Respect To Successive Income Beneficiaries — 
                        
                        
If  there is an election under this paragraph with respect to any beneficiary,  an
                           election under this paragraph shall be treated as made by each  successive beneficiary
                           unless such beneficiary affirmatively refuses to  consent to such election. 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(B)(iii) Time, Manner, And Form Of Election — 
                        
                        
Any  election, or refusal, under this paragraph shall be made in such manner  and
                           form, and at such time, as the Secretary may prescribe. 



I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(C) Election Irrevocable — 
                     
                     
An  election under this paragraph, once made, may be revoked only with the  consent
                        of the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(2)(D) Grace Period — 
                     
                     
An  election under this paragraph shall be effective up to 15 days and 2  months before
                        the date of the election. 



I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3) Qualified Subchapter S Trust — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection, the term “qualified subchapter S trust” means  a
                     trust— 

I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  terms of which require that— 

I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
during  the life of the current income beneficiary, there shall be only 1 income 
                           beneficiary of the trust, 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
any  corpus distributed during the life of the current income beneficiary may  be
                           distributed only to such beneficiary, 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
the  income interest of the current income beneficiary in the trust shall  terminate
                           on the earlier of such beneficiary's death or the termination of  the trust, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3)(A)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
upon  the termination of the trust during the life of the current income  beneficiary,
                           the trust shall distribute all of its assets to such  beneficiary, and 



I.R.C. § 1361(d)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
all  of the income (within the meaning of section 643(b))
                        of which is  distributed (or required to be distributed) currently to 1 individual
                        who  is a citizen or resident of the United States. 


A substantially separate and independent share of a
                        trust within the meaning of section 663(c) shall be treated as a separate  trust for purposes of this subsection and subsection
                        (c).



I.R.C. § 1361(d)(4) Trust Ceasing To Be Qualified


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(4)(A) Failure To Meet Requirements Of Paragraph (3)(A) — 
                     
                     
If  a qualified subchapter S trust ceases to meet any requirement of paragraph  (3)(A),
                        the provisions of this subsection shall not apply to such trust as  of the date it
                        ceases to meet such requirement. 


I.R.C. § 1361(d)(4)(B) Failure To Meet Requirements Of Paragraph (3)(B) — 
                     
                     
If  any qualified subchapter S trust ceases to meet any requirement of  paragraph
                        (3)(B) but continues to meet the requirements of paragraph  (3)(A), the provisions
                        of this subsection shall not apply to such trust as  of the first day of the first
                        taxable year beginning after the first  taxable year for which it failed to meet the
                        requirements of paragraph  (3)(B).
I.R.C. § 1361(e) Electing Small Business Trust Defined


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1) Electing Small Business Trust — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), the term “electing small business trust”
                        means any trust if— 

I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
such  trust does not have as a beneficiary any person other than (I) an  individual,
                           (II) an estate, (III) an organization described in paragraph  (2), (3), (4), or (5)
                           of section 170(c), or  (IV) an organization
                           described in section 170(c)(1) which holds a contingent  interest in such  trust and is not a potential current
                           beneficiary, 


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
no  interest in such trust was acquired by purchase, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
an  election under this subsection applies to such  trust. 



I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(B) Certain Trusts Not Eligible — 
                     
                     
The  term “electing small business trust”
                        shall not include— 

I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
any  qualified subchapter S trust (as defined in subsection (d)(3)) if an  election
                           under subsection (d)(2)
                           applies to any corporation the stock of  which is held by such trust, 


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
any  trust exempt from tax under this subtitle, and 


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
any  charitable remainder annuity trust or charitable remainder unitrust (as  defined
                           in section 664(d)). 



I.R.C. § 1361(e)(1)(C) Purchase — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subparagraph (A), the term “purchase”
                        means any acquisition if  the basis of the property acquired is determined under section
                        1012. 



I.R.C. § 1361(e)(2) Potential Current Beneficiary — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this section, the term “potential current beneficiary” means,  with
                     respect to any period, any person who at any time during such period  is entitled
                     to, or at the discretion of any person may receive, a  distribution from the principal
                     or income of the trust  (determined  without regard to any power of appointment to
                     the extent such power  remains unexercised at the end of such period). If a trust
                     disposes of all  of the stock which it holds in an S corporation, then, with respect
                     to  such corporation, the term “potential current beneficiary” does not  include any
                     person who first met the requirements of the preceding  sentence during the  1-year
                     period ending on the date of such  disposition. 


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(3) Election — 
                  
                  
An  election under this subsection shall be made by the trustee. Any such  election
                     shall apply to the taxable year of the trust for which made and  all subsequent taxable
                     years of such trust unless revoked with the consent  of the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 1361(e)(4) Cross Reference — 
                  
                  
For  special treatment of electing small business trusts, see section  641(c).
I.R.C. § 1361(f) Restricted Bank Director Stock


I.R.C. § 1361(f)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Restricted  bank director stock shall not be taken into account as outstanding  stock
                     of the S corporation in applying this subchapter (other than  section 1368(f)). 


I.R.C. § 1361(f)(2) Restricted Bank Director Stock — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection, the term “restricted bank director  stock” means
                     stock in a bank (as defined in section 581) or a  depository institution holding company (as defined in section 3(w)(1)  of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (12 U.S.C. 1813(w)(1))), if such  stock— 

I.R.C. § 1361(f)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
is  required to be held by an individual under applicable Federal or  State law in
                        order to permit such individual to serve as a director,  and 


I.R.C. § 1361(f)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
is  subject to an agreement with such bank or company (or a corporation  which controls
                        (within the meaning of section 368(c))
                        such bank or  company) pursuant to which the holder is required to sell back such
                        stock (at the same price as the individual acquired such stock) upon ceasing to hold
                        the office of director. 



I.R.C. § 1361(f)(3) Cross Reference — 
                  
                  
For  treatment of certain distributions with respect to restricted bank director stock,
                     see section 1368(f).
I.R.C. § 1361(g) Special Rule For Bank Required To Change From The Reserve Method Of Accounting On
                  Becoming S Corporation — 
               
               
In  the case of a bank which changes from the reserve method of accounting  for bad
                  debts described in section 585 or 593 for its first taxable  year for which an election under section 1362(a) is in    effect, the bank  may elect to take into account any adjustments under section
                  481 by
                  reason of such change for the taxable year immediately preceding such first taxable
                  year.
