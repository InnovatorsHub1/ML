Sec. 1363. Effect Of Election On Corporation

I.R.C. § 1363(a) General Rule — 
               
               
Except  as otherwise provided in this subchapter, an S corporation shall not be  subject
                  to the taxes imposed by this chapter.
I.R.C. § 1363(b) Computation Of Corporation's Taxable Income — 
               
               
The  taxable income of an S corporation shall be computed in the same manner as  in
                  the case of an individual, except that— 

I.R.C. § 1363(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  items described in section 1366(a)(1)(A) shall be separately stated, 


I.R.C. § 1363(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  deductions referred to in section 703(a)(2) shall not be allowed to the  corporation, 


I.R.C. § 1363(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
section  248 shall apply, and 


I.R.C. § 1363(b)(4)  — 
                  
                  
section  291 shall apply if the S corporation (or any predecessor) was a C  corporation for any
                     of the 3 immediately preceding taxable years.
I.R.C. § 1363(c) Elections Of The S Corporation


I.R.C. § 1363(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Except  as provided in paragraph (2), any election affecting the computation of  items
                     derived from an S corporation shall be made by the corporation. 


I.R.C. § 1363(c)(2) Exceptions — 
                  
                  
In  the case of an S corporation, elections under the following provisions  shall
                     be made by each shareholder separately— 

I.R.C. § 1363(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
section  617 (relating to deduction and recapture of certain mining exploration  expenditures),
                        and 


I.R.C. § 1363(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
section  901 (relating to taxes of foreign countries and possessions of the United  States).
I.R.C. § 1363(d) Recapture Of LIFO Benefits


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
If— 

I.R.C. § 1363(d)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
an S  corporation was a C corporation for the last taxable year before the first 
                        taxable year for which the election under section 1362(a) was effective,  and 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  corporation inventoried goods under the LIFO method for such last taxable  year, 


the LIFO recapture amount shall be included in the gross income of  the corporation
                        for such last taxable year (and appropriate adjustments to  the basis of inventory
                        shall be made to take into account the amount  included in gross income under this
                        paragraph).



I.R.C. § 1363(d)(2) Additional Tax Payable In Installments


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Any  increase in the tax imposed by this chapter by reason of this subsection  shall
                        be payable in 4 equal installments. 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(2)(B) Date For Payment Of Installments — 
                     
                     
The  first installment under subparagraph (A) shall be paid on or before the  due
                        date (determined without regard to extensions)
                        for the return of the  tax imposed by this chapter for the last taxable year for which
                        the  corporation was a C corporation and the 3 succeeding installments shall be  paid
                        on or before the due date (as so determined)
                        for the corporation's  return for the 3 succeeding taxable years. 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(2)(C) No Interest For Period Of Extension — 
                     
                     
Notwithstanding  section 6601(b), for purposes of section 6601, the date prescribed for the  payment of each installment under this paragraph shall
                        be determined under  this paragraph. 



I.R.C. § 1363(d)(3) LIFO Recapture Amount — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection, the term “LIFO recapture amount” means the  amount
                     (if any) by which— 

I.R.C. § 1363(d)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  inventory amount of the inventory asset under the first-in, first-out  method
                        authorized by section 471, exceeds 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  inventory amount of such assets under the LIFO method. 


For purposes of the preceding sentence, inventory  amounts shall be determined as
                        of the close of the last taxable year  referred to in paragraph (1).



I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4) Other Definitions — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection— 

I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4)(A) LIFO Method — 
                     
                     
The  term “LIFO method” means the method authorized by section 472. 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4)(B) Inventory Assets — 
                     
                     
The  term “inventory assets” means stock in trade of the corporation, or other  property
                        of a kind which would properly be included in the inventory of  the corporation if
                        on hand at the close of the taxable year. 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4)(C) Method Of Determining Inventory Amount — 
                     
                     
The  inventory amount of assets under a method authorized by section 471 shall
                        be determined— 

I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4)(C)(i)  — 
                        
                        
if  the corporation uses the retail method of valuing inventories under  section 472, by using such method, or 


I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4)(C)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
if  clause (i) does not apply, by using cost or market, whichever is lower. 



I.R.C. § 1363(d)(4)(D) Not Treated As Member Of Affiliated Group — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in regulations, the corporation referred to in paragraph (1) 
                        shall not be treated as a member of an affiliated group with respect to  the amount
                        included in gross income under paragraph  (1). 



I.R.C. § 1363(d)(5) Special Rule — 
                  
                  
Sections  1367(a)(2)(D) and 1371(c)(1) shall not apply with respect to any  increase
                     in the tax imposed by reason of this subsection.
