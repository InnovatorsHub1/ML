Sec. 1373. Foreign Income

I.R.C. § 1373(a) S Corporation Treated As Partnership, Etc. — 
               
               
For  purposes of subparts A and F of part III, and part V, of subchapter N  (relating
                  to income from sources without the United States)— 

I.R.C. § 1373(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
an S  corporation shall be treated as a partnership, and 


I.R.C. § 1373(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  shareholders of such corporation shall be treated as partners of such  partnership.
I.R.C. § 1373(b) Recapture Of Overall Foreign Loss — 
               
               
For  purposes of section 904(f) (relating to recapture of overall foreign  loss), the making or termination of an
                  election to be treated as an S  corporation shall be treated as a disposition of the
                  business.
