Sec. 1375. Tax Imposed When Passive Investment Income Of Corporation Having Accumulated Earnings And Profits Exceeds 25 Percent Of Gross Receipts

I.R.C. § 1375(a) General Rule — 
               
               
If  for the taxable year an S corporation has— 

I.R.C. § 1375(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
accumulated earnings and profits at the close of such taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 1375(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
gross  receipts more than 25 percent of which are passive investment income, 


then  there is hereby imposed a tax on the income of such corporation for such  taxable
                     year. Such tax shall be computed by multiplying the excess net  passive income by
                     the highest rate of tax specified in section 11(b).
I.R.C. § 1375(b) Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1) Excess Net Passive Income


I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), the term “excess net passive income”  means
                        an amount which bears the same ratio to the net passive income for  the taxable year
                        as-- 

I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  amount by which the passive investment income for the taxable year exceeds  25
                           percent of the gross receipts for the taxable year, bears to 


I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the  passive investment income for the taxable year. 



I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1)(B) Limitation — 
                     
                     
The  amount of the excess net passive income for any taxable year shall not  exceed
                        the amount of the corporation's taxable income for such taxable  year as determined
                        under section 63(a)— 

I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
without  regard to the deductions allowed by part VIII of subchapter B (other than
                           the deduction allowed by section 248, relating to organization  expenditures), and 


I.R.C. § 1375(b)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
without  regard to the deduction under section 172. 




I.R.C. § 1375(b)(2) Net Passive Income — 
                  
                  
The  term “net passive income” means— 

I.R.C. § 1375(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
passive  investment income, reduced by 


I.R.C. § 1375(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  deductions allowable under this chapter which are directly connected with  the
                        production of such income (other than deductions allowable under  section 172 and part VIII of subchapter B). 



I.R.C. § 1375(b)(3) Passive Investment Income, Etc. — 
                  
                  
The  terms “passive investment income” and “gross receipts"
                     have the same  respective meanings as when used in paragraph (3) of section 1362(d). 


I.R.C. § 1375(b)(4) Coordination With Section 1374 — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding  paragraph (3), the amount of passive investment income shall be determined
                     by not taking into account any recognized built-in gain or loss of the S  corporation
                     for any taxable year in the recognition period. Terms used in  the preceding sentence
                     shall have the same respective meanings as when  used in section 1374.
I.R.C. § 1375(c) Credits Not Allowable — 
               
               
No  credit shall be allowed under part IV of subchapter A of this chapter  (other
                  than section 34)
                  against the tax imposed by subsection (a).
I.R.C. § 1375(d) Waiver Of Tax In Certain Cases — 
               
               
If  the S corporation establishes to the satisfaction of the Secretary that— 

I.R.C. § 1375(d)(1)  — 
                  
                  
it  determined in good faith that it had no  accumulated  earnings and profits  at
                     the close of a taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 1375(d)(2)  — 
                  
                  
during  a reasonable period of time after it was determined that it did have   accumulated
                     earnings and profits at the close of such taxable year such  earnings and profits
                     were distributed, 


the Secretary may waive the tax 
                     imposed by subsection (a) for such taxable year.
