Sec. 1378. Taxable Year Of S Corporation

I.R.C. § 1378(a) General Rule — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subtitle, the taxable year of an S corporation shall be a  permitted
                  year.
I.R.C. § 1378(b) Permitted Year Defined — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term “permitted year” means a taxable year  which— 

I.R.C. § 1378(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
is a  year ending December 31, or 


I.R.C. § 1378(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
is  any other accounting period for which the corporation establishes a  business
                     purpose to the satisfaction of the Secretary. 


For purposes of paragraph (2), any deferral of income to  shareholders shall not be
                     treated as a business purpose.
