Sec. 1385. Amounts Includible In Patron's Gross Income

I.R.C. § 1385(a) General Rule — 
               
               
Except  as otherwise provided in subsection (b), each person shall include in  gross
                  income— 

I.R.C. § 1385(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  amount of any patronage dividend which is paid in money, a qualified  written
                     notice of allocation, or other property (except a nonqualified  written notice of
                     allocation), and which is received by him during the  taxable year from an organization
                     described in section 1381(a), 


I.R.C. § 1385(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any  amount, described in section 1382(c)(2)(A)  (relating to certain  nonpatronage distributions by tax-exempt farmers' cooperatives),
                     which is  paid in money, a qualified written notice of allocation, or other property
                     (except a nonqualified written notice of allocation), and which is  received by him
                     during the taxable year from an organization described in  section 1381(a)(1), and 


I.R.C. § 1385(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
the  amount of any per-unit retain allocation which is paid in qualified  per-unit
                     retain certificates and which is received by him during the  taxable year from an
                     organization described in section 1381(a).
I.R.C. § 1385(b) Exclusion From Gross Income — 
               
               
Under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, the amount of any patronage  dividend,
                  and any amount received on the redemption, sale, or other  disposition of a nonqualified
                  written notice of allocation which was paid  as a patronage dividend, shall not be
                  included in gross income to the  extent that such amount— 

I.R.C. § 1385(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
is  properly taken into account as an adjustment to basis of property, or 


I.R.C. § 1385(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
is  attributable to personal, living, or family items.
I.R.C. § 1385(c) Treatment Of Certain Nonqualified Written Notices Of Allocation And Certain Nonqualified
                  Per-Unit Retain Certificates


I.R.C. § 1385(c)(1) Application Of Subsection — 
                  
                  
This  subsection shall apply to— 

I.R.C. § 1385(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any  nonqualified written notice of allocation which— 

I.R.C. § 1385(c)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
was  paid as a patronage dividend, or 


I.R.C. § 1385(c)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
was  paid by an organization described in section 1381(a)(1) on a patronage  basis with respect to earnings derived from business or sources described
                           in section 1382(c)(2)(A), and 



I.R.C. § 1385(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any  nonqualified per-unit retain certificate which was paid as a per-unit  retain
                        allocation. 



I.R.C. § 1385(c)(2) Basis; Amount Of Gain — 
                  
                  
In  the case of any nonqualified written notice of allocation or nonqualified  per-unit
                     retain certificate to which this subsection applies, for purposes  of this chapter— 

I.R.C. § 1385(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  basis of such written notice of allocation or per-unit retain certificate  in
                        the hands of the patron to whom such written notice of allocation or  per-unit retain
                        certificate was paid shall be zero, 


I.R.C. § 1385(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  basis of such written notice of allocation or per-unit retain certificate  which
                        was acquired from a decedent shall be its basis in the hands of the  decedent, and 


I.R.C. § 1385(c)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
gain  on the redemption, sale, or other disposition of such written notice of  allocation
                        or per-unit retain certificate by any person shall, to the  extent that the stated
                        dollar amount of such written notice of allocation  or per-unit retain certificate
                        exceeds its basis, be considered as  ordinary income.
