Sec. 1393. Definitions And Special Rules

I.R.C. § 1393(a) In General — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 1393(a)(1) Appropriate Secretary — 
                  
                  
The  term “appropriate Secretary” means— 

I.R.C. § 1393(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  Secretary of Housing and Urban Development in the case of any nominated  area
                        which is located in an urban area, and 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  Secretary of Agriculture in the case of any nominated area which is  located
                        in a rural area. 



I.R.C. § 1393(a)(2) Rural Area — 
                  
                  
The  term “rural area” means any area which is— 

I.R.C. § 1393(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
outside  of a metropolitan statistical area (within the meaning of section  143(k)(2)(B)), or 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
determined  by the Secretary of Agriculture, after consultation with the Secretary
                        of  Commerce, to be a rural area. 



I.R.C. § 1393(a)(3) Urban Area — 
                  
                  
The  term “urban area” means an area which is not a rural area. 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(4) Special Rules For Indian Reservations


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(4)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
No  empowerment zone or enterprise community may include any area within an  Indian
                        reservation. 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(4)(B) Indian Reservation Defined — 
                     
                     
The  term “Indian reservation” has the meaning given such term by section  168(j)(6). 



I.R.C. § 1393(a)(5) Local Government — 
                  
                  
The  term “local government” means— 

I.R.C. § 1393(a)(5)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any  county, city, town, township, parish, village, or other general purpose  political
                        subdivision of a State, and 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(5)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any  combination of political subdivisions described in subparagraph (A)  recognized
                        by the appropriate Secretary. 



I.R.C. § 1393(a)(6) Nominated Area — 
                  
                  
The  term “nominated area” means an area which is nominated by 1 or more local  governments
                     and the State or States in which it is located for designation  under section 1391. 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(7) Governments — 
                  
                  
If  more than 1 State or local government seeks to nominate an area under this  part,
                     any reference to, or requirement of, this subchapter shall apply to  all such governments. 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(8) Special Rule — 
                  
                  
An  area shall be treated as nominated by a State and a local government if it  is
                     nominated by an economic development corporation chartered by the  State. 


I.R.C. § 1393(a)(9) Use Of Census Data — 
                  
                  
Population  and poverty rate shall be determined by the most recent decennial census
                     data available.
I.R.C. § 1393(b) Empowerment Zone; Enterprise Community — 
               
               
For  purposes of this title, the terms ”empowerment zone” and “enterprise  community”
                  mean areas designated as such under section 1391.
