Sec. 1397F. Regulations

I.R.C. § 1397F(1)  — 
               
               
regulations  limiting the benefit of parts II and III in circumstances where such
                  benefits, in combination with benefits provided under other Federal  programs, would
                  result in an activity being 100 percent or more subsidized  by the Federal Government,
I.R.C. § 1397F(2)  — 
               
               
regulations  preventing abuse of the provisions of parts II and III, and
I.R.C. § 1397F(3)  — 
               
               
regulations  dealing with inadvertent failures of entities to be enterprise zone 
                  businesses.
