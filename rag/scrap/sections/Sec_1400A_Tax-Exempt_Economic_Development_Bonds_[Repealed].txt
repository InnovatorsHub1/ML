Sec. 1400A. Tax-Exempt Economic Development Bonds [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1400A(a) In General — 
               
               
In  the case of the  District of Columbia Enterprise Zone, subparagraph (A) of  section
                  1394(c)(1)  (relating to limitation on amount of bonds) shall be  applied by  substituting “$15,000,000”
                  for “$3,000,000”.
I.R.C. § 1400A(b) Period Of Applicability — 
               
               
This  section  shall apply to bonds issued during the period beginning on  January
                  1, 1998,  and ending on  December 31,
                  2011 and section  1394(b)(3)(B)(iii) shall be applied  without regard to the employee  residency  requirement.
