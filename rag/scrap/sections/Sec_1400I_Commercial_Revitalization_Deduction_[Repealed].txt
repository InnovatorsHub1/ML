Sec. 1400I. Commercial Revitalization Deduction [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1400I(a)  General Rule — 
               
               
At  the election of the taxpayer, either— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
one-half of any qualified revitalization expenditures chargeable to  capital account
                     with respect to any qualified revitalization building  shall be allowable as a deduction
                     for the taxable year in which the  building is placed in service, or 


I.R.C. § 1400I(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
a  deduction for all such expenditures shall be allowable ratably over the  120-month
                     period beginning with the month in which the building is placed  in service.
I.R.C. § 1400I(b)  Qualified Revitalization Buildings And Expenditures — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(1)  Qualified Revitalization Building — 
                  
                  
The  term “qualified revitalization building”
                     means any building (and its  structural components) if— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  building is placed in service by the taxpayer in a renewal community and  the
                        original use of the building begins with the taxpayer, or 


I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
in  the case of such building not described in subparagraph (A), such  building— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
is  substantially rehabilitated (within the meaning of section 47(c)(1)(C))
                           by  the taxpayer, and 


I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
is  placed in service by the taxpayer after the rehabilitation in a renewal  community. 




I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)  Qualified Revitalization Expenditure


I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)(A)  In General — 
                     
                     
The  term “qualified revitalization expenditure”
                        means any amount properly  chargeable to capital account for property for which depreciation
                        is  allowable under section 168 (without regard to this section)
                        and which  is— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
nonresidential real property (as defined in section 168(e)),
                           or 


I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
section 1250 property (as defined in section 1250(c)) which is  functionally related and subordinate to property described in clause 
                           (i). 



I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)(B)  Certain Expenditures Not Included


I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)(B)(i)  Acquisition Cost — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a building described in paragraph (1)(B), the cost of  acquiring the
                           building or interest therein shall be treated as a qualified  revitalization expenditure
                           only to the extent that such cost does not  exceed 30 percent of the aggregate qualified
                           revitalization expenditures  (determined without regard to such cost) with respect
                           to such  building. 


I.R.C. § 1400I(b)(2)(B)(ii)  Credits — 
                        
                        
The  term “qualified revitalization expenditure”
                           does not include any  expenditure which the taxpayer may take into account in computing
                           any  credit allowable under this title unless the taxpayer elects to take the  expenditure
                           into account only for purposes of this  section.
I.R.C. § 1400I(c)  Dollar Limitation — 
               
               
The  aggregate amount which may be treated as qualified revitalization  expenditures
                  with respect to any qualified revitalization building shall  not exceed the lesser
                  of— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
$10,000,000, or 


I.R.C. § 1400I(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  commercial revitalization expenditure amount allocated to such building  under
                     this section by the commercial revitalization agency for the State  in which the building
                     is located.
I.R.C. § 1400I(d)  Commercial Revitalization Expenditure Amount


I.R.C. § 1400I(d)(1)  In General — 
                  
                  
The  aggregate commercial revitalization expenditure amount which a commercial  revitalization
                     agency may allocate for any calendar year is the amount of  the State commercial revitalization
                     expenditure ceiling determined under  this paragraph for such calendar year for such
                     agency. 


I.R.C. § 1400I(d)(2)  State Commercial Revitalization Expenditure Ceiling — 
                  
                  
The  State commercial revitalization expenditure ceiling applicable to any  State— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
for  each calendar year after 2001 and
                        before 2010 is $12,000,000 for each  renewal community in the State, and 


I.R.C. § 1400I(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
for  each calendar year thereafter is zero. 



I.R.C. § 1400I(d)(3)  Commercial Revitalization Agency — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this section, the term “commercial revitalization agency”  means
                     any agency authorized by a State to carry out this  section. 


I.R.C. § 1400I(d)(4)  Time And Manner Of Allocations — 
                  
                  
Allocations  under this section shall be made at the same time and in the same manner
                     as under paragraphs (1) and (7)
                     of section 42(h).
I.R.C. § 1400I(e)  Responsibilities Of Commercial Revitalization Agencies


I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(1)  Plans For Allocation — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding  any other provision of this section, the commercial revitalization
                     expenditure amount with respect to any building shall be zero unless— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such  amount was allocated pursuant to a qualified allocation plan of the  commercial
                        revitalization agency which is approved (in accordance with  rules similar to the
                        rules of section 147(f)(2) (other than subparagraph  (B)(ii) thereof)) by the governmental unit of which such
                        agency is a part,  and 


I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such  agency notifies the chief executive officer (or its equivalent) of the  local
                        jurisdiction within which the building is located of such allocation  and provides
                        such individual a reasonable opportunity to comment on the  allocation. 



I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)  Qualified Allocation Plan — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection, the term “qualified allocation plan” means  any
                     plan— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
which sets forth selection criteria to be used to determine priorities of  the commercial
                        revitalization agency which are appropriate to local  conditions, 


I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
which considers— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  degree to which a project contributes to the implementation of a strategic  plan
                           that is devised for a renewal community through a citizen  participation process, 


I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the amount of any increase in permanent, full-time employment by reason of  any project,
                           and 


I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
the active involvement of residents and nonprofit groups within the  renewal community,
                           and 



I.R.C. § 1400I(e)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
which provides a procedure that the agency (or its agent) will follow in  monitoring
                        compliance with this
                        section.
I.R.C. § 1400I(f)  Special Rules


I.R.C. § 1400I(f)(1)  Deduction In Lieu Of Depreciation — 
                  
                  
The  deduction provided by this section for qualified revitalization  expenditures
                     shall— 

I.R.C. § 1400I(f)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
with  respect to the deduction determined under subsection (a)(1), be in lieu of 
                        any depreciation deduction otherwise allowable on account of one-half of  such expenditures,
                        and 


I.R.C. § 1400I(f)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
with  respect to the deduction determined
                        under subsection (a)(2), be in lieu  of any depreciation deduction otherwise  allowable
                        on account of all of  such expenditures. 



I.R.C. § 1400I(f)(2)  Basis Adjustment, Etc. — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of sections 1016 and 1250, the deduction under this section shall  be treated in the same manner as a depreciation
                     deduction. For purposes of  section 1250(b)(5), the straight line method of adjustment shall be  determined without regard to this
                     section. 


I.R.C. § 1400I(f)(3)  Substantial Rehabilitations Treated As Separate Buildings — 
                  
                  
A  substantial rehabilitation (within the meaning of section 47(c)(1)(C))
                     of  a building shall be treated as a separate building for purposes of  subsection
                     (a). 


I.R.C. § 1400I(f)(4)  Clarification Of Allowance Of Deduction Under Minimum Tax — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding  section 56(a)(1), the deduction under this section shall be allowed in  determining alternative minimum
                     taxable income under section  55.
I.R.C. § 1400I(g)  Termination — 
               
               
This  section shall not apply to any building placed in service after December  31,
                  2009.
