Sec. 1400P. Housing Tax Benefits [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1400P(a) Exclusion Of Employer Provided Housing For Individual Affected
                  By Hurricane Katrina


I.R.C. § 1400P(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Gross  income of a qualified employee shall not include
                     the  value of any  lodging furnished in-kind to such employee (and
                     such employee's       spouse or any of such employee's dependents)
                     by or on behalf of a  qualified employer for any month during the
                     taxable year. 


I.R.C. § 1400P(a)(2) Limitation — 
                  
                  
The  amount which may be excluded under paragraph (1)
                     for any month for  which lodging is furnished during the taxable year
                     shall not      exceed  $600. 


I.R.C. § 1400P(a)(3) Treatment Of Exclusion — 
                  
                  
The  exclusion under paragraph (1) shall be treated
                     as an exclusion under  section 119 (other
                     than for purposes of sections 3121(a)(19) and
                     3306(b)(14)).
I.R.C. § 1400P(b) Employer Credit For Housing Employees Affected By Hurricane
                  Katrina — 
               
               
For  purposes of section 38,
                  in the case of a qualified employer, the Hurricane Katrina housing
                  credit for any month during the taxable year is an  amount equal to
                  30 percent of any amount which is excludable from the  gross income
                  of a qualified employee of such employer under subsection  (a) and
                  not otherwise excludable under section 119.
I.R.C. § 1400P(c) Qualified Employee — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term “qualified employee"
                  means, with  respect to any month, an individual— 

I.R.C. § 1400P(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
who  had a principal residence (as
                     defined in section 121)
                     in the Gulf       Opportunity Zone on August 28, 2005, and 


I.R.C. § 1400P(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
who  performs substantially all employment
                     services— 

I.R.C. § 1400P(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
in  the Gulf Opportunity Zone, and 


I.R.C. § 1400P(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
for  the qualified employer which furnishes
                        lodging to such  individual.
I.R.C. § 1400P(d) Qualified Employer — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term “‘qualified
                  employer” means any  employer with a trade or business located in
                  the Gulf Opportunity  Zone.
I.R.C. § 1400P(e) Certain Rules To Apply — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subsection, rules similar to the
                  rules of sections  51(i)(1) and 52 shall apply.
I.R.C. § 1400P(f) Application Of Section — 
               
               
This  section shall apply to lodging furnished during
                  the period— 

I.R.C. § 1400P(f)(1)  — 
                  
                  
beginning on the first day of the
                     first month beginning after the date of the enactment of this section,
                     and 


I.R.C. § 1400P(f)(2)  — 
                  
                  
ending on the date which is 6 months
                     after the first day described in paragraph (1).
