Sec. 1400T. Special Rules For Mortgage Revenue Bonds [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1400T(a) In General — 
               
               
In  the case of financing provided with respect to 
                  owner-occupied  residences in the GO Zone, the Rita GO Zone, or
                  the Wilma GO    Zone,  section 143 shall
                  be applied— 

I.R.C. § 1400T(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
by  treating any such residence in the
                     Rita GO Zone or the Wilma GO Zone as a targeted area residence, 


I.R.C. § 1400T(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
by  applying subsection (f)(3) thereof
                     without regard to subparagraph (A)  thereof, and 


I.R.C. § 1400T(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
by  substituting “$150,000” for “$15,000"
                     in subsection (k)(4) thereof.
I.R.C. § 1400T(b) Application — 
               
               
Subsection  (a) shall not apply to financing provided
                  after December 31, 2010.
