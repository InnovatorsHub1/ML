Sec. 1400U-1. Allocation Of Recovery Zone Bonds [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a) Allocations


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(1) In General


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(1)(A) General Allocation — 
                     
                     
The Secretary shall allocate the national recovery zone
                        economic development bond limitation and the national recovery zone
                        facility bond limitation among the States in the proportion that each
                        such State's 2008 State employment decline bears to the aggregate
                        of the 2008 State employment declines for all of the States. 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(1)(B) Minimum Allocation — 
                     
                     
The Secretary shall adjust the allocations under subparagraph
                        (A) for any calendar year for each State to the extent necessary to
                        ensure that no State receives less than 0.9 percent of the national
                        recovery zone economic development bond limitation and 0.9 percent
                        of the national recovery zone facility bond limitation. 



I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(2) 2008 State Employment Decline — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this subsection, the term “2008
                     State employment decline” means, with respect to any State,
                     the excess (if any) of— 

I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the number of individuals employed in
                        such State determined for December 2007, over 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the number of individuals employed in
                        such State determined for December 2008. 



I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(3) Allocations By States


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Each State with respect to which an allocation is made
                        under paragraph (1) shall reallocate such allocation among the counties
                        and large municipalities in such State in the proportion to each such
                        county's or municipality's 2008 employment decline bears to the aggregate
                        of the 2008 employment declines for all the counties and municipalities
                        in such State. A county or municipality may waive any portion of an
                        allocation made under this subparagraph. 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(3)(B) Large Municipalities — 
                     
                     
For purposes of subparagraph (A), the term “large
                        municipality” means a municipality with a population of more
                        than 100,000. 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(3)(C) Determination Of Local Employment Declines — 
                     
                     
For purposes of this paragraph, the employment decline
                        of any municipality or county shall be determined in the same manner
                        as determining the State employment decline under paragraph (2), except
                        that in the case of a municipality any portion of which is in a county,
                        such portion shall be treated as part of such municipality and not
                        part of such county. 



I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(4) National Limitations


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(4)(A) Recovery Zone Economic Development Bonds — 
                     
                     
There is a national recovery zone economic development
                        bond limitation of $10,000,000,000. 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(a)(4)(B) Recovery Zone Facility Bonds — 
                     
                     
There is a national recovery zone facility bond limitation
                        of $15,000,000,000.
I.R.C. § 1400U-1(b) Recovery Zone — 
               
               
For purposes of this part, the term “recovery
                  zone” means— 

I.R.C. § 1400U-1(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any area designated by the issuer as
                     having significant poverty, unemployment, rate of home foreclosures,
                     or general distress, 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any area designated by the issuer as
                     economically distressed by reason of the closure or realignment of
                     a military installation pursuant to the Defense Base Closure and Realignment
                     Act of 1990, and 


I.R.C. § 1400U-1(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
any area for which a designation as an
                     empowerment zone or renewal community is in effect.
