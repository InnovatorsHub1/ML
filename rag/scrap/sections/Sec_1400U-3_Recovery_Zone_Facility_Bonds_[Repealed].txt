Sec. 1400U-3. Recovery Zone Facility Bonds [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 1400U-3(a) In General — 
               
               
—For purposes of part IV of subchapter B (relating
                  to tax exemption requirements for State and local bonds), the term ‘exempt
                  facility bond’ includes any recovery zone facility bond.
I.R.C. § 1400U-3(b) Recovery Zone Facility Bond


I.R.C. § 1400U-3(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this section, the term ‘recovery
                     zone facility bond’ means any bond issued as part of an issue
                     if— 

I.R.C. § 1400U-3(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
95 percent or more of the net proceeds
                        (as defined in section 150(a)(3))
                        of such issue are to be used for recovery zone property, 


I.R.C. § 1400U-3(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such bond is issued before January 1,
                        2011, and 


I.R.C. § 1400U-3(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
the issuer designates such bond for
                        purposes of this section. 



I.R.C. § 1400U-3(b)(2) Limitation On Amount Of Bonds Designated — 
                  
                  
The maximum aggregate face amount of bonds which may
                     be designated by any issuer under paragraph (1) shall not exceed the
                     amount of recovery zone facility bond limitation allocated to such
                     issuer under section 1400U–1.
I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c) Recovery Zone Property — 
               
               
For purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The term “recovery zone property” means any
                     property to which section 168 applies
                     (or would apply but for section 179)
                     if— 

I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such property was constructed, reconstructed,
                        renovated, or acquired by purchase (as defined in section 179(d)(2)) by the taxpayer
                        after the date on which the designation of the recovery zone took
                        effect, 


I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the original use of which in the recovery
                        zone commences with the taxpayer, and 


I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
substantially all of the use of which
                        is in the recovery zone and is in the active conduct of a qualified
                        business by the taxpayer in such zone. 



I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(2) Qualified Business — 
                  
                  
The term “qualified business” means any trade
                     or business except that— 

I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the rental to others of real property
                        located in a recovery zone shall be treated as a qualified business
                        only if the property is not residential rental property (as defined
                        in section 168(e)(2)),
                        and 


I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such term shall not include any trade
                        or business consisting of the operation of any facility described
                        in section 144(c)(6)(B). 



I.R.C. § 1400U-3(c)(3) Special Rules For Substantial Renovations And Sale-Leaseback — 
                  
                  
Rules similar to the rules of subsections (a)(2) and
                     (b) of section 1397D shall
                     apply for purposes of this subsection.
I.R.C. § 1400U-3(d) Nonapplication Of Certain Rules — 
               
               
Sections 146 (relating
                  to volume cap) and 147(d) (relating
                  to acquisition of existing property not permitted) shall not apply
                  to any recovery zone facility bond.
