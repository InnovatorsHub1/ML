Sec. 2044. Certain Property For Which Marital Deduction Was Previously Allowed

I.R.C. § 2044(a) General Rule — 
               
               
The  value of the gross estate shall include the value of any property to which  this
                  section applies in which the decedent had a qualifying income  interest for life.
I.R.C. § 2044(b) Property To Which This Section Applies — 
               
               
This  section applies to any property if— 

I.R.C. § 2044(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
a deduction was allowed with respect to the transfer of  such property to the decedent— 

I.R.C. § 2044(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
under section 2056 by reason of subsection (b)(7)  thereof, or 


I.R.C. § 2044(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
under section 2523 by reason of subsection (f) thereof,  and 



I.R.C. § 2044(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
section 2519 (relating to dispositions of certain life  estates) did not apply with respect to
                     a disposition by the decedent of  part or all of such property.
I.R.C. § 2044(c) Property Treated As Having Passed From Decedent — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter and chapter 13, property includible in the gross  estate
                  of the decedent under subsection (a) shall be treated as property
                  passing from the decedent.
