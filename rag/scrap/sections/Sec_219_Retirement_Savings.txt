Sec. 219. Retirement Savings

I.R.C. § 219(a) Allowance Of Deduction — 
               
               
In the case of an individual, there shall be allowed as a deduction an amount equal
                  to the qualified retirement contributions of the individual for the taxable year.
I.R.C. § 219(b) Maximum Amount Of Deduction


I.R.C. § 219(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The amount allowable as a deduction under subsection (a) to any individual for any
                     taxable year shall not exceed the lesser of— 

I.R.C. § 219(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the deductible amount, or 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
an amount equal to the compensation includible in the individual's gross income for
                        such taxable year. 



I.R.C. § 219(b)(2) Special Rule For Employer Contributions Under Simplified Employee Pensions — 
                  
                  
This section shall not apply with respect to an employer contribution to a simplified
                     employee pension. 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(3) Plans Under Section 501(c)(18) — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding paragraph (1), the amount allowable as a deduction under subsection
                     (a) with respect to any contributions on behalf of an employee to a plan described
                     in section 501(c)(18) shall not exceed the lesser of— 

I.R.C. § 219(b)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
$7,000, or 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
an amount equal to 25 percent of the compensation (as defined in section 415(c)(3)) includible in the individual's gross income for such taxable year. 



I.R.C. § 219(b)(4) Special Rule For Simple Retirement Accounts — 
                  
                  
This section shall not apply with respect to any amount contributed to a simple retirement
                     account established under section 408(p). 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5) Deductible Amount — 
                  
                  
For purposes of paragraph (1)(A)— 

I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The deductible amount is $5,000. 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(B) Catch-Up Contributions For Individuals 50 Or Older


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(B)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
In the case of an individual who has attained the age of 50 before the close of the
                           taxable year, the deductible amount for such taxable year shall be increased by the
                           applicable amount. 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(B)(ii) Applicable Amount — 
                        
                        
For purposes of clause (i), the applicable amount is $1,000. 



I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C) Cost-Of-Living Adjustment


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
In the case of any taxable year beginning in a calendar year after 2008, the $5,000
                           amount under subparagraph (A) shall be increased by an amount equal to— 

I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(i)(I)  — 
                           
                           
such dollar amount, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(i)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for the calendar year in which the taxable year begins, determined by substituting
                              “calendar year 2007” for “calendar year 2016” in subparagraph (A)(ii) thereof. 



I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(ii) Rounding Rules — 
                        
                        
If any amount after adjustment under clause (i) is not a multiple of $500, such amount
                           shall be rounded to the next lower multiple of $500. 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(iii) Indexing of Catch-up Limitation — 
                        
                        
In the case of any taxable year beginning in a calendar year after 2023, the $1,000
                           amount under subparagraph (B)(ii) shall be increased by an amount equal to-- 

I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(iii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
such dollar amount, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 219(b)(5)(C)(iii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
— the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for the calendar
                              year in which the taxable year begins, determined by substituting ‘calendar year 2022’
                              for ‘calendar year 2016’ in subparagraph (A)(ii) thereof. 


If any amount after adjustment under the preceding sentence is not a multiple of $100,
                              such amount shall be rounded to the next lower multiple of $100.
I.R.C. § 219(c) Kay Bailey Hutchison Spousal IRA


I.R.C. § 219(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In the case of an individual to whom this paragraph applies for the taxable year,
                     the limitation of paragraph (1) of subsection (b) shall be equal to the lesser of— 

I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the dollar amount in effect under subsection (b)(1)(A) for the taxable year, or 


I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the compensation includible in such individual's gross income for the taxable year,
                           plus 


I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the compensation includible in the gross income of such individual's spouse for the
                           taxable year reduced by-- 

I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(B)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
the amount allowed as a deduction under subsection (a) to such spouse for such taxable
                              year, 


I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(B)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the amount of any designated nondeductible contribution (as defined in section 408(o)) on behalf of such spouse for such taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 219(c)(1)(B)(ii)(III)  — 
                           
                           
the amount of any contribution on behalf of such spouse to a Roth IRA under section
                              408A for such taxable year. 





I.R.C. § 219(c)(2) Individuals To Whom Paragraph (1) Applies — 
                  
                  
Paragraph (1) shall apply to any individual if— 

I.R.C. § 219(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such individual files a joint return for the taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 219(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the amount of compensation (if any) includible in such individual's gross income for
                        the taxable year is less than the compensation includible in the gross income of such
                        individual's spouse for the taxable year.
I.R.C. § 219(d) Other Limitations And Restrictions


I.R.C. § 219(d)(1) Repealed — 
                  
                  
[Repealed by Pub. L. 116-94, Div. O, Sec. 107(a).] 


I.R.C. § 219(d)(2) Recontributed Amounts — 
                  
                  
No deduction shall be allowed under this section with respect to a rollover contribution
                     described in section 402(c), 403(a)(4), 403(b)(8), 408(d)(3), or 457(e)(16). 


I.R.C. § 219(d)(3) Amounts Contributed Under Endowment Contract — 
                  
                  
In the case of an endowment contract described in section 408(b), no deduction shall be allowed under this section for that portion of the amounts
                     paid under the contract for the taxable year which is properly allocable, under regulations
                     prescribed by the Secretary, to the cost of life insurance. 


I.R.C. § 219(d)(4) Denial Of Deduction For Amount Contributed To Inherited Annuities Or Accounts — 
                  
                  
No deduction shall be allowed under this section with respect to any amount paid to
                     an inherited individual retirement account or individual retirement annuity (within
                     the meaning of section 408(d)(3)(C)(ii)).
I.R.C. § 219(e) Qualified Retirement Contribution — 
               
               
For purposes of this section, the term “qualified retirement contribution” means— 

I.R.C. § 219(e)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any amount paid in cash for the taxable year by or on behalf of an individual to an
                     individual retirement plan for such individual's benefit, and 


I.R.C. § 219(e)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any amount contributed on behalf of any individual to a plan described in section
                     501(c)(18).
I.R.C. § 219(f) Other Definitions And Special Rules


I.R.C. § 219(f)(1) Compensation — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this section, the term “compensation” includes earned income (as defined
                     in section 401(c)(2)). The term “compensation” does not include any amount received as a pension or annuity
                     and does not include any amount received as deferred compensation. For purposes of
                     this paragraph, section 401(c)(2) shall be applied as if the term trade or business for purposes of section 1402 included service described in subsection (c)(6). The term “compensation” includes
                     any differential wage payment (as defined in section 3401(h)(2)). The term “compensation” shall include any amount which is included in the individual's
                     gross income and paid to the individual to aid the individual in the pursuit of graduate
                     or postdoctoral study. 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(2) Married Individuals — 
                  
                  
The maximum deduction under subsection (b) shall be computed separately for each individual,
                     and this section shall be applied without regard to any community property laws. 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(3) Time When Contributions Deemed Made — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this section, a taxpayer shall be deemed to have made a contribution
                     to an individual retirement plan on the last day of the preceding taxable year if
                     the contribution is made on account of such taxable year and is made not later than
                     the time prescribed by law for filing the return for such taxable year (not including
                     extensions thereof). 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(4) Reports — 
                  
                  
[Repealed. Pub. L. 113-295, Div. A, title II, Sec. 221(a)(39)(A).] 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(5) Employer Payments — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this title, any amount paid by an employer to an individual retirement
                     plan shall be treated as payment of compensation to the employee (other than a self-employed
                     individual who is an employee within the meaning of section 401(c)(1)) includible in his gross income in the taxable year for which the amount was contributed,
                     whether or not a deduction for such payment is allowable under this section to the
                     employee. 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(6) Excess Contributions Treated As Contribution Made During Subsequent Year For Which
                     There Is An Unused Limitation


I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
If for the taxable year the maximum amount allowable as a deduction under this section
                        for contributions to an individual retirement plan exceeds the amount contributed,
                        then the taxpayer shall be treated as having made an additional contribution for the
                        taxable year in an amount equal to the lesser of-- 

I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the amount of such excess, or 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the amount of the excess contributions for such taxable year (determined under section
                           4973(b)(2) without regard to subparagraph (C) thereof). 



I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(B) Amount Contributed — 
                     
                     
For purposes of this paragraph, the amount contributed— 

I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
shall be determined without regard to this paragraph, and 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
shall not include any rollover contribution. 



I.R.C. § 219(f)(6)(C) Special Rule Where Excess Deduction Was Allowed For Closed Year — 
                     
                     
Proper reduction shall be made in the amount allowable as a deduction by reason of
                        this paragraph for any amount allowed as a deduction under this section for a prior
                        taxable year for which the period for assessing deficiency has expired if the amount
                        so allowed exceeds the amount which should have been allowed for such prior taxable
                        year. 



I.R.C. § 219(f)(7) Special Rule For Compensation Earned By Members Of The Armed Forces For Service In
                     A Combat Zone — 
                  
                  
For purposes of subsections (b)(1)(B) and (c), the amount of compensation includible
                     in an individual's gross income shall be determined without regard to section 112. 


I.R.C. § 219(f)(8) Election Not To Deduct Contributions — 
                  
                  
For election not to deduct contributions to individual retirement plans, see section
                     408(o)(2)(B)(ii).
I.R.C. § 219(g) Limitation On Deduction For Active Participants In Certain Pension Plans


I.R.C. § 219(g)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
If (for any part of any plan year ending with or within a taxable year) an individual
                     or the individual's spouse is an active participant, each of the dollar limitations
                     contained in subsections (b)(1)(A) and (c)(1)(A) for such taxable year shall be reduced
                     (but not below zero) by the amount determined under paragraph (2). 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(2) Amount Of Reduction


I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The amount determined under this paragraph with respect to any dollar limitation shall
                        be the amount which bears the same ratio to such limitation as— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the excess of— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(A)(i)(I)  — 
                           
                           
the taxpayer's adjusted gross income for such taxable year, over 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(A)(i)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the applicable dollar amount, bears to 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
$10,000 ($20,000 in the case of a joint return). 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(B) No Reduction Below $200 Until Complete Phase-Out — 
                     
                     
No dollar limitation shall be reduced below $200 under paragraph (1) unless (without
                        regard to this subparagraph) such limitation is reduced to zero. 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(2)(C) Rounding — 
                     
                     
Any amount determined under this paragraph which is not a multiple of $10 shall be
                        rounded to the next lowest $10. 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(3) Adjusted Gross Income; Applicable Dollar Amount — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this subsection— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(A) Adjusted Gross Income — 
                     
                     
Adjusted gross income of any taxpayer shall be determined— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
after application of sections 86 and 469, and 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
without regard to sections 85(c), 135, 137, 221, and 911 or the deduction allowable under this section. 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(B) Applicable Dollar Amount — 
                     
                     
The term “applicable dollar amount” means the following: 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
In the case of a taxpayer filing a joint return, $80,000. 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
In the case of any other taxpayer (other than a married individual filing a separate
                           return), $50,000. 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(3)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
In the case of a married individual filing a separate return, zero. 




I.R.C. § 219(g)(4) Special Rule For Married Individuals Filing Separately And Living Apart — 
                  
                  
A husband and wife who— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(4)(A)  — 
                     
                     
file separate returns for any taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(4)(B)  — 
                     
                     
live apart at all times during such taxable year, shall not be treated as married
                        individuals for purposes of this subsection. 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(5) Active Participant — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this subsection, the term “active participant” means, with respect
                     to any plan year, an individual— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)  — 
                     
                     
who is an active participant in— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a plan described in section 401(a) which includes a trust exempt from tax under section 501(a), 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
an annuity plan described in section 403(a), 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
a plan established for its employees by the United States, by a State or political
                           subdivision thereof, or by an agency or instrumentality of any of the foregoing, 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
an annuity contract described in section 403(b), 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)(v)  — 
                        
                        
a simplified employee pension (within the meaning of section 408(k)), or 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(A)(vi)  — 
                        
                        
any simple retirement account (within the meaning of section 408(p)), or 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(5)(B)  — 
                     
                     
who makes deductible contributions to a trust described in section 501(c)(18). 


The determination of whether an individual is an active participant shall be made
                        without regard to whether or not such individual's rights under a plan, trust, or
                        contract are nonforfeitable. An eligible deferred compensation plan (within the meaning
                        of section 457(b)) shall not be treated as a plan described in subparagraph (A)(iii).



I.R.C. § 219(g)(6) Certain Individuals Not Treated As Active Participants — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this subsection, any individual described in any of the following
                     subparagraphs shall not be treated as an active participant for any taxable year solely
                     because of any participation so described: 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(6)(A) Members Of Reserve Components — 
                     
                     
Participation in a plan described in subparagraph (A)(iii) of paragraph (5) by reason
                        of service as a member of a reserve component of the Armed Forces (as defined in section
                        10101 of title 10), unless such individual has served in excess of 90 days on active
                        duty (other than active duty for training) during the year. 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(6)(B) Volunteer Firefighters — 
                     
                     
A volunteer firefighter— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(6)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
who is a participant in a plan described in subparagraph (A)(iii) of paragraph (5)
                           based on his activity as a volunteer firefighter, and 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(6)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
whose accrued benefit as of the beginning of the taxable year is not more than an
                           annual benefit of $1,800 (when expressed as a single life annuity commencing at age
                           65). 




I.R.C. § 219(g)(7) Special Rule For Spouses Who Are Not Active Participants — 
                  
                  
If this subsection applies to an individual for any taxable year solely because their
                     spouse is an active participant, then, in applying this subsection to the individual
                     (but not their spouse)— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(7)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the applicable dollar amount under paragraph (3)(B)(i) shall be $150,000, and 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(7)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the amount applicable under paragraph (2)(A)(ii) shall be $10,000. 



I.R.C. § 219(g)(8) Inflation Adjustment — 
                  
                  
In the case of any taxable year beginning in a calendar year after 2006, each of the
                     dollar amounts in paragraphs (3)(B)(i), (3)(B)(ii), and (7)(A) shall be increased
                     by an amount equal to— 

I.R.C. § 219(g)(8)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such dollar amount, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 219(g)(8)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for the calendar year in which the taxable year begins, determined by substituting
                        “calendar year 2005” for “calendar year 2016” in subparagraph (A)(ii) thereof. 


Any increase determined under the preceding sentence shall be rounded to the nearest
                        multiple of $1,000.
