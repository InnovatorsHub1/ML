Sec. 244. Dividends Received On Certain Preferred Stock [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 244(a) General Rule — 
               
               
In  the case of a corporation, there shall be allowed as a deduction an amount  computed
                  as follows: 

I.R.C. § 244(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
First  determine the amount received as dividends on the preferred stock of a  public
                     utility which is subject to taxation under this chapter and with  respect to which
                     the deduction provided in section 247 for dividends paid  is allowable. 


I.R.C. § 244(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
Then  multiply the amount determined under paragraph (1) by the fraction— 

I.R.C. § 244(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  numerator of which is 14 percent, and 


I.R.C. § 244(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  denominator of which is that percentage which equals the highest rate of  tax
                        specified in section 11(b). 



I.R.C. § 244(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
Finally  ascertain the amount which is 70 percent of the excess of— 

I.R.C. § 244(a)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  amount determined under paragraph
                        (1), over 


I.R.C. § 244(a)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  amount determined under paragraph
                        (2).
I.R.C. § 244(b) Exception — 
               
               
If  the dividends described in subsection (a)(1) are qualifying dividends (as  defined
                  in section 243(b)(1), but determined without regard to section  243(d)(4))— 

I.R.C. § 244(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
subsection  (a) shall be applied separately to such qualifying dividends, and 


I.R.C. § 244(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
for  purposes of subsection (a)(3), the percentage applicable to such  qualifying
                     dividends shall be 100 percent in lieu of 70 percent.
