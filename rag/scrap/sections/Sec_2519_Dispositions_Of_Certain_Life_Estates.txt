Sec. 2519. Dispositions Of Certain Life Estates

I.R.C. § 2519(a) General Rule — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter and chapter 11, any disposition of all or part of  a
                  qualifying income interest for life in any property to which this  section applies
                  shall be treated as a transfer of all interests in such  property other than the qualifying
                  income interest.
I.R.C. § 2519(b) Property To Which This Subsection Applies — 
               
               
This  section applies to any property if a deduction was allowed with respect to 
                  the transfer of such property to the donor— 

I.R.C. § 2519(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
under section 2056 by reason of subsection (b)(7)
                     thereof, or 


I.R.C. § 2519(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
under section 2523 by reason of subsection (f)
                     thereof.
I.R.C. § 2519(c) Cross Reference — 
               
               
For  right of recovery for gift tax in the case of property treated as  transferred
                  under this section, see section 2207A(b).
