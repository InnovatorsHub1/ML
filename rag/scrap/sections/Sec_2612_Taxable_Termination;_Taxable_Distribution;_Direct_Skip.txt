Sec. 2612. Taxable Termination; Taxable Distribution; Direct Skip

I.R.C. § 2612(a) Taxable Termination


I.R.C. § 2612(a)(1) General Rule — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this chapter, the term “taxable termination"
                     means the  termination (by death, lapse of time, release of power, or otherwise) of
                     an interest in property held in a trust unless— 

I.R.C. § 2612(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
immediately after such termination, a non-skip person  has an interest in such property,
                        or 


I.R.C. § 2612(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
at no time after such termination may a distribution  (including distributions on
                        termination) be made from such trust to a skip  person. 



I.R.C. § 2612(a)(2) Certain Partial Terminations Treated As Taxable — 
                  
                  
If,  upon the termination of an interest in property held in trust by reason of  the
                     death of a lineal descendant of the transferor, a specified portion of  the trust's
                     assets are distributed to 1 or more skip persons (or 1 or more  trusts for the exclusive
                     benefit of such persons), such termination shall  constitute a taxable termination
                     with respect to such portion of the trust  property.
I.R.C. § 2612(b) Taxable Distribution — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter, the term “taxable distribution"
                  means any  distribution from a trust to a skip person (other than a taxable  termination
                  or a direct skip).
I.R.C. § 2612(c) Direct Skip — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter— 

I.R.C. § 2612(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  term “direct skip” means a transfer subject to a tax imposed by chapter 11  or
                     12 of an interest in property to a skip person. 


I.R.C. § 2612(c)(2) Look-Thru Rules Not To Apply — 
                  
                  
Solely  for purposes of determining whether any transfer to a trust is a direct  skip,
                     the rules of section 2651(f)(2) shall not apply.
