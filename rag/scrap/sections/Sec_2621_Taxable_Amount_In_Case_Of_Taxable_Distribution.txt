Sec. 2621. Taxable Amount In Case Of Taxable Distribution

I.R.C. § 2621(a) In General — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter, the taxable amount in the case of any taxable  distribution
                  shall be— 

I.R.C. § 2621(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the value of the property received by the transferee,  reduced by 


I.R.C. § 2621(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any expense incurred by the transferee in connection  with the determination, collection,
                     or refund of the tax imposed by this  chapter with respect to such distribution.
I.R.C. § 2621(b) Payment Of GST Tax Treated As Taxable Distribution — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter, if any of the tax imposed by this chapter with  respect
                  to any taxable distribution is paid out of the trust, an amount  equal to the portion
                  so paid shall be treated as a taxable distribution.
