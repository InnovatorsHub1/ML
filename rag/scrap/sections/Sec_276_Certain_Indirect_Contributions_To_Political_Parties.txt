Sec. 276. Certain Indirect Contributions To Political Parties

I.R.C. § 276(a) Disallowance Of Deduction — 
               
               
No  deduction otherwise allowable under this chapter shall be allowed for any  amount
                  paid or incurred for— 

I.R.C. § 276(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
advertising  in a convention program of a political party, or in any other publication
                     if any part of the proceeds of such publication directly or indirectly  inures (or
                     is intended to inure) to or for the use of a political party or  a political candidate, 


I.R.C. § 276(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
admission  to any dinner or program, if any part of the proceeds of such dinner or
                     program directly or indirectly inures (or is intended to inure) to or for  the use
                     of a political party or a political candidate, or 


I.R.C. § 276(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
admission  to an inaugural ball, inaugural gala, inaugural parade, or inaugural  concert,
                     or to any similar event which is identified with a political  party or a political
                     candidate.
I.R.C. § 276(b) Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 276(b)(1) Political Party — 
                  
                  
The  term “political party” means— 

I.R.C. § 276(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
a  political party; 


I.R.C. § 276(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
a  National, State, or local committee of a political party; or 


I.R.C. § 276(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
a  committee, association, or organization, whether incorporated or not,  which directly
                        or indirectly accepts contributions (as defined in section  271(b)(2)) or make expenditures
                        (as defined in section 271(b)(3))
                        for the  purpose of influencing or attempting to influence the selection,
                        nomination, or election of any individual to any Federal, State, or local  elective
                        public office, or the election of presidential and  vice-presidential electors, whether
                        or not such individual or electors are  selected, nominated, or elected. 



I.R.C. § 276(b)(2) Proceeds Inuring To Or For The Use Of Political Candidates — 
                  
                  
Proceeds  shall be treated as inuring to or for the use of a political candidate 
                     only if— 

I.R.C. § 276(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such  proceeds may be used directly or indirectly for the purpose of furthering  his
                        candidacy for selection, nomination, or election to any elective  public office, and 


I.R.C. § 276(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such  proceeds are not received by such candidate in the ordinary course of a  trade
                        or business (other than the trade or business of holding elective  public office).
I.R.C. § 276(c) Cross Reference — 
               
               
For  disallowance of certain entertainment, etc., expenses, see section 274.
