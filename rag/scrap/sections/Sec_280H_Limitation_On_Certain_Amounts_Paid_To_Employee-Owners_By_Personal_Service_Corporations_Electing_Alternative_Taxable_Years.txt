Sec. 280H. Limitation On Certain Amounts Paid To Employee-Owners By Personal Service Corporations Electing Alternative Taxable Years

I.R.C. § 280H(a) General Rule — 
               
               
If— 

I.R.C. § 280H(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
an  election by a personal service corporation under section 444 is in effect  for a taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 280H(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
such  corporation does not meet the minimum distribution requirements of  subsection
                     (c) for such taxable year, 


then the deduction otherwise allowed
                     under this chapter for applicable amounts paid or incurred by such
                     corporation to employee-owners shall not exceed the maximum deductible
                     amount. The preceding sentence shall not apply for purposes of subchapter
                     G (relating to personal holding companies).
I.R.C. § 280H(b) Carryover Of Nondeductible Amounts — 
               
               
If  any amount is not allowed as a deduction for a taxable year under  subsection
                  (a), such amount shall be treated as paid or incurred in the  succeeding taxable year.
I.R.C. § 280H(c) Minimum Distribution Requirement — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 280H(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
A  personal service corporation meets the minimum distribution requirements  of this
                     subsection if the applicable amounts paid or incurred during the  deferral period
                     of the taxable year (determined without regard to  subsection (b)) equal or exceed
                     the lesser of— 

I.R.C. § 280H(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  product of— 

I.R.C. § 280H(c)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  applicable amounts paid during the preceding taxable year, divided by the  number
                           of months in such taxable year, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 280H(c)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the  number of months in the deferral period of the preceding taxable year, or 



I.R.C. § 280H(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  applicable percentage of the adjusted taxable income for the deferral  period
                        of the taxable year. 



I.R.C. § 280H(c)(2) Applicable Percentage — 
                  
                  
The  term “applicable percentage” means the percentage
                     (not in excess of 95  percent) determined by dividing— 

I.R.C. § 280H(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  applicable amounts paid or incurred during the 3 taxable years immediately  preceding
                        the taxable year, by 


I.R.C. § 280H(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  adjusted taxable income of such corporation for such 3 taxable years.
I.R.C. § 280H(d) Maximum Deductible Amount — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term “maximum deductible amount” means the  sum
                  of— 

I.R.C. § 280H(d)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  applicable amounts paid during the deferral period, plus 


I.R.C. § 280H(d)(2)  — 
                  
                  
an  amount equal to the product of— 

I.R.C. § 280H(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  amount determined under paragraph
                        (1), divided by the number of months in  the deferral period, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 280H(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  number of months in the nondeferral period.
I.R.C. § 280H(e) Disallowance Of Net Operating Loss Carrybacks — 
               
               
No  net operating loss carryback shall be allowed to
                  (or from) any taxable  year of a personal service corporation to which an election
                  under section  444 applies.
I.R.C. § 280H(f) Other Definitions And Special Rules — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 280H(f)(1) Applicable Amount — 
                  
                  
The  term “applicable amount” means any amount paid to an employee-owner which  is
                     includible in the gross income of such employee, other than-- 

I.R.C. § 280H(f)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any  gain from the sale or exchange of property between the owner-employee and  the
                        corporation, or 


I.R.C. § 280H(f)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any  dividend paid by the corporation. 



I.R.C. § 280H(f)(2) Employee-Owner — 
                  
                  
The  term “employee-owner” has the meaning given such term by section  269A(b)(2) (as modified by section 441(i)(2)). 


I.R.C. § 280H(f)(3) Nondeferral And Deferral Periods


I.R.C. § 280H(f)(3)(A) Deferral Period — 
                     
                     
The  term “deferral period” has the meaning given to such term by section  444(b)(4). 


I.R.C. § 280H(f)(3)(B) Nondeferral Period — 
                     
                     
The  term “nondeferral period” means the portion of the taxable year of the  personal
                        service corporation which occurs after the portion of such year  constituting the
                        deferral period. 



I.R.C. § 280H(f)(4) Adjusted Taxable Income — 
                  
                  
The  term “adjusted taxable income” means taxable income determined without  regard
                     to— 

I.R.C. § 280H(f)(4)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any  amount paid to an employee-owner which is includible in the gross income  of
                        such employee-owner, and 


I.R.C. § 280H(f)(4)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any  net operating loss carryover to the extent such carryover is attributable  to
                        amounts described in subparagraph (A). 



I.R.C. § 280H(f)(5) Personal Service Corporation — 
                  
                  
The  term “personal service corporation” has the meaning given to such term by  section
                     441(i)(2).
