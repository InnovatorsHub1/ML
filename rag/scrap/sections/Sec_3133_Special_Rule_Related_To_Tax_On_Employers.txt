Sec. 3133. Special Rule Related To Tax On Employers

I.R.C. § 3133(a) In General — 
               
               
The credit allowed by  section 3131 and the credit allowed by  section 3132 shall each be increased by the amount of the taxes imposed by subsections (a) and
                  (b) of  section 3111 and  section 3221(a) on qualified sick leave wages, or qualified family leave wages, for which credit
                  is allowed under such  section 3131 or 3132 (respectively).
I.R.C. § 3133(b) Denial Of Double Benefit — 
               
               
For denial of double benefit with respect to the credit increase under subsection
                  (a), see  sections 3131(f)(3) and 3132(f)(3).
