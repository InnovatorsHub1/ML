Sec. 3. Tax Tables For Individuals

I.R.C. § 3(a) Imposition Of Tax Table Tax


I.R.C. § 3(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In  lieu of the tax imposed by section 1, there is hereby imposed for each
                     taxable year on the taxable income of every individual— 

I.R.C. § 3(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
who  does not itemize his deductions for the taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 3(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
whose  taxable income for such taxable year does not exceed the ceiling amount, 


a  tax determined under tables, applicable to such taxable year, which shall  be prescribed
                        by the Secretary and which shall be in such form as he  determines appropriate. In
                        the table so prescribed, the amounts of the tax  shall be computed on the basis of
                        the rates prescribed by section 1.



I.R.C. § 3(a)(2) Ceiling Amount Defined — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of paragraph (1), the term “ceiling amount” means, with respect  to any taxpayer, the amount (not
                     less than $20,000) determined by the  Secretary for the tax rate category in which
                     such taxpayer falls. 


I.R.C. § 3(a)(3) Authority To Prescribe Tables For Taxpayers Who Itemize Deductions — 
                  
                  
The  Secretary may provide that this section shall apply also for any taxable  year
                     to individuals who itemize their deductions. Any tables prescribed  under the preceding
                     sentence shall be on the basis of taxable income.
I.R.C. § 3(b) Section Inapplicable To Certain Individuals — 
               
               
This  section shall not apply to— 

I.R.C. § 3(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
an  individual making a return under section 443(a)(1) for a period of less  than 12 months on account of a change in annual accounting
                     period, and 


I.R.C. § 3(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
an  estate or trust.
I.R.C. § 3(c) Tax Treated As Imposed By Section 1 — 
               
               
For  purposes of this title, the tax imposed by this section shall be treated  as
                  tax imposed by section 1.
I.R.C. § 3(d) Taxable Income — 
               
               
Whenever  it is necessary to determine the taxable income of an individual to whom
                  this section applies, the taxable income shall be determined under section  63.
I.R.C. § 3(e) Cross Reference — 
               
               
For  computation of tax by Secretary, see section 6014.
