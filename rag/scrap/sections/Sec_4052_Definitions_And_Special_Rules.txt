Sec. 4052. Definitions And Special Rules

I.R.C. § 4052(a) First Retail Sale — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4052(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  term “first retail sale” means the first sale, for a purpose other than  for
                     resale or leasing in a long-term lease, after production, manufacture,  or importation. 


I.R.C. § 4052(a)(2) Leases Considered As Sales — 
                  
                  
Rules  similar to the rules of section 4217 shall apply. 


I.R.C. § 4052(a)(3) Use Treated As Sale


I.R.C. § 4052(a)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
If  any person uses an article taxable under section 4051 before the first  retail sale of such article, then such person shall be liable for
                        tax  under section 4051 in the same manner as if such article were sold at  retail by him. 


I.R.C. § 4052(a)(3)(B) Exemption For Use In Further Manufacture — 
                     
                     
Subparagraph  (A) shall not apply to use of an article as material in the manufacture
                        or  production of, or as a component part of, another article to be  manufactured
                        or produced by him. 


I.R.C. § 4052(a)(3)(C) Computation Of Tax — 
                     
                     
In  the case of any person made liable for tax by subparagraph
                        (A), the tax  shall be computed on the price at which similar articles are sold at
                        retail in the ordinary course of trade, as determined by the Secretary.
I.R.C. § 4052(b) Determination Of Price


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In  determining price for purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
there shall be included any charge incident to placing  the article in condition ready
                        for use, 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
there shall be excluded— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the amount of the tax imposed by this subchapter, 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
if stated as a separate charge, the amount of any retail  sales tax imposed by any
                           State or political subdivision thereof or the  District of Columbia, whether the liability
                           for such tax is imposed on the  vendor or vendee, and 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
the  value of any component of such article if— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(B)(iii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
such component is furnished by the first user of such  article, and 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(B)(iii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
such component has been used before such furnishing, and 




I.R.C. § 4052(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
the price shall be determined without regard to any  trade-in. 



I.R.C. § 4052(b)(2) Sales Not At Arm's Length — 
                  
                  
In  the case of any article sold (otherwise than through an arm's-length  transaction)
                     at less than the fair market price, the tax under this  subchapter shall be computed
                     on the price for which similar articles are  sold at retail in the ordinary course
                     of trade, as determined by the  Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3) Long-Term Lease


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
In  the case of any long-term lease of an article which is treated as the  first retail
                        sale of such article, the tax under this subchapter shall be  computed on a price
                        equal to— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(A)(i)(I)  — 
                           
                           
the price (determined under this subchapter but without  regard to paragraph (4))
                              at which such article was sold to the lessor, and 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(A)(i)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the cost of any parts and accessories installed by the  lessor on such article before
                              the first use by the lessee or leased in  connection with such long-term lease, plus 



I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
an amount equal to the presumed markup percentage of the  sum described in clause
                           (i). 



I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(B) Presumed Markup Percentage — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subparagraph (A), the term “presumed markup percentage” means  the
                        average markup percentage of retailers of articles of the type  involved, as determined
                        by the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(3)(C) Exceptions Under Regulations — 
                     
                     
To  the extent provided in regulations prescribed by the Secretary,  subparagraph
                        (A) shall not apply to specified types of leases where its  application is not necessary
                        to carry out the purposes of this subsection. 



I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4) Special Rule Where Tax Paid By Manufacturer, Producer, Or Importer


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
In  any case where the manufacturer, producer, or importer of any article (or  a related
                        person) is liable for tax imposed by this subchapter with  respect to such article,
                        the tax under this subchapter shall be computed  on a price equal to the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the price which would (but for this paragraph) be  determined under this subchapter,
                           plus 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the product of the price referred to in clause (i) and  the presumed markup percentage
                           determined under paragraph (3)(B). 



I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4)(B) Related Person — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph— 

I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4)(B)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
Except  as provided in clause (ii), the term “related person” means any person who
                           is a member of the same controlled group
                           (within the meaning of section  5061(e)(3))
                           as the manufacturer, producer, or importer. 


I.R.C. § 4052(b)(4)(B)(ii) Exception For Retail Establishment — 
                        
                        
To  the extent provided in regulations prescribed by the Secretary, a person  shall
                           not be treated as a related person with respect to the sale of any  article if such
                           article is sold through a permanent retail establishment  in the normal course of
                           the trade or business of being a retailer.
I.R.C. § 4052(c) Certain Combinations Not Treated As Manufacture


I.R.C. § 4052(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subchapter (other than subsection
                     (a)(3)(B)), a person  shall not be treated as engaged in the manufacture of any article
                     by  reason of merely combining such article with any item listed in paragraph  (2). 


I.R.C. § 4052(c)(2) Items — 
                  
                  
The  items listed in this paragraph are any coupling device (including any  fifth
                     wheel), wrecker crane, loading and unloading equipment (including  any crane, hoist,
                     winch, or power liftgate), aerial ladder or tower, snow  and ice control equipment,
                     earthmoving, excavation and construction  equipment, spreader, sleeper cab, cab shield,
                     or wood or metal floor.
I.R.C. § 4052(d) Certain Other Rules Made Applicable — 
               
               
Under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, rules similar to the rules of  subsections
                  (c) and (d) of section 4216 (relating to partial payments)
                  shall apply for purposes of this subchapter.
I.R.C. § 4052(e) Long-Term Lease — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term “long-term lease"
                  means any lease with  a term of 1 year or more. In determining a lease term for purposes
                  of the  preceding sentence, the rules of section 168(i)(3)(A) shall  apply.
I.R.C. § 4052(f)  Certain Repairs And Modifications Not Treated As Manufacture


I.R.C. § 4052(f)(1)  In General — 
                  
                  
An  article described in section 4051(a)(1) shall not be
                     treated as  manufactured or produced solely by reason of repairs or  modifications
                     to  the article (including any modification which changes the  transportation  function
                     of the article or restores a wrecked article to a  functional  condition) if the cost
                     of such repairs and modifications does  not exceed  75 percent of the retail price
                     of a comparable new article. 


I.R.C. § 4052(f)(2)  Exception — 
                  
                  
Paragraph  (1) shall not apply if the article (as repaired
                     or modified) would, if  new, be taxable under section 4051 and the article  when new was not  taxable under such section or the corresponding
                     provision
                     of prior  law.
I.R.C. § 4052(g)  Regulations — 
               
               
The  Secretary shall prescribe regulations which permit,
                  in lieu of any other  certification, persons who are purchasing articles
                  taxable under this  subchapter for resale or leasing in a long-term lease  to execute
                  a  statement (made under penalties of perjury) on the sale  invoice that such  sale
                  is for resale. The Secretary shall not impose any  registration  requirement as a
                  condition of using such procedure.
