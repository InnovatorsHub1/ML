Sec. 4053. Exemptions

I.R.C. § 4053(1) Camper Coaches Bodies For Self-Propelled Mobile Homes — 
               
               
Any  article designed— 

I.R.C. § 4053(1)(A)  — 
                  
                  
to be mounted or placed on automobile trucks, automobile  truck chassis, or automobile
                     chassis, and 


I.R.C. § 4053(1)(B)  — 
                  
                  
to be used primarily as living quarters or camping  accommodations.
I.R.C. § 4053(2) Feed, Seed, And Fertilizer Equipment — 
               
               
Any  body primarily designed— 

I.R.C. § 4053(2)(A)  — 
                  
                  
to process or prepare seed, feed, or fertilizer for use  on farms, 


I.R.C. § 4053(2)(B)  — 
                  
                  
to haul feed, seed, or fertilizer to and on farms, 


I.R.C. § 4053(2)(C)  — 
                  
                  
to spread feed, seed, or fertilizer on farms, 


I.R.C. § 4053(2)(D)  — 
                  
                  
to load or unload feed, seed, or fertilizer on farms, or 


I.R.C. § 4053(2)(E)  — 
                  
                  
for any combination of the foregoing.
I.R.C. § 4053(3) House Trailers — 
               
               
Any  house trailer.
I.R.C. § 4053(4) Ambulances, Hearses, Etc. — 
               
               
Any  ambulance, hearse, or combination ambulance-hearse.
I.R.C. § 4053(5) Concrete Mixers — 
               
               
Any  article designed— 

I.R.C. § 4053(5)(A)  — 
                  
                  
to be placed or mounted on an automobile truck chassis  or truck trailer or semitrailer
                     chassis, and 


I.R.C. § 4053(5)(B)  — 
                  
                  
to be used to process or prepare concrete.
I.R.C. § 4053(6) Trash Containers, Etc. — 
               
               
Any  box, container, receptacle, bin or other similar article— 

I.R.C. § 4053(6)(A)  — 
                  
                  
which is designed to be used as a trash container and is  not designed for the transportation
                     of freight other than trash, and 


I.R.C. § 4053(6)(B)  — 
                  
                  
which is not designed to be permanently mounted on or  permanently affixed to an automobile
                     truck chassis or body.
I.R.C. § 4053(7) Rail Trailers And Rail Vans — 
               
               
Any  chassis or body of a trailer or semitrailer which is designed for use both  as
                  a highway vehicle and a railroad car. For purposes of the preceding  sentence, piggy-back
                  trailer or semitrailer shall not be treated as  designed for use as a railroad car.
I.R.C. § 4053(8) Mobile Machinery — 
               
               
Any  vehicle which consists of a chassis— 

I.R.C. § 4053(8)(A)  — 
                  
                  
to  which there has been permanently mounted (by welding, bolting, riveting,  or other
                     means) machinery or equipment to perform a construction,  manufacturing, processing,
                     farming, mining, drilling, timbering, or  similar operation if the operation of the
                     machinery or equipment is  unrelated to transportation on or off the public highways, 


I.R.C. § 4053(8)(B)  — 
                  
                  
which has been specially designed to serve only as a mobile carriage and  mount (and
                     a power source, where applicable) for the particular machinery  or equipment involved,
                     whether or not such machinery or equipment is in  operation, and 


I.R.C. § 4053(8)(C)  — 
                  
                  
which,  by reason of such special design, could not, without substantial  structural
                     modification, be used as a component of a vehicle designed to  perform a function
                     of transporting any load other than that particular  machinery or equipment or similar
                     machinery or equipment requiring such a  specially designed chassis.
I.R.C. § 4053(9) Idling Reduction Device — 
               
               
Any  device or system of devices which— 

I.R.C. § 4053(9)(A)  — 
                  
                  
is designed to provide to a vehicle those services (such as heat, air conditioning,
                     or electricity) that would otherwise require the operation of the main drive engine
                     while the vehicle is temporarily parked or remains stationary using one or more devices
                     affixed to a tractor, and 


I.R.C. § 4053(9)(B)  — 
                  
                  
is determined by the Administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency, in consultation
                     with the Secretary of Energy and the Secretary of Transportation, to reduce idling
                     of such vehicle at a motor vehicle rest stop or other location where such vehicles
                     are temporarily parked or remain stationary.
I.R.C. § 4053(10) Advanced Insulation — 
               
               
Any insulation that has an R value of not less than R35 per inch.
