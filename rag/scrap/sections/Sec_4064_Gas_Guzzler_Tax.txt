I.R.C. § 4064(a) Imposition Of Tax — 
               
               
There  is hereby imposed on the sale by the manufacturer of each automobile a tax
                  determined in accordance with the following table:

      which the automobile falls is:              The tax is:

      At least 22.5  ......................................$0
      At least 21.5 but less than 22.5..................1,000
      At least 20.5 but less than 21.5...................1,300
      At least 19.5 but less than 20.5...................1,700
      At least 18.5 but less than 19.5...................2,100

I.R.C. § 4064(b) Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1) Automobile


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  term “automobile” means any 4-wheeled vehicle propelled by fuel— 

I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
which is manufactured primarily for use on public  streets, roads, and highways (except
                           any vehicle operated exclusively on a  rail or rails), and 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
which is rated at 6,000 pounds unloaded gross vehicle  weight or less. 



I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(B) Exception For Certain Vehicles — 
                     
                     
The  term “automobile” does not include any vehicle which is treated as a  nonpassenger
                        automobile under the rules which were prescribed by the  Secretary of Transportation
                        for purposes of section 32901 of title 49,  United States Code, and which were in
                        effect on the date of the enactment  of this section. 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(C) Exception For Emergency Vehicles — 
                     
                     
The  term “automobile” does not include any vehicle sold for use and used— 

I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(C)(i)  — 
                        
                        
as an ambulance or combination ambulance-hearse, 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(C)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
by the United States or by a State or local government  for police or other law enforcement
                           purposes, or 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(1)(C)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
for other emergency uses prescribed by the Secretary by  regulations. 




I.R.C. § 4064(b)(2) Fuel Economy — 
                  
                  
The  term “fuel economy” means the average number of miles traveled by an  automobile
                     per gallon of gasoline (or equivalent amount of other fuel)  consumed, as determined
                     by the EPA Administrator in accordance with  procedures established under subsection
                     (c). 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(3) Model Type — 
                  
                  
The  term “model type” means a particular class of automobile as determined by  regulation
                     by the EPA Administrator. 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(4) Model Year — 
                  
                  
The  term “model year”, with reference to any specific calendar year, means a  manufacturer's
                     annual production period (as determined by the EPA  Administrator) which includes
                     January 1 of such calendar year. If a  manufacturer has no annual production period,
                     the term “model year” means  the calendar year. 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(5) Manufacturer


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(5)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  term “manufacturer” includes a producer or importer. 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(5)(B) Lengthening Treated As Manufacture — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this section, subchapter G of this chapter, and section  6416(b)(3), the lengthening of an automobile by any person shall be  treated as the manufacture
                        of an automobile by such person. 



I.R.C. § 4064(b)(6) EPA Administrator — 
                  
                  
The  term “EPA Administrator” means the Administrator of the Environmental  Protection
                     Agency. 


I.R.C. § 4064(b)(7) Fuel — 
                  
                  
The  term “fuel” means gasoline and diesel fuel. The Secretary (after  consultation
                     with the Secretary of Transportation)
                     may, by regulation,  include any product of petroleum or natural gas within the meaning
                     of such  term if he determines that such inclusion is consistent with the need of
                     the Nation to conserve energy.
I.R.C. § 4064(c) Determination Of Fuel Economy — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 4064(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Fuel  economy for any model type shall be measured in accordance with testing  and
                     calculation procedures established by the EPA Administrator by  regulation. Procedures
                     so established shall be the procedures utilized by  the EPA Administrator for model
                     year 1975 (weighted 55 percent urban  cycle, and 45 percent highway cycle), or procedures
                     which yield comparable  results. Procedures under this subsection, to the extent practicable,
                     shall require that fuel economy tests be conducted in conjunction with  emissions
                     tests conducted under section 206 of the Clean Air Act. The EPA  Administrator shall
                     report any measurements of fuel economy to the  Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 4064(c)(2) Special Rule For Fuels Other Than Gasoline — 
                  
                  
The  EPA Administrator shall by regulation determine that quantity of any other  fuel
                     which is the equivalent of one gallon of gasoline. 


I.R.C. § 4064(c)(3) Time By Which Regulations Must Be Issued — 
                  
                  
Testing  and calculation procedures applicable to a model year, and any amendment
                     to such procedures (other than a technical or clerical amendment), shall  be promulgated
                     not less than 12 months before the model year to which such  procedures apply.
