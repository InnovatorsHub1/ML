Sec. 4132. Definitions And Special Rules

I.R.C. § 4132(a) Definitions Relating To Taxable Vaccines — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1) Taxable Vaccine — 
                  
                  
The  term “taxable vaccine” means any of the following vaccines which are  manufactured
                     or produced in the United States or entered into the United  States for consumption,
                     use, or warehousing: 

I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine containing diphtheria toxoid. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine containing tetanus toxoid. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine containing pertussis bacteria, extracted or partial cell bacteria,  or
                        specific pertussis antigens. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against measles. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(E)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against mumps. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(F)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against rubella. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(G)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine containing polio virus. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(H)  — 
                     
                     
Any  HIB vaccine. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(I)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against hepatitis A. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(J)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against hepatitis B. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(K)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against chicken pox. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(L)  — 
                     
                     
Any vaccine against rotavirus gastroenteritis. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(M)  — 
                     
                     
Any  conjugate vaccine against streptococcus pneumoniae. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(N)  — 
                     
                     
Any  trivalent vaccine against influenza or any other vaccine against seasonal influenza. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(O)  — 
                     
                     
Any  meningococcal vaccine. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(1)(P)  — 
                     
                     
Any  vaccine against the human papillomavirus. 



I.R.C. § 4132(a)(2) Vaccine — 
                  
                  
The  term “vaccine” means any substance designed to be administered to a human  being
                     for the prevention of 1 or more diseases. 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(3) United States — 
                  
                  
The  term “United States” has the meaning given such term by section  4612(a)(4). 


I.R.C. § 4132(a)(4) Importer — 
                  
                  
The  term “importer” means the person entering the vaccine for consumption,  use,
                     or warehousing.
I.R.C. § 4132(b) Credit Or Refund Where Vaccine Returned To Manufacturer, Etc., Or Destroyed


I.R.C. § 4132(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, whenever any vaccine on which tax
                     was imposed by section 4131 is— 

I.R.C. § 4132(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
returned (other than for resale)
                        to the person who paid  such tax, or 


I.R.C. § 4132(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
destroyed, the Secretary shall abate such tax or allow a  credit, or pay a refund
                        (without interest), to such person equal to the  tax paid under section 4131 with respect to such vaccine. 



I.R.C. § 4132(b)(2) Claim Must Be Filed Within 6 Months — 
                  
                  
Paragraph  (1) shall apply to any returned or destroyed vaccine only with respect
                     to  claims filed within 6 months after the date the vaccine is returned or  destroyed. 


I.R.C. § 4132(b)(3) Condition Of Allowance Of Credit Or Refund — 
                  
                  
No  credit or refund shall be allowed or made under paragraph (1) with respect  to any vaccine unless the person who paid the tax establishes that
                     he— 

I.R.C. § 4132(b)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
has repaid or agreed to repay the amount of the tax to  the ultimate purchaser of
                        the vaccine, or 


I.R.C. § 4132(b)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
has obtained the written consent of such purchaser to  the allowance of the credit
                        or the making of the refund. 



I.R.C. § 4132(b)(4) Tax Imposed Only Once — 
                  
                  
No  tax shall be imposed by section 4131 on the sale of any vaccine if tax was  imposed by section 4131 on any prior sale of such vaccine and such tax is  not abated, credited, or refunded.
I.R.C. § 4132(c) Other Special Rules


I.R.C. § 4132(c)(1) Certain Uses Treated As Sales — 
                  
                  
Any  manufacturer, producer, or importer of a vaccine which uses such vaccine  before
                     it is sold shall be liable for the tax imposed by section 4131 in
                     the same manner as if such vaccine were sold by such manufacturer,
                     producer, or importer. 


I.R.C. § 4132(c)(2) Treatment Of Vaccines Shipped To United States Possessions — 
                  
                  
Section  4221(a)(2) shall not apply to any vaccine shipped to a possession of the  United States. 


I.R.C. § 4132(c)(3) Fractional Part Of A Dose — 
                  
                  
In  the case of a fraction of a dose, the tax imposed by section 4131 shall be  the same fraction of the amount of such tax imposed by a whole dose. 


I.R.C. § 4132(c)(4) Disposition Of Revenues From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands — 
                  
                  
The  provisions of subsections (a)(3) and (b)(3) of section 7652 shall not  apply to any tax imposed by section 4131.
