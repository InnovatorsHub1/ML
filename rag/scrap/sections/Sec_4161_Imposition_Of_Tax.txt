Sec. 4161. Imposition Of Tax

I.R.C. § 4161(a) Sport Fishing Equipment


I.R.C. § 4161(a)(1) Imposition Of Tax


I.R.C. § 4161(a)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
There  is hereby imposed on the sale of any article of sport fishing equipment by
                        the manufacturer, producer, or importer a tax equal to 10 percent of the  price for
                        which so sold. 


I.R.C. § 4161(a)(1)(B) Limitation On Tax Imposed On Fishing Rods And Poles — 
                     
                     
The  tax imposed by subparagraph (A) on any fishing rod or pole shall not  exceed $10. 



I.R.C. § 4161(a)(2) 3 Percent Rate Of Tax For Electric Outboard Motors — 
                  
                  
In  the case of an electric outboard motor, paragraph (1) shall be  applied by substituting “3 percent” for “10 percent”. 


I.R.C. § 4161(a)(3) 3 Percent Rate Of Tax For Tackle Boxes — 
                  
                  
In the case of fishing tackle boxes, paragraph (1) shall be applied  by substituting “3 percent” for “10 percent”. 


I.R.C. § 4161(a)(4) Parts Or Accessories Sold In Connection With Taxable Sale — 
                  
                  
In  the case of any sale by the manufacturer, producer, or importer of any  article
                     of sport fishing equipment, such article shall be treated as  including any parts
                     or accessories of such article sold on or in  connection therewith or with the sale
                     thereof.
I.R.C. § 4161(b)  Bows And Arrows, Etc.


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(1) Bows


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
There is hereby imposed on the sale by the manufacturer, producer,  or importer of
                        any bow which has a peak draw weight of 30 pounds or  more, a tax equal to 11 percent
                        of the price for which so  sold. 


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(1)(B) Archery Equipment — 
                     
                     
There is hereby imposed on the sale by the manufacturer, producer,  or importer— 

I.R.C. § 4161(b)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
of  any part or accessory suitable for inclusion in or attachment to  a bow described
                           in subparagraph (A), and 


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
of  any  quiver, broadhead, or point
                           suitable for use with an arrow described  in paragraph (2), 


a tax equal to 11 percent of the
                           price for which so sold.




I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)  Arrows


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
There  is hereby imposed on the first sale by the manufacturer,
                        producer, or  importer of any shaft (whether sold separately or incorporated as part
                        of  a finished or unfinished product) of a type used in  the manufacture of  any arrow
                        which after its assembly— 

I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
measures 18 inches overall or more in length,  or 


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
measures less than 18 inches overall in length but is  suitable for  use with a bow
                           described in paragraph (1)(A), 


a tax equal to 39 cents per  shaft.



I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(B) Exemption For Certain Wooden Arrow Shafts — 
                     
                     
Subparagraph (A) shall not apply to any shaft consisting of all natural wood with no laminations or
                        artificial means of enhancing the spine of such shaft (whether sold separately or
                        incorporated as part of a finished or unfinished product) of a type used in the manufacture
                        of any arrow which after its assembly— 

I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
measures 5/16 of an inch or less in diameter, and 


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
is not suitable for use with a bow described in paragraph (1)(A). 



I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(C) Adjustment For Inflation


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(C)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
In  the case of any calendar year beginning after 2005, the 39-cent amount  specified
                           in subparagraph (A) shall be increased by an amount equal to the  product of— 

I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(C)(i)(I)  — 
                           
                           
such  amount, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(C)(i)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the cost-of-living adjustment determined under section 1(f)(3) for such  calendar year, determined by substituting “2004” for “2016”
                              in subparagraph (A)(ii) thereof. 



I.R.C. § 4161(b)(2)(C)(ii) Rounding — 
                        
                        
If  any increase determined under clause (i) is not a multiple of 1 cent, such  increase shall be rounded to the nearest multiple
                           of 1  cent. 




I.R.C. § 4161(b)(3)  Coordination With Subsection (a) — 
                  
                  
No  tax shall be imposed under this  subsection with respect to any article  taxable
                     under subsection (a).
