Sec. 4162. Definitions; Treatment Of Certain Resales

I.R.C. § 4162(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
fishing rods and poles (and component parts therefor), 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
fishing reels, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
fly fishing lines, and other fishing lines not over 130  pounds test, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(4)  — 
                  
                  
fishing spears, spear guns, and spear tips, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)  — 
                  
                  
items of terminal tackle, including— 

I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(A)  — 
                     
                     
leaders, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(B)  — 
                     
                     
artificial lures, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(C)  — 
                     
                     
artificial baits, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(D)  — 
                     
                     
artificial flies, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(E)  — 
                     
                     
fishing hooks, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(F)  — 
                     
                     
bobbers, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(G)  — 
                     
                     
sinkers, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(H)  — 
                     
                     
snaps, 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(I)  — 
                     
                     
drayles, and 


I.R.C. § 4162(a)(5)(J)  — 
                     
                     
swivels, 


but not including natural bait or any item of  terminal tackle designed for use and
                        ordinarily used on fishing lines not  described in paragraph (3), and

I.R.C. § 4162(a)(6)(J)  —

I.R.C. § 4162(a)(7)  —

I.R.C. § 4162(b) Treatment Of Certain Resales


I.R.C. § 4162(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
If— 

I.R.C. § 4162(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the manufacturer, producer, or importer sells any  article taxable under section 4161(a) to any person, 


I.R.C. § 4162(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the constructive sale price rules of section 4216(b) do
                        not apply to such sale, and 


I.R.C. § 4162(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
such person (or any other person)
                        sells such article to  a related person with respect to the manufacturer, producer,
                        or importer, 


then such related person shall be liable for tax under section 4161 in the  same manner as if such related person were the manufacturer of the  article.



I.R.C. § 4162(b)(2) Credit For Tax Previously Paid — 
                  
                  
If— 

I.R.C. § 4162(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
tax is imposed on the sale of any article by reason of  paragraph (1), and 


I.R.C. § 4162(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the related person establishes the amount of the tax  which was paid on the sale described
                        in paragraph (1)(A), 


the amount of  the tax so paid shall be allowed as a credit against the tax imposed
                        by  reason of paragraph (1).



I.R.C. § 4162(b)(3) Related Person — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection, the term “related person” has the meaning  given
                     such term by section 465(b)(3)(C). 


I.R.C. § 4162(b)(4) Regulations — 
                  
                  
Except  to the extent provided in regulations, rules similar to the rules of this
                     subsection shall also apply in cases
                     (not described in paragraph (1))
                     in  which intermediaries or other devices are used for purposes of reducing  the amount
                     of the tax imposed by section 4161(a).
