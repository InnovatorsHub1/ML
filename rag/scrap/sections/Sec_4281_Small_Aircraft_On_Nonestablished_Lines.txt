Sec. 4281. Small Aircraft On Nonestablished Lines

I.R.C. § 4281(a) In General — 
               
               
The taxes imposed by  sections 4261 and  4271 shall not apply to transportation by an aircraft having a  maximum certificated takeoff
                  weight of 6,000 pounds or less, except  when such aircraft is operated on an established
                  line or when  such aircraft is a jet aircraft.
I.R.C. § 4281(b) Maximum Certificated Takeoff Weight — 
               
               
For purposes  of this section, the term “maximum certificated takeoff weight”  means
                  the maximum such weight contained in the type certificate  or airworthiness certificate.
I.R.C. § 4281(c) Sightseeing — 
               
               
For purposes of this section, an aircraft  shall not be considered as operated on
                  an established line at any  time during which such aircraft is being operated on a
                  flight the  sole purpose of which is sightseeing.
I.R.C. § 4281(d) Jet Aircraft — 
               
               
—For purposes of this section, the term  “jet aircraft” shall not include any aircraft
                  which is a rotorcraft
                  or propeller aircraft.
