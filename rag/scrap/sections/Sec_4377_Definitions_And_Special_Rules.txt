Sec. 4377. Definitions And Special Rules

I.R.C. § 4377(a) Definitions — 
               
               
For purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4377(a)(1) Accident And Health Coverage — 
                  
                  
The term “accident and health coverage” means any coverage which, if provided by an
                     insurance policy, would cause such policy to be a specified health insurance policy
                     (as defined in section 4375(c)). 


I.R.C. § 4377(a)(2) Insurance Policy — 
                  
                  
The term “insurance policy” means any policy or other instrument whereby a contract
                     of insurance is issued, renewed, or extended. 


I.R.C. § 4377(a)(3) United States — 
                  
                  
The term “United States” includes any possession of the United States.
I.R.C. § 4377(b) Treatment Of Governmental Entities


I.R.C. § 4377(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4377(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the term “person” includes any governmental entity, and 


I.R.C. § 4377(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
notwithstanding any other law or rule of law, governmental entities shall not be exempt
                        from the fees imposed by this subchapter except as provided in paragraph (2). 



I.R.C. § 4377(b)(2) Treatment Of Exempt Governmental Programs — 
                  
                  
In the case of an exempt governmental program, no fee shall be imposed under section
                     4375 or section 4376 on any covered life under such program. 


I.R.C. § 4377(b)(3) Exempt Governmental Program Defined — 
                  
                  
For purposes of this subchapter, the term “exempt governmental program” means— 

I.R.C. § 4377(b)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any insurance program established under title XVIII of the Social Security Act, 


I.R.C. § 4377(b)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the medical assistance program established by title XIX or XXI of the Social Security
                        Act, 


I.R.C. § 4377(b)(3)(C)  — 
                     
                     
any program established by Federal law for providing medical care (other than through
                        insurance policies)
                        to individuals (or the spouses and dependents thereof) by reason of such individuals
                        being members of the Armed Forces of the United States or veterans, and 


I.R.C. § 4377(b)(3)(D)  — 
                     
                     
any program established by Federal law for providing medical care (other than through
                        insurance policies)
                        to members of Indian tribes (as defined in section 4(d) of the Indian Health Care
                        Improvement Act).
I.R.C. § 4377(c) Treatment As Tax — 
               
               
For purposes of subtitle F, the fees imposed by this subchapter shall be treated as
                  if they were taxes.
I.R.C. § 4377(d) No Cover Over To Possessions — 
               
               
Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no amount collected under this subchapter
                  shall be covered over to any possession of the United States.
