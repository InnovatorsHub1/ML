Sec. 4411. Imposition Of Tax

I.R.C. § 4411(a) In General — 
               
               
There  shall be imposed a special tax of $500 per year to be paid by each person 
                  who is liable for the tax imposed under section 4401 or who is engaged in  receiving wagers for or on behalf of any person so liable.
I.R.C. § 4411(b) Authorized Persons — 
               
               
Subsection  (a) shall be applied by substituting “$50"
                  for “$500” in the case of— 

I.R.C. § 4411(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any person whose liability for tax under section 4401 is
                     determined only under paragraph (1) of section 4401(a), and 


I.R.C. § 4411(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any person who is engaged in receiving wagers only for  or on behalf of persons described
                     in paragraph (1).
