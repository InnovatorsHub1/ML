Sec. 4461. Imposition Of Tax

I.R.C. § 4461(a) General Rule — 
               
               
There  is hereby imposed a tax on any port use.
I.R.C. § 4461(b) Amount Of Tax — 
               
               
The  amount of the tax imposed by subsection (a) on any port use shall be an  amount
                  equal to 0.125 percent of the value of the commercial cargo  involved.
I.R.C. § 4461(c) Liability And Time Of Imposition Of Tax


I.R.C. § 4461(c)(1) Liability — 
                  
                  
The  tax imposed by subsection (a) shall be paid by— 

I.R.C. § 4461(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of cargo entering the United States, the  importer,  or 


I.R.C. § 4461(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
in any other case, the shipper. 



I.R.C. § 4461(c)(2) Time Of Imposition — 
                  
                  
Except  as provided by regulations, the tax imposed by subsection (a) shall be   imposed
                     at the time of unloading.
