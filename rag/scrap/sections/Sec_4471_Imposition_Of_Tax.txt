Sec. 4471. Imposition Of Tax

I.R.C. § 4471(a) In General — 
               
               
There  is hereby imposed a tax of $3 per passenger on a covered voyage.
I.R.C. § 4471(b) By Whom Paid — 
               
               
The  tax imposed by this section shall be paid by the person providing the  covered
                  voyage.
I.R.C. § 4471(c) Time Of Imposition — 
               
               
The  tax imposed by this section shall be imposed only once for each passenger  on
                  a covered voyage, either at the time of first embarkation or  disembarkation in the
                  United States.
