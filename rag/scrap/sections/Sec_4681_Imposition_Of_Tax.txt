Sec. 4681. Imposition Of Tax

I.R.C. § 4681(a) General Rule — 
               
               
There  is hereby imposed a tax on-- 

I.R.C. § 4681(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any ozone-depleting chemical sold or used by the  manufacturer, producer, or importer
                     thereof, and 


I.R.C. § 4681(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any imported taxable product sold or used by the  importer thereof.
I.R.C. § 4681(b) Amount Of Tax


I.R.C. § 4681(b)(1) Ozone-Depleting Chemicals


I.R.C. § 4681(b)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  amount of the tax imposed by subsection (a) on each pound of  ozone-depleting
                        chemical shall be an amount equal to— 

I.R.C. § 4681(b)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the base tax amount, multiplied by 


I.R.C. § 4681(b)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the ozone-depletion factor for such
                           chemical. 



I.R.C. § 4681(b)(1)(B) Base Tax Amount — 
                     
                     
The  base tax amount for purposes of subparagraph (A)
                        with respect to any sale  or use during any calendar year after 1995 shall be $5.35
                        increased by 45  cents for each year after 1995. 



I.R.C. § 4681(b)(2) Imported Taxable Product


I.R.C. § 4681(b)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  amount of the tax imposed by subsection (a) on any imported taxable  product
                        shall be the amount of tax which would have been imposed by  subsection (a) on the
                        ozone-depleting chemicals used as materials in the  manufacture or production of such
                        product if such ozone- depleting  chemicals had been sold in the United States on
                        the date of the sale of  such imported taxable product. 


I.R.C. § 4681(b)(2)(B) Certain Rules To Apply — 
                     
                     
Rules  similar to the rules of paragraphs (2) and (3)
                        of section 4671(b) shall
                        apply.
