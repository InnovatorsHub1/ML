Sec. 4682. Definitions And Special Rules

I.R.C. § 4682(a) Ozone-Depleting Chemical — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4682(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  term “ozone-depleting chemical” means any substance— 

I.R.C. § 4682(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
which, at the time of the sale or use by the  manufacturer, producer, or importer,
                        is listed as an ozone-depleting chemical in the table contained in paragraph (2),
                        and 


I.R.C. § 4682(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
which is manufactured or produced in the United States or entered into the United
                        States for  consumption, use, or warehousing.

I.R.C. § 4682(c) Imported Taxable Product — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4682(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  term “imported taxable product” means any product (other than an  ozone-depleting
                     chemical) entered into the United States for consumption,  use, or warehousing if
                     any ozone-depleting chemical was used as material  in the manufacture or production
                     of such product. 


I.R.C. § 4682(c)(2) De Minimis Exception — 
                  
                  
The  term “imported taxable product” shall not include any product specified in  regulations
                     prescribed by the Secretary as using a de minimis amount of  ozone-depleting chemicals
                     as materials in the manufacture or production  thereof. The preceding sentence shall
                     not apply to any product in which  any ozone-depleting chemical (other than methyl
                     chloroform) is used for  purposes of refrigeration or air conditioning, creating an
                     aerosol or  foam, or manufacturing electronic components.
I.R.C. § 4682(d) Exceptions


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(1) Recycling — 
                  
                  
No  tax shall be imposed by section 4681 on any ozone-depleting chemical which  is diverted or recovered in the United States
                     as part of a recycling  process  (and not as part of the original manufacturing or
                     production  process), or on any recycled Halon-1301 or  recycled Halon-2402 imported
                     from  any country which is a signatory to the Montreal Protocol on  Substances that
                     Deplete the Ozone Layer. 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2) Use In Further Manufacture


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
No  tax shall be imposed by section 4681— 

I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
on the use of any ozone-depleting chemical in the  manufacture or production of any
                           other chemical if the ozone-depleting  chemical is entirely consumed in such use, 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
on the sale by the manufacturer, producer, or importer  of any ozone-depleting chemical— 

I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(A)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
for a use by the purchaser which meets the requirements  of clause (i), or 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(A)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
for resale by the purchaser to a second purchaser for a  use by the second purchaser
                              which meets the requirements of clause (i). 



Clause (ii) shall apply only if the manufacturer,  producer, and importer, and the
                           1st and 2d purchasers (if any), meet such  registration requirements as may be prescribed
                           by the  Secretary.



I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(B) Credit Or Refund — 
                     
                     
Under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, if— 

I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a tax under this subchapter was paid with respect to any  ozone-depleting chemical,
                           and 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
such chemical was used (and entirely consumed) by any  person in the manufacture or
                           production of any other chemical, 


then an  amount equal to the tax so paid shall be allowed as a credit or refund  (without
                           interest)
                           to such person in the same manner as if it were an  overpayment of tax imposed by
                           section 4681.




I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3) Exports


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), rules similar to the rules of section  4662(e) (other than section 4662(e)(2)(A)(ii)(II))
                        shall apply for  purposes of this subchapter. 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(B) Limit On Benefit


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(B)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
The  aggregate tax benefit allowable under subparagraph
                           (A) with respect to  ozone- depleting chemicals manufactured, produced or imported
                           by any  person during a calendar year shall not exceed the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(B)(i)(I)  — 
                           
                           
the amount equal to the 1986 export percentage of the  aggregate tax which would (but
                              for this subsection and subsection (g)) be  imposed by this subchapter with respect
                              to the maximum quantity of  ozone-depleting chemicals permitted to be manufactured
                              or produced by such  person during such calendar year under regulations prescribed
                              by the  Environmental Protection Agency
                              (other than chemicals with respect to  which subclause (II) applies), 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(B)(i)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the aggregate tax which would (but for this subsection  and subsection (g)) be imposed
                              by this subchapter with respect to any  additional production allowance granted to
                              such person with respect to  ozone-depleting chemicals manufactured or produced by
                              such person during  such calendar year by the Environmental Protection Agency under
                              40 CFR Part 82 (as in effect on September 14, 1989), and 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(B)(i)(III)  — 
                           
                           
the aggregate tax which was imposed by this subchapter  with respect to ozone-depleting
                              chemicals imported by such person during  the calendar year. 



I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(B)(ii) 1986 Export Percentage — 
                        
                        
A  person's 1986 export percentage is the percentage equal to the ozone-  depletion
                           factor adjusted pounds of ozone-depleting chemicals manufactured  or produced by such
                           person during 1986 which were exported during 1986,  divided by the ozone-depletion
                           factor adjusted pounds of all ozone-  depleting chemicals manufactured or produced
                           by such person during 1986.  The percentage determined under the preceding sentence
                           shall be computed  by taking into account the sum of such person's direct 1986 exports
                           (as  determined by the Environmental Protection Agency) and such person's  indirect
                           1986 exports (as allocated to such person by such Agency in  determining such person's
                           consumption and production rights for ozone  depleting chemicals). 



I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(C)  Separate Application Of Limit For Newly Listed Chemicals


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(C)(i) In General — 
                        
                        
Subparagraph  (B) shall be applied separately with respect to newly listed chemicals
                           and  other chemicals. 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(C)(ii) Application To Newly Listed Chemicals — 
                        
                        
In  applying subparagraph (B) to newly listed chemicals— 

I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(C)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
subparagraph (B) shall be applied by substituting “1989”  for “1986” each place it
                              appears, and 


I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(C)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
clause (i)(II) thereof shall be applied by substituting  for the regulations referred
                              to therein any regulations (whether or not  prescribed by the Secretary) which the
                              Secretary determines are comparable  to the regulations referred to in such clause
                              with respect to newly listed  chemicals. 



I.R.C. § 4682(d)(3)(C)(iii) Newly Listed Chemical — 
                        
                        
For  purposes of this subparagraph, the term “newly listed chemical” means any  substance
                           which appears in the table contained in subsection (a)(2) below  Halon-2402.
I.R.C. § 4682(e) Other Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4682(e)(1) Importer — 
                  
                  
The term “importer” means the person entering the article for consumption, use,  or
                     warehousing. 


I.R.C. § 4682(e)(2) United States — 
                  
                  
The  term United States has the meaning given such term by section 4612(a)(4).
I.R.C. § 4682(f) Special Rules


I.R.C. § 4682(f)(1) Fractional Parts Of A Pound — 
                  
                  
In  the case of a fraction of a pound, the tax imposed by this subchapter  shall be
                     the same fraction of the amount of such tax imposed on a whole  pound. 


I.R.C. § 4682(f)(2) Disposition Of Revenues From Puerto Rico And The Virgin Islands — 
                  
                  
The  provisions of subsections (a)(3) and (b)(3) of section 7652 shall not  apply to any tax imposed by this subchapter.
I.R.C. § 4682(g)  Chemicals Used As Propellants In Metered-Dose Inhalers


I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1) Exemption From Tax


I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1)(A)  In General — 
                     
                     
No  tax shall be imposed by section 4681 on— 

I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
any use of any substance as a propellant in metered-dose  inhalers, or 


I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
any qualified sale by the manufacturer, producer, or  importer of any  substance. 



I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1)(B) Qualified Sale — 
                     
                     
For purposes of subparagraph (A), the term “qualified sale” means any sale by  the
                        manufacturer, producer, or importer 
                        of any substance— 

I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
for use by the purchaser as a propellant in metered  dose inhalers, or 


I.R.C. § 4682(g)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
for resale by the purchaser to a 2d purchaser for such  use by the 2d  purchaser. 


The preceding sentence shall apply
                           only if the manufacturer, producer, and  importer, and the 1st and 2d  purchasers
                           (if any) meet such registration  requirements as may be  prescribed by the Secretary.




I.R.C. § 4682(g)(2)  Overpayments — 
                  
                  
If  any substance on which tax was paid under this 
                     subchapter is used by any  person as a propellant in metered-dose inhalers,  credit
                     or refund without  interest shall be allowed to such person in an  amount equal to
                     the tax so  paid. Amounts payable under the preceding  sentence with respect to uses
                     during the taxable year shall be treated as  described in section 34(a)  for such year unless claim thereof has been  timely filed under this  paragraph.
I.R.C. § 4682(h) Imposition Of Floor Stocks Taxes


I.R.C. § 4682(h)(1) In General


I.R.C. § 4682(h)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
If,  on any tax-increase date, any ozone-depleting chemical is held by any  person
                        (other than the manufacturer, producer, or importer thereof) for  sale or for use
                        in further manufacture, there is hereby imposed a floor  stocks tax. 


I.R.C. § 4682(h)(1)(B) Amount Of Tax — 
                     
                     
The  amount of the tax imposed by subparagraph (A) shall be the excess (if any)  of— 

I.R.C. § 4682(h)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the tax which would be imposed under section 4681 on
                           such substance if the sale of such chemical by the manufacturer, producer,  or importer
                           thereof had occurred on the tax-increase date,
                           over 


I.R.C. § 4682(h)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the prior tax (if any) imposed by this subchapter on  such substance. 



I.R.C. § 4682(h)(1)(C) Tax-Increase Date — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph, the term “tax-increase date” means January 1  of
                        any calendar year. 



I.R.C. § 4682(h)(2) Due Date — 
                  
                  
The  taxes imposed by this subsection on  January 1 of any calendar year shall be
                     paid on or  before June 30 of such year. 


I.R.C. § 4682(h)(3) Application Of Other Laws — 
                  
                  
All  other provisions of law, including penalties, applicable with respect to  the
                     taxes imposed by section 4681 shall apply to the floor stocks taxes  imposed by this subsection.
