Sec. 4963. Definitions

I.R.C. § 4963(a) First Tier Tax — 
               
               
For purposes of this subchapter, the term “first tier  tax” means any tax imposed
                  by subsection (a)
                  of section 4941, 4942, 4943,  4944, 4945, 4951, 4952, 4955, 4958, 4966, 4967, 4971, or 4975.
I.R.C. § 4963(b) Second Tier Tax — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter, the term “second tier tax” means any tax  imposed
                  by subsection (b) of section 4941, 4942, 4943, 4944, 4945, 4951,  4952, 4955, 4958, 4971, or 4975.
I.R.C. § 4963(c) Taxable Event — 
               
               
For purposes of this subchapter, the term “taxable  event” means any act (or failure
                  to act) giving rise to liability for tax  under section 4941, 4942, 4943, 4944, 4945, 4951, 4952, 4955, 4958, 4966, 4967, 4971, or 4975.
I.R.C. § 4963(d) Correct — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4963(d)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Except  as provided in paragraph (2), the term “correct"
                     has the same meaning as  when used in the section which imposes the second tier tax. 


I.R.C. § 4963(d)(2) Special Rules — 
                  
                  
The  term “correct” means— 

I.R.C. § 4963(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of the second tier tax imposed by section  4942(b), reducing the amount of the undistributed income to zero, 


I.R.C. § 4963(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of the second tier tax imposed by section  4943(b), reducing the amount of the excess business holdings to zero, and 


I.R.C. § 4963(d)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of the second tier tax imposed by section  4944, removing the investment from jeopardy.
I.R.C. § 4963(e) Correction Period — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 4963(e)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  term “correction period” means, with respect to any taxable event, the  period
                     beginning on the date on which such event occurs and ending 90 days  after the date
                     of mailing under section 6212 of a notice of deficiency
                     with respect to the second tier tax imposed on such taxable event,
                     extended by— 

I.R.C. § 4963(e)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any period in which a deficiency cannot be assessed  under section 6213(a) (determined without regard to the last sentence of  section 4961(b)), and 


I.R.C. § 4963(e)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any other period which the Secretary determines is  reasonable and necessary to bring
                        about correction of the taxable event. 



I.R.C. § 4963(e)(2) Special Rules For When Taxable Event Occurs — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of paragraph (1), the taxable event shall be treated as  occurring— 

I.R.C. § 4963(e)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of section 4942, on the first day of the
                        taxable year for which there was a failure to distribute income, 


I.R.C. § 4963(e)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of section 4943, on the first day on which
                        there are excess business holdings, 


I.R.C. § 4963(e)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of section 4971, on the last day of the plan
                        year in which there is an accumulated funding deficiency, and 


I.R.C. § 4963(e)(2)(D)  — 
                     
                     
in any other case, the date on which such event occurred.
