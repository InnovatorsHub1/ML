Sec. 5173. Bonds

I.R.C. § 5173(a) Operations At, And Withdrawals From, Distilled Spirits Plant Must Be Covered By Bond


I.R.C. § 5173(a)(1) Operations — 
                  
                  
Except as provided under section 5551(d), no  person intending to establish a distilled
                     spirits plant may commence  operations at such plant unless such person has furnished
                     bond covering  operations at such plant. 


I.R.C. § 5173(a)(2) Withdrawals — 
                  
                  
Except as provided under section 5551(d), no  distilled spirits (other than distilled
                     spirits withdrawn under section  5214 or 7510) may be withdrawn from bonded premises
                     except on payment of
                     tax unless the proprietor of the bonded premises has furnished bond
                     covering such withdrawal.
I.R.C. § 5173(b) Operations Bonds — 
               
               
The  bond required by paragraph (1) of subsection (a)
                  shall meet the  requirements of paragraph (1), (2), or (3) of this subsection: 

I.R.C. § 5173(b)(1) One Plant Bond — 
                  
                  
The  bond covers operations at a single distilled spirits plant. 


I.R.C. § 5173(b)(2) Adjacent Wine Cellar Bond — 
                  
                  
The  bond covers operations at a distilled spirits plant and at an adjacent  bonded
                     wine cellar. 


I.R.C. § 5173(b)(3) Area Bond — 
                  
                  
The  bond covers operations at 2 or more distilled spirits plants (and adjacent  bonded
                     wine cellars) which— 

I.R.C. § 5173(b)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
are located in the same geographical area (as designated  in regulations prescribed
                        by the Secretary), and 


I.R.C. § 5173(b)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
are operated by the same person
                        (or, in the case of a  corporation, by such corporation and its controlled subsidiaries).
I.R.C. § 5173(c) Withdrawal Bonds — 
               
               
The  bond required by paragraph (2) of subsection (a)
                  shall cover withdrawals  from 1 or more bonded premises the operations at which could
                  be covered by  the same operations bond under subsection
                  (b).
I.R.C. § 5173(d) Unit Bonds — 
               
               
Under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary, the requirements of paragraphs  (1)
                  and (2) of subsection (a) shall be treated as met by a unit bond which  covers both
                  operations at, and withdrawals from, 1 or more bonded premises  which could be covered
                  by the same operations bond under subsection (b).
I.R.C. § 5173(e) Terms And Conditions


I.R.C. § 5173(e)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Any  bond furnished under this section shall be conditioned that the person  furnishing
                     the bond— 

I.R.C. § 5173(e)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
will faithfully comply with all provisions of law and  regulations relating to the
                        activities covered by such bond, and 


I.R.C. § 5173(e)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
will pay— 

I.R.C. § 5173(e)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
all taxes imposed by this chapter, and 


I.R.C. § 5173(e)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
all penalties incurred by, or fines imposed on, such  person for violation of any
                           such provision. 




I.R.C. § 5173(e)(2) Other Terms And Conditions — 
                  
                  
Any  bond furnished under this section shall contain such other terms and  conditions
                     as may be required by regulations prescribed by the Secretary.
I.R.C. § 5173(f) Amount


I.R.C. § 5173(f)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  penal sum of any bond shall be the amount determined under regulations  prescribed
                     by the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 5173(f)(2) Maximum And Minimum Amount — 
                  
                  
The  Secretary shall by regulations prescribe a minimum amount and a maximum  amount
                     for each type of bond which may be furnished under this section.
I.R.C. § 5173(g) Total Amount Available — 
               
               
The  total amount of any bond furnished under this section shall be available  for
                  the satisfaction of any liability incurred under the terms and  conditions of such
                  bond.
I.R.C. § 5173(h) Special Rules — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 5173(h)(1) Withdrawal Bonds — 
                  
                  
In  the case of any bond furnished under this section which covers withdrawals  but
                     not operations— 

I.R.C. § 5173(h)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such bond shall be in addition to the operations bond,  and 


I.R.C. § 5173(h)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
if distilled spirits are withdrawn under such bond, the  operations bond shall no
                        longer cover liability for payment of the tax on  the spirits withdrawn. 



I.R.C. § 5173(h)(2) Adjacent Wine Cellars


I.R.C. § 5173(h)(2)(A) Requirements — 
                     
                     
No  wine cellar shall be treated as being adjacent to a distilled spirits  plant unless— 

I.R.C. § 5173(h)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
such distilled spirits plant is qualified under this  subchapter for the production
                           of distilled spirits, and 


I.R.C. § 5173(h)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
such wine cellar and the distilled spirits plant are  operated by the same person
                           (or, in the case of a corporation, by such  corporation and its controlled subsidiaries). 



I.R.C. § 5173(h)(2)(B) Bond In Lieu Of Wine Cellar Bond — 
                     
                     
In  the case of any adjacent wine cellar, a bond furnished under this section  which
                        covers operations at such wine cellar shall be in lieu of any bond  which would otherwise
                        be required under section 5354 with respect to such  wine cellar (other than supplemental bonds required under the
                        second  sentence of section 5354).
