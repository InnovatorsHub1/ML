Sec. 5177. Other Provisions Relating To Bonds

I.R.C. § 5177(a) General Provisions Relating To Bonds — 
               
               
The  provisions of section 5551 shall be applicable to the bonds required by or  given under sections 5173 and 5175.
I.R.C. § 5177(b) Cross References


I.R.C. § 5177(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
For deposit of United States bonds or notes in lieu of  sureties, see section 9303
                     of title 31, United States Code. 


I.R.C. § 5177(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
For penalty and forfeiture for failure or refusal to  give bond, or for giving false,
                     forged, or fraudulent bond, or carrying on  the business of a distiller without giving
                     bond, see sections 5601(a)(4),
                     5601(a)(5), 5601(b), and 5615(3).
