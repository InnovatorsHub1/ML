Sec. 5276. Occupational Tax [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 5276(a) General Rule — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 109-59, Sec. 11125(a)(3), repealed Sec. 5276, effective July 1, 2008, but does not apply to taxes imposed
                           for periods before such date.




Except as otherwise provided in this section, a permit  issued under section 5271 shall not be valid with respect to acts  conducted at any place unless the person
                     holding such permit pays a  special tax of $250 with respect to such place.
I.R.C. § 5276(b) Certain Occupational Tax Rules To Apply — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 109-59, Sec. 11125(a)(3), repealed Sec. 5276, effective July 1, 2008, but does not apply to taxes imposed
                           for periods before such date.




Rules similar to the rules of subpart G of part II of  subchapter A shall apply for
                     purposes of this section.
I.R.C. § 5276(c) Exception For United States — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 109-59, Sec. 11125(a)(3), repealed Sec. 5276, effective July 1, 2008, but does not apply to taxes imposed
                           for periods before such date.




Subsection (a) shall not apply to any permit issued to  an agency or instrumentality
                     of the United States.
