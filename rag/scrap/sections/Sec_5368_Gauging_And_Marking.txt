Sec. 5368. Gauging And Marking

I.R.C. § 5368(a) Gauging And Marking — 
               
               
All  wine or wine spirits shall be locked, sealed, and gauged, and shall be  marked,
                  branded, labeled, or otherwise identified, in such manner as the  Secretary may by
                  regulations prescribe.
I.R.C. § 5368(b) Marking — 
               
               
Wines  shall be removed in such containers (including vessels, vehicles, and  pipelines)
                  bearing such marks and labels evidencing compliance with this  chapter, as the Secretary
                  may by regulations prescribe.
