Sec. 5414. Transfer Of Beer Between Bonded Facilities

I.R.C. § 5414(a) In General — 
               
               
Beer may be removed from one brewery to another brewery, without payment of tax, and
                  may be mingled with beer at the receiving brewery, subject to such conditions, including
                  payment of the tax, and in such containers, as the Secretary by regulations shall
                  prescribe, which shall include— 

I.R.C. § 5414(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any removal from one brewery to another brewery belonging to the same brewer, 


I.R.C. § 5414(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any removal from a brewery owned by one corporation to a brewery owned by another
                     corporation when— 

I.R.C. § 5414(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
one such corporation owns the controlling interest in the other such corporation,
                        or 


I.R.C. § 5414(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the controlling interest in each such corporation is owned by the same person or persons,
                        and 



I.R.C. § 5414(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
any removal from one brewery to another brewery when— 

I.R.C. § 5414(a)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the proprietors of transferring and receiving premises are independent of each other
                        and neither has a proprietary interest, directly or indirectly, in the business of
                        the other, and 


I.R.C. § 5414(a)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the transferor has divested itself of all interest in the beer so transferred and
                        the transferee has accepted responsibility for payment of the tax.
I.R.C. § 5414(b) Transfer Of Liability For Tax — 
               
               
For purposes of subsection (a)(3), such relief from liability shall be effective from
                  the time of removal from the transferor's premises, or from the time of divestment
                  of interest, whichever is later.
