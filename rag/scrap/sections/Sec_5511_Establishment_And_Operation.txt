Sec. 5511. Establishment And Operation

I.R.C. § 5511(1)  —

I.R.C. § 5511(2)  —

I.R.C. § 5511(3)  — 
               
               
the manufacturer thereof makes such application, keeps  such records, renders such

                  of the mash or juice from  which such concentrate is produced, as the Secretary may
                  by regulations  prescribe as necessary for the protection of the revenue.
