Sec. 5553. Supervision Of Premises And Operations

I.R.C. § 5553(a) Assignment Of Internal Revenue Officers — 
               
               
The  Secretary is authorized to assign to any premises established under the  provisions
                  of this chapter such number of internal revenue officers as may  be deemed necessary.
I.R.C. § 5553(b) Functions Of Internal Revenue Officer — 
               
               
When  used in this chapter, the term “internal revenue officer assigned to the  premises”
                  means the internal revenue officer assigned by the Secretary to  duties at premises
                  established and operated under the provisions of this  chapter.
