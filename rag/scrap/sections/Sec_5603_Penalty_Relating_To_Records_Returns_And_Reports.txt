Sec. 5603. Penalty Relating To Records, Returns, And Reports

I.R.C. § 5603(a) Fraudulent Noncompliance — 
               
               
Any  person required by this chapter (other than subchapters F and G) or  regulations
                  issued pursuant thereto to keep or file any record, return,  report, summary, transcript,
                  or other document, who, with intent to  defraud the United States, shall— 

I.R.C. § 5603(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
fail to keep any such document or to make required  entries therein; or 


I.R.C. § 5603(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
make any false entry in such document;
                     or 


I.R.C. § 5603(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
cancel, alter, or obliterate any part of such document  or any entry therein, or destroy
                     any part of such document or any entry  therein; or 


I.R.C. § 5603(a)(4)  — 
                  
                  
hinder or obstruct any internal revenue officer from  inspecting any such document
                     or taking any abstracts therefrom; or 


I.R.C. § 5603(a)(5)  — 
                  
                  
fail or refuse to preserve or produce any such document,  as required by this chapter
                     or regulations issued pursuant thereto; or who  shall, with intent to defraud the
                     United States, cause or procure the same  to be done, shall be fined not more than
                     $10,000, or imprisoned not more  than 5 years, or both, for each such offense.
I.R.C. § 5603(b) Failure To Comply — 
               
               
Any  person required by this chapter (other than subchapters F and G) or  regulations
                  issued pursuant thereto to keep or file any record, return,  report, summary, transcript,
                  or other document, who, otherwise than with  intent to defraud the United States,
                  shall— 

I.R.C. § 5603(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
fail to keep any such document or to make required  entries therein; or 


I.R.C. § 5603(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
make any false entry in such document;
                     or 


I.R.C. § 5603(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
cancel, alter, or obliterate any part of such document  or any entry therein, or destroy
                     any part of such document, or any entry  therein, except as provided by this title
                     or regulations issued pursuant  thereto; or 


I.R.C. § 5603(b)(4)  — 
                  
                  
hinder or obstruct any internal revenue officer from  inspecting any such document
                     or taking any abstracts therefrom; or 


I.R.C. § 5603(b)(5)  — 
                  
                  
fail to refuse to preserve or produce any such document,  as required by this chapter
                     or regulations issued pursuant thereto; or who  shall, otherwise than with intent
                     to defraud the United States, cause or  procure the same to be done, shall be fined
                     not more than $1,000, or  imprisoned not more than 1 year, or both, for each such
                     offense. 

(Added by Pub. L. 85-859, title II, 201, Sept. 2, 1958, 72  Stat. 1400; amended by Pub. L. 115-141, Div. U, title IV, Sec. 401(a)(249), Mar. 23, 2018, 132 Stat. 348.)
