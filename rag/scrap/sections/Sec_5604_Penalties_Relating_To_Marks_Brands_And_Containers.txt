Sec. 5604. Penalties Relating To Marks, Brands, And Containers

I.R.C. § 5604(a) In General — 
               
               
Any  person who shall— 

I.R.C. § 5604(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
transport, possess, buy, sell, or transfer any distilled  spirits unless the immediate
                     container bears the type of closure or other  device required by section 5301(d), 


I.R.C. § 5604(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
with intent to defraud the United States, empty a  container bearing the closure or
                     other device required by section 5301(d) 
                     without breaking such closure or other device, 


I.R.C. § 5604(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
empty, or cause to be emptied, any distilled spirits  from an immediate container
                     bearing any mark or brand required by law  without effacing and obliterating such
                     mark or brand as required by  section 5206(d), 


I.R.C. § 5604(a)(4)  — 
                  
                  
place any distilled spirits in any bottle, or reuse any  bottle for the purpose of
                     containing distilled spirits, which has once  been filled and fitted with a closure
                     or other device under the provisions  of this chapter, without removing and destroying
                     such closure or other  device, 


I.R.C. § 5604(a)(5)  — 
                  
                  
willfully and unlawfully remove, change, or deface any  mark, brand, label, or seal
                     affixed to any case of distilled spirits, or  to any bottle contained therein, 


I.R.C. § 5604(a)(6)  — 
                  
                  
with intent to defraud the United States, purchase,  sell, receive with intent to
                     transport, or transport any empty cask or  package having thereon any mark or brand
                     required by law to be affixed to  any cask or package containing distilled spirits,
                     or 


I.R.C. § 5604(a)(7)  — 
                  
                  
change or alter any mark or brand on any cask or package  containing distilled spirits,
                     or put into any cask or package spirits of  greater strength than is indicated by
                     the inspection mark thereon, or  fraudulently use any cask or package having any inspection
                     mark thereon,  for the purpose of selling other spirits, or spirits of quantity or
                     quality different from the spirits previously inspected, 


shall be fined  not more than $10,000 or imprisoned not more than 5 years, or both,
                     for  each such offense.
I.R.C. § 5604(b) Cross References — 
               
               
For  provisions relating to the authority of internal revenue officers to  enforce
                  provisions of this section, see sections 5203, 5557, and 7608.
