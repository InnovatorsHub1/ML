Sec. 5612. Forfeiture Of Taxpaid Distilled Spirits Remaining On Bonded Premises

I.R.C. § 5612(a) General — 
               
               
No  distilled spirits on which tax has been paid or determined shall be stored  or
                  allowed to remain on the bonded premises of any distilled spirits  plant, under the
                  penalty of forfeiture of all spirits so found.
I.R.C. § 5612(b) Exceptions — 
               
               
Subsection  (a) shall not apply in the case of— 

I.R.C. § 5612(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
distilled spirits in the process of prompt removal from  bonded premises on payment
                     or determination of the tax; or 


I.R.C. § 5612(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
distilled spirits returned to bonded premises in  accordance with the provisions of
                     section 5215.
