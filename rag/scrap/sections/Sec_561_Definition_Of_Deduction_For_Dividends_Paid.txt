Sec. 561. Definition Of Deduction For Dividends Paid

I.R.C. § 561(a) General Rule — 
               
               
The  deduction for dividends paid shall be the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 561(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  dividends paid during the taxable year, 


I.R.C. § 561(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  consent dividends for the taxable year (determined under section 565), and 


I.R.C. § 561(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
in  the case of a personal holding company, the dividend carryover described  in section
                     564.
I.R.C. § 561(b) Special Rules Applicable — 
               
               
In  determining the deduction for dividends paid, the rules provided in  section 562 (relating to rules applicable in determining dividends  eligible for dividends paid
                  deduction) and section 563 (relating to  dividends paid after the close of the taxable year) shall be applicable.
