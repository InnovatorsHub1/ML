Sec. 5684. Penalties Relating To The Payment And Collection Of Liquor Taxes

I.R.C. § 5684(a) Failure To Pay Tax — 
               
               
Whoever  fails to pay any tax imposed by part I of subchapter A at the time  prescribed
                  shall, in addition to any other penalty provided in this title,  be liable to a penalty
                  of 5 percent of the tax due but unpaid.
I.R.C. § 5684(b) Applicability Of Section 6665 — 
               
               
The  penalties imposed by subsection (a) shall be assessed, collected, and paid  in
                  the same manner as taxes, as provided in section 6665(a).
