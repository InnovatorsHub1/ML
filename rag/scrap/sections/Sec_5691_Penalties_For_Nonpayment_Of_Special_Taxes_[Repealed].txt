Sec. 5691. Penalties For Nonpayment Of Special Taxes [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 5691(a) General — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 109-59, Sec. 11125(b)(19)(A), repealed Sec. 5691,
                           effective July 1, 2008, but does not apply to taxes imposed for periods before such
                           date.




Any person who shall carry on a business subject to a  special tax imposed by part
                     II of subchapter A or section 5276 (relating
                     to occupational taxes) and willfully fail to pay the special tax as  required by law,
                     shall be fined not more than $5,000, or imprisoned not  more than 2 years, or both,
                     for each such offense.
I.R.C. § 5691(b) Presumption In Case Of The Sale Of 20 Wine Gallons Or More — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 109-59, Sec. 11125(b)(19)(A), repealed Sec. 5691,
                           effective July 1, 2008, but does not apply to taxes imposed for periods before such
                           date.




For the purposes of this chapter, the sale, or offer for  sale, of distilled spirits,
                     wines, or beer, in quantities of 20 wine  gallons or more to the same person at the
                     same time, shall be presumptive  evidence that the person making such sale, or offer
                     for sale, is engaged  in or carrying on the business of a wholesale dealer in liquors
                     or a  wholesale dealer in beer, as the case may be. Such presumption may be  overcome
                     by evidence satisfactorily showing that such sale, or offer for  sale, was made to
                     a person other than a dealer, as defined in section  5112(a).
