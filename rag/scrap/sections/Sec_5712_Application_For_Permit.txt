I.R.C. § 5712(1)  —

I.R.C. § 5712(1)(2)  —

I.R.C. § 5712(3)  —

I.R.C. § 5712(3)(A)  —

                     compliance with this chapter, 


I.R.C. § 5712(3)(B)  —

has failed to disclose any material information required or made any material false
                     statement in the application therefor.
