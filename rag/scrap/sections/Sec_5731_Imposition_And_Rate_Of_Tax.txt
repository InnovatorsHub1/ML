Sec. 5731. Imposition And Rate Of Tax

I.R.C. § 5731(a) General Rule — 
               
               
Every  person engaged in business as— 

I.R.C. § 5731(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
a manufacturer of tobacco products, 


I.R.C. § 5731(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
a manufacturer of cigarette papers
                     and tubes, or 


I.R.C. § 5731(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
an export warehouse proprietor,
                     shall pay a tax of  $1,000 per year in respect of each premises at
                     which such business is  carried on.
I.R.C. § 5731(b) Reduced Rates For Small Proprietors


I.R.C. § 5731(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Subsection  (a) shall be applied by substituting “$500”
                     for “$1,000” with respect to  any taxpayer the gross receipts
                     of which (for the most recent taxable year  ending before the 1st
                     day of the taxable period to which the tax imposed  by subsection
                     (a) relates) are less than $500,000. 


I.R.C. § 5731(b)(2) Controlled Group Rules — 
                  
                  
All  persons treated as 1 taxpayer under section 5061(e)(3) shall be treated
                     as  1 taxpayer for purposes of paragraph (1). 


I.R.C. § 5731(b)(3) Certain Rules To Apply — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of paragraph (1), rules similar to the
                     rules of subparagraphs (B)  and (C) of section 448(c)(3) shall apply.
I.R.C. § 5731(c) Penalty For Failure To Register — 
               
               
Any  person engaged in a business referred to in subsection
                  (a) who willfully  fails to pay the tax imposed by subsection (a)
                  shall be fined not more  than $5,000, or imprisoned not more than
                  2 years, or both, for each such  offense.
