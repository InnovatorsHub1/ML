Sec. 5751. Purchase, Receipt, Possession, Or Sale Of Tobacco Products And Cigarette Papers And Tubes, After Removal

I.R.C. § 5751(a) Restriction — 
               
               
No  person shall— 

I.R.C. § 5751(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
with intent to defraud the United States, purchase,  receive, possess, offer for sale,
                     or sell or otherwise dispose of, after  removal, any tobacco products or cigarette
                     papers or tubes— 

I.R.C. § 5751(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
upon which the tax has not been paid or determined in  the manner and at the time
                        prescribed by this chapter or regulations  thereunder; or 


I.R.C. § 5751(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
which, after removal without payment of tax pursuant to  section 5704, have been diverted from the applicable purpose or use  specified in that section;
                        or 



I.R.C. § 5751(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
with intent to defraud the United States, purchase,  receive, possess, offer for sale,
                     or sell or otherwise dispose of, after  removal, any tobacco products or cigarette
                     papers or tubes, which are not  put up in packages as required under section 5723 or which are put up in  packages not bearing the marks, labels, and notices, as required
                     under  such section; or 


I.R.C. § 5751(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
otherwise than with intent to defraud the United States,  purchase, receive, possess,
                     offer for sale, or sell or otherwise dispose  of, after removal, any tobacco products
                     or cigarette papers or tubes,  which are not put up in packages as required under
                     section 5723 or which  are put up in packages not bearing the marks, labels, and notices, as 
                     required under such section. This paragraph shall not prevent the sale or  delivery
                     of tobacco products or cigarette papers or tubes directly to  consumers from proper
                     packages, nor apply to such articles when so sold or  delivered.
I.R.C. § 5751(b) Liability To Tax — 
               
               
Any  person who possesses tobacco products or cigarette papers or tubes in  violation
                  of subsection (a)(1) or (a)(2) shall be liable for a tax equal  to the tax on such
                  articles.
