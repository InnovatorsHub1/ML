Sec. 5811. Transfer Tax

I.R.C. § 5811(a) Rate — 
               
               
There  shall be levied, collected, and paid on firearms transferred a tax at the 
                  rate of $200 for each firearm transferred, except, the transfer tax on any  firearm
                  classified as any other weapon under section 5845(e) shall be at  the rate of $5 for each such firearm transferred.
I.R.C. § 5811(b) By Whom Paid — 
               
               
The  tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be paid by the  transferor.
I.R.C. § 5811(c) Payment — 
               
               
The  tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be payable by the  appropriate
                  stamps prescribed for payment by the Secretary.
