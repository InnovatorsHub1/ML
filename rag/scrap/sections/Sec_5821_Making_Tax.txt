Sec. 5821. Making Tax

I.R.C. § 5821(a) Rate — 
               
               
There  shall be levied, collected, and paid upon the making of a firearm a tax at
                  the rate of $200 for each firearm made.
I.R.C. § 5821(b) By Whom Paid — 
               
               
The  tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be paid by the person  making
                  the firearm.
I.R.C. § 5821(c) Payment — 
               
               
The  tax imposed by subsection (a) of this section shall be payable by the  stamp
                  prescribed for payment by the Secretary.
