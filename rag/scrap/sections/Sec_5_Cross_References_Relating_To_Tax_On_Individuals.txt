Sec. 5. Cross References Relating To Tax On Individuals

I.R.C. § 5(a) Other Rates Of Tax On Individuals, Etc.


I.R.C. § 5(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
For  rates of tax on nonresident aliens, see section 871. 


I.R.C. § 5(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
For  doubling of tax on citizens of certain foreign countries, see section  891. 


I.R.C. § 5(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
For  rate of withholding in the case of nonresident aliens, see section  1441. 


I.R.C. § 5(a)(4)  — 
                  
                  
For  alternative minimum tax, see section 55.
I.R.C. § 5(b) Special Limitations On Tax


I.R.C. § 5(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
For  limitation on tax in case of income of members of Armed Forces, astronauts, 
                     and victims of certain terrorist attacks on death, see section 692. 


I.R.C. § 5(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
For  computation of tax where taxpayer restores substantial amount held under  claim
                     of right, see section 1341.
