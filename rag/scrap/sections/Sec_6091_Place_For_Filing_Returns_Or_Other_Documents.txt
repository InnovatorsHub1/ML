Sec. 6091. Place For Filing Returns Or Other Documents

I.R.C. § 6091(a) General Rule — 
               
               
When  not otherwise provided for by this title, the Secretary shall by  regulations
                  prescribe the place for the filing of any return, declaration,  statement, or other
                  document, or copies thereof, required by this title or  by regulations.
I.R.C. § 6091(b) Tax Returns — 
               
               
In  the case of returns of tax required under authority of part II of this  subchapter— 

I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1) Persons Other Than Corporations


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(A) General Rule — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), a return (other than a corporation  return)
                        shall be made to the Secretary-- 

I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
in the internal revenue district in which is located the  legal residence or principal
                           place of business of the person making the  return, or 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
at a service center serving the internal revenue  district referred to in clause (i),
                           as the Secretary may by regulations  designate. 



I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(B) Exception — 
                     
                     
Returns  of— 

I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
persons who have no legal residence or principal place  of business in any internal
                           revenue district, 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
citizens of the United States whose principal place of  abode for the period with
                           respect to which the return is filed is outside  the United States, 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
persons who claim the benefits of section 911 (relating
                           to citizens or residents of the United States living abroad), section 931  (relating to income from sources within Guam, American Samoa, or the  Northern Mariana
                           Islands), or section 933 (relating to income from sources  within Puerto Rico), 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(B)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
nonresident alien persons, and 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(1)(B)(v)  — 
                        
                        
persons with respect to whom an assessment was made  under section 6851(a) or 6852(a) (relating to termination assessments)  with respect to the taxable year, 


shall be made at such place as the
                           Secretary may by regulations designate.




I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2) Corporations


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(A) General Rule — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), a return of a corporation shall be made 
                        to the Secretary— 

I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
in the internal revenue district in which is located the  principal place of business
                           or principal office or agency of the  corporation, or 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
at a service center serving the internal revenue  district referred to in clause (i),
                           as the Secretary may by regulations  designate. 



I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(B) Exception — 
                     
                     
Returns  of— 

I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
corporations which have no principal place of business  or principal office or agency
                           in any internal revenue district, 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
foreign corporations, and 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(2)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
corporations with respect to which an assessment was  made under section 6851(a) (relating to termination assessments) with  respect to the taxable year, 


shall be made at such place as the Secretary  may by regulations designate.




I.R.C. § 6091(b)(3) Estate Tax Returns


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(3)(A) General Rule — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), returns of estate tax required under  section
                        6018 shall be made to the Secretary— 

I.R.C. § 6091(b)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
in the internal revenue district in which was the  domicile of the decedent at the
                           time of his death, or 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
at a service center serving the internal revenue  district referred to in clause (i),
                           as the Secretary may by regulations  designate. 



I.R.C. § 6091(b)(3)(B) Exception — 
                     
                     
If  the domicile of the decedent was not in an internal revenue district, or  if he
                        had no domicile, the estate tax return required under section 6018 
                        shall be made at such place as the Secretary may by regulations designate. 



I.R.C. § 6091(b)(4) Hand-Carried Returns — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding  paragraph (1), (2), or (3), a return to which paragraph (1)(A), (2)(A),
                     or  (3)(A) would apply, but for this paragraph, which is made to the Secretary  by
                     handcarrying shall, under regulations prescribed by the Secretary, be  made in the
                     internal revenue district referred to in paragraph (1)(A)(i),  (2)(A)(i), or
                     (3)(A)(i), as the case may be. 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(5) Exceptional Cases — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding  paragraph (1), (2), (3), or (4) of this subsection, the Secretary
                     may  permit a return to be filed in any internal revenue district, and may  require
                     the return of any officer or employee of the Treasury Department  to be filed in any
                     internal revenue district selected by the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 6091(b)(6) Alcohol, Tobacco, And Firearms Returns, Etc. — 
                  
                  
In  the case of any return of tax imposed by section 4181 or subtitle E  (relating to taxes on alcohol, tobacco, and firearms), subsection
                     (a)  shall apply (and this subsection shall not apply).
