Sec. 6112. Material Advisors Of Reportable Transactions Must Keep Lists Of Advisees, Etc.

I.R.C. § 6112(a) In General — 
               
               
Each material advisor (as defined in section 6111) with    respect to any 
                  reportable transaction (as defined in section 6707A(c)) shall    (whether
                  or not required to file a return under section 6111 with respect to such transaction)
                  maintain (in such manner as the Secretary may by  regulations    prescribe)
                  a list— 

I.R.C. § 6112(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
identifying each person with respect to whom such advisor acted as a  material advisor
                     with respect to such transaction, and 


I.R.C. § 6112(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
containing such other information as the Secretary may by regulations  require.
I.R.C. § 6112(b) Special Rules


I.R.C. § 6112(b)(1) Availability For Inspection; Retention Of Information On List — 
                  
                  
Any  person who is required to maintain a list under subsection (a)  (or was  required
                     to maintain a list under subsection
                     (a) as in effect before the  enactment of the American Jobs Creation Act of 2004)— 

I.R.C. § 6112(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
shall make such list available to the Secretary for  inspection upon  written  request
                        by the Secretary, and 


I.R.C. § 6112(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
except as otherwise provided under regulations  prescribed by the Secretary, shall
                        retain any information which is  required to be included on such list for 7 years. 



I.R.C. § 6112(b)(2) Lists Which Would Be Required To Be Maintained By 2 Or More Persons — 
                  
                  
The  Secretary  may prescribe  regulations which provide that, in cases in  which
                     2 or more persons are required under subsection
                     (a) to maintain the  same list (or portion thereof), only 1 person shall be required
                     to  maintain such list (or portion).
