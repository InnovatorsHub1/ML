Sec. 6155. Payment On Notice And Demand

I.R.C. § 6155(a) General Rule — 
               
               
Upon  receipt of notice and demand from the Secretary, there shall be paid at  the
                  place and time stated in such notice the amount of any tax (including  any interest,
                  additional amounts, additions to tax, and assessable  penalties) stated in such notice
                  and demand.
I.R.C. § 6155(b) Cross References


I.R.C. § 6155(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
For restrictions on assessment and collection of  deficiency assessments of taxes
                     subject to the jurisdiction of the Tax  Court, see sections 6212 and 6213. 


I.R.C. § 6155(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
For provisions relating to assessment of claims allowed  in a receivership proceeding,
                     see section 6873. 


I.R.C. § 6155(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
For provisions relating to jeopardy assessments, see  subchapter A of chapter 70.
