Sec. 6221. Determination At Partnership Level

I.R.C. § 6221(a) In General — 
               
               
Any adjustment to a partnership-related item shall be
                  determined, and any tax attributable thereto shall be assessed and
                  collected, and the applicability of any penalty, addition to tax,
                  or additional amount which relates to an adjustment to any such item
                  shall be determined, at the partnership level, except to the extent
                  otherwise provided in this subchapter.
I.R.C. § 6221(b) Election Out For Certain Partnerships With 100 Or Fewer Partners,
                  Etc.


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
This subchapter shall not apply with respect to any partnership
                     for any taxable year if— 

I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the partnership elects the application
                        of this subsection for such taxable year, 


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
for such taxable year the partnership
                        is required to furnish 100 or fewer statements under section 6031(b)
                        with respect to its partners, 


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
each of the partners of such partnership
                        is an individual, a C corporation, any foreign entity that would be
                        treated as a C corporation were it domestic, an S corporation, or
                        an estate of a deceased partner, 


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
the election— 

I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(D)(i)  — 
                        
                        
is made with a timely filed return for
                           such taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(D)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
includes (in the manner prescribed by
                           the Secretary) a disclosure of the name and taxpayer identification
                           number of each partner of such partnership, and 



I.R.C. § 6221(b)(1)(E)  — 
                     
                     
the partnership notifies each such partner
                        of such election in the manner prescribed by the Secretary. 



I.R.C. § 6221(b)(2) Special Rules Relating To Certain Partners


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(2)(A) S Corporation Partners — 
                     
                     
In the case of a partner that is an S corporation— 

I.R.C. § 6221(b)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the partnership shall only be treated
                           as meeting the requirements of paragraph (1)(C) with respect to such
                           partner if such partnership includes (in the manner prescribed by
                           the Secretary) a disclosure of the name and taxpayer identification
                           number of each person with respect to whom such S corporation is required
                           to furnish a statement under section 6037(b) for the taxable year
                           of the S corporation ending with or within the partnership taxable
                           year for which the application of this subsection is elected, and 


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the statements such S corporation is
                           required to so furnish shall be treated as statements furnished by
                           the partnership for purposes of paragraph (1)(B). 



I.R.C. § 6221(b)(2)(B) Foreign Partners — 
                     
                     
For purposes of paragraph (1)(D)(ii), the Secretary may
                        provide for alternative identification of any foreign partners. 


I.R.C. § 6221(b)(2)(C) Other Partners — 
                     
                     
The Secretary may by regulation or other guidance prescribe
                        rules similar to the rules of subparagraph (A) with respect to any
                        partners not described in such subparagraph or paragraph (1)(C).
