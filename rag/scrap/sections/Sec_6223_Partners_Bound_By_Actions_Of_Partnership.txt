Sec. 6223. Partners Bound By Actions Of Partnership

I.R.C. § 6223(a) Designation Of Partnership Representative — 
               
               
Each partnership shall designate
                  (in the manner prescribed by the Secretary) a partner (or other person)
                  with a substantial presence in the United States as the partnership
                  representative who shall have the sole authority to act on behalf
                  of the partnership under this subchapter. In any case in which such
                  a designation is not in effect, the Secretary may select any person
                  as the partnership representative.
I.R.C. § 6223(b) Binding Effect — 
               
               
A partnership and all partners of
                  such partnership shall be bound— 

I.R.C. § 6223(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
by actions taken under this subchapter
                     by the partnership, and 


I.R.C. § 6223(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
by any final decision in a proceeding
                     brought under this subchapter with respect to the partnership.
