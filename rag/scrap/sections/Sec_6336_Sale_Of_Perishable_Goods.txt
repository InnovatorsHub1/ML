Sec. 6336. Sale Of Perishable Goods

I.R.C. § 6336(1) Return To Owner — 
               
               
If  the owner of the property can be readily found, the Secretary shall give  him
                  notice of such determination of the appraised value of the property.  The property
                  shall be returned to the owner if, within such time as may be  specified in the notice,
                  the owner— 

I.R.C. § 6336(1)(A)  — 
                  
                  
Pays to the Secretary an amount equal to the appraised  value, or 


I.R.C. § 6336(1)(B)  — 
                  
                  
Gives bond in such form, with such sureties, and in such  amount as the Secretary
                     shall prescribe, to pay the appraised amount at  such time as the Secretary determines
                     to be appropriate in the  circumstances.
I.R.C. § 6336(2) Immediate Sale — 
               
               
If  the owner does not pay such amount or furnish such bond in accordance with  this
                  section, the Secretary shall as soon as practicable make public sale  of the property
                  in accordance with such regulations as may be prescribed  by the Secretary.
