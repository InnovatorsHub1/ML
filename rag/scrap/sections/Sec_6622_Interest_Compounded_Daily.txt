Sec. 6622. Interest Compounded Daily

I.R.C. § 6622(a) General Rule — 
               
               
In  computing the amount of any interest required to be paid under this title  or
                  sections 1961(c)(1) or 2411 of title 28, United States Code, by the  Secretary or
                  by the taxpayer, or any other amount determined by reference  to such amount of interest,
                  such interest and such amount shall be  compounded daily.
I.R.C. § 6622(b) Exception For Penalty For Failure To File Estimated Tax — 
               
               
Subsection  (a) shall not apply for purposes of computing the amount of any addition
                  to tax under section 6654 or 6655.
