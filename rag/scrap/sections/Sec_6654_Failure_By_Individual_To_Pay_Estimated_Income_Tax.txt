Sec. 6654. Failure By Individual To Pay Estimated Income Tax

I.R.C. § 6654(a) Addition To The Tax — 
               
               
Except  as otherwise provided in
                  this section, in the case of any underpayment of  estimated tax by
                  an individual, there shall be added to the tax under  chapter 1, the
                  tax under chapter 2, and the tax under chapter 2A for the taxable
                  year an amount  determined by applying— 

I.R.C. § 6654(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the underpayment rate established under section 6621, 


I.R.C. § 6654(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
to the amount of the underpayment, 


I.R.C. § 6654(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
for the period of the underpayment.
I.R.C. § 6654(b) Amount Of Underpayment; Period Of Underpayment — 
               
               
For  purposes of subsection (a)— 

I.R.C. § 6654(b)(1) Amount — 
                  
                  
The  amount of the underpayment shall be the excess
                     of— 

I.R.C. § 6654(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the required installment, over 


I.R.C. § 6654(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the amount (if any) of the installment
                        paid on or before  the due date for the installment. 



I.R.C. § 6654(b)(2) Period Of Underpayment — 
                  
                  
The  period of the underpayment shall run from the due
                     date for the installment  to whichever of the following dates is the
                     earlier— 

I.R.C. § 6654(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the 15th day of the 4th month following
                        the close of the  taxable year, or 


I.R.C. § 6654(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
with respect to any portion of the
                        underpayment, the  date on which such portion is paid. 



I.R.C. § 6654(b)(3) Order Of Crediting Payments — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of paragraph (2)(B), a payment of
                     estimated tax shall be credited  against unpaid required installments
                     in the order in which such  installments are required to be paid.
I.R.C. § 6654(c) Number Of Required Installments; Due Dates — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 6654(c)(1) Payable In 4 Installments — 
                  
                  
There  shall be 4 required installments for each taxable
                     year. 


I.R.C. § 6654(c)(2)  Time For Payment Of Installments







In the case of the following required installments:
The due date is:


1st  … 
April 15


2nd  … 
June 15


3rd  … 
September 15


4th  … 
January 15 of the following taxable year.
I.R.C. § 6654(d) Amount Of Required Installments — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1) Amount


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in paragraph (2), the amount of any required
                        installment shall  be 25 percent of the required annual payment. 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(B) Required Annual Payment — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subparagraph (A), the term “required
                        annual payment” means the  lesser of— 

I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
90 percent of the tax shown on the
                           return for the  taxable year (or, if no return is filed, 90 percent
                           of the tax for such  year), or 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
100 percent of the tax shown on
                           the return of the  individual for the preceding taxable year. 


Clause (ii) shall
                           not apply if the preceding taxable  year was not a taxable year of
                           12 months or if the individual did not file  a return for such preceding
                           taxable year.



I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(C) Limitation On Use Of Preceding Year's Tax


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(C)(i)  In General — 
                        
                        
If  the adjusted gross income shown on the return of
                           the individual for the  preceding taxable year beginning in any calendar
                           year exceeds $150,000,  clause (ii) of subparagraph (B) shall be applied
                           by substituting “110 percent” for “100 percent”. 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(C)(ii) Separate Returns — 
                        
                        
In  the case of a married individual (within the meaning
                           of section 7703)
                           who  files a separate return for the taxable year for which the amount
                           of the  installment is being determined, clause (i) shall be applied
                           by  substituting “$75,000” for “$150,000”. 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(1)(C)(iii) Special Rule — 
                        
                        
In  the case of an estate or trust, adjusted gross income
                           shall be determined  as provided in section 67(e). 




I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2) Lower Required Installment Where Annualized Income Installment
                     Is Less Than Amount Determined Under Paragraph (1)


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
In  the case of any required installment, if the individual
                        establishes that  the annualized income installment is less than the
                        amount determined under  paragraph (1)— 

I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the amount of such required installment
                           shall be the  annualized income installment, and 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
any reduction in a required installment
                           resulting from  the application of this subparagraph shall be recaptured
                           by increasing the  amount of the next required installment determined
                           under paragraph (1) by
                           the amount of such reduction (and by increasing subsequent required
                           installments to the extent that the reduction has not previously
                           been  recaptured under this clause). 



I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(B) Determination Of Annualized Income Installment — 
                     
                     
In  the case of any required installment, the annualized
                        income installment is  the excess (if any) of— 

I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
an amount equal to the applicable
                           percentage of the tax  for the taxable year computed by placing on
                           an annualized basis the  taxable income, alternative minimum taxable
                           income, and adjusted  self-employment income for months in the taxable
                           year ending before the  due date for the installment, over 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the aggregate amount of any prior
                           required installments  for the taxable year. 



I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(C) Special Rules — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph— 

I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(C)(i) Annualization — 
                        
                        
The  taxable income, alternative minimum taxable income,
                           and adjusted  self-employment income shall be placed on an annualized
                           basis under  regulations prescribed by the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(C)(ii) Applicable Percentage







In the case of the following
                                       required installments:
The applicable percentage is:


1st  … 
22.5


2nd  … 
45


3rd  … 
67.5


4th  … 
90






I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(C)(iii) Adjusted Self-Employment Income — 
                        
                        
The  term “adjusted self-employment income” means self-employment
                           income (as  defined in section 1402(b));
                           except that section 1402(b) shall
                           be applied  by placing wages (within the meaning of section 1402(b)) for months in the
                           taxable year ending before the due date for the installment on an
                           annualized basis consistent with clause (i). 



I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(D) Treatment Of  Subpart F Income


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(D)(i) In General  — 
                        
                        
Any  amounts required to be included in gross income
                           under section 951(a) (and
                           credits properly allocable thereto) shall be taken into  account in
                           computing any annualized income installment under subparagraph  (B) in a manner similar
                           to the manner under which partnership income  inclusions (and credits
                           properly allocable thereto) are taken into  account. 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(D)(ii) Prior Year Safe Harbor — 
                        
                        
If  a taxpayer elects to have this clause apply to any
                           taxable year— 

I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(D)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
clause (i) shall not apply,
                              and 


I.R.C. § 6654(d)(2)(D)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
for purposes of computing any annualized
                              income  installment for such taxable year, the taxpayer shall be treated
                              as having  received ratably during such taxable year items of income
                              and credit  described in clause (i) in an amount equal
                              to the amount of such items  shown on the return of the taxpayer for
                              the preceding taxable year (the  second preceding taxable year in
                              the case of the first and second required  installments for such taxable
                              year).
I.R.C. § 6654(e) Exceptions


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(1) Where Tax Is Small Amount — 
                  
                  
No  addition to tax shall be imposed under subsection (a) for any taxable year  if
                     the tax shown on the return for such taxable year (or, if no return
                     is  filed, the tax), reduced by the credit allowable under section 31, is less  than $1,000. 


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(2) Where No Tax Liability For Preceding Taxable Year — 
                  
                  
No  addition to tax shall be imposed under subsection (a) for any taxable year  if— 

I.R.C. § 6654(e)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the preceding taxable year was a
                        taxable year of 12  months, 


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the individual did not have any
                        liability for tax for  the preceding taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
the individual was a citizen or
                        resident of the United  States throughout the preceding taxable year. 



I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3) Waiver In Certain Cases


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
No  addition to tax shall be imposed under subsection (a) with respect to any  underpayment
                        to the extent the Secretary determines that by reason of  casualty,
                        disaster, or other unusual circumstances the imposition of such  addition
                        to tax would be against equity and good conscience. 


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3)(B) Newly Retired Or Disabled Individuals — 
                     
                     
No  addition to tax shall be imposed under subsection (a) with respect to any  underpayment
                        if the Secretary determines that— 

I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the taxpayer— 

I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3)(B)(i)(I)  — 
                           
                           
retired after having attained age
                              62, or 


I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3)(B)(i)(II)  — 
                           
                           
became disabled, in the taxable
                              year for which estimated  payments were required to be made or in
                              the taxable year preceding such  taxable year, and 



I.R.C. § 6654(e)(3)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
such underpayment was due to reasonable
                           cause and not to  willful neglect.
I.R.C. § 6654(f) Tax Computed After Application Of Credits Against Tax — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the
                  term “tax” means— 

I.R.C. § 6654(f)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the tax imposed by chapter 1 (other than any increase
                     in  such tax by reason of section 143(m)),
                     plus 


I.R.C. § 6654(f)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the tax imposed by chapter 2, plus 


I.R.C. § 6654(f)(3)  — 
                  
                  
the tax imposed by chapter 2A, minus 


I.R.C. § 6654(f)(4)  — 
                  
                  
the credits against tax provided by part IV of  subchapter
                     A of chapter 1, other than the credit against tax provided by  section 31 (relating to tax withheld on
                     wages).
I.R.C. § 6654(g) Application Of Section In Case Of Tax Withheld On Wages


I.R.C. § 6654(g)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of applying this section, the amount of
                     the credit allowed under  section 31 for
                     the taxable year shall be deemed a payment of estimated  tax, and
                     an equal part of such amount shall be deemed paid on each due  date
                     for such taxable year, unless the taxpayer establishes the dates on
                     which all amounts were actually withheld, in which case the amounts
                     so  withheld shall be deemed payments of estimated tax on the dates
                     on which  such amounts were actually withheld. 


I.R.C. § 6654(g)(2) Separate Application — 
                  
                  
The  taxpayer may apply paragraph (1) separately with respect
                     to— 

I.R.C. § 6654(g)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
wage withholding, and 


I.R.C. § 6654(g)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
all other amounts withheld for which
                        credit is allowed  under section 31.
I.R.C. § 6654(h) Special Rule Where Return Filed On Or Before January 31 — 
               
               
If,  on or before January 31 of the following taxable
                  year, the taxpayer files  a return for the taxable year and pays in
                  full the amount computed on the  return as payable, then no addition
                  to tax shall be imposed under  subsection (a) with respect to any underpayment
                  of the 4th required  installment for the taxable year.
I.R.C. § 6654(i) Special Rules For Farmers And Fishermen — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
If  an individual is a farmer or fisherman for any taxable
                     year— 

I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
there shall be only 1 required installment
                        for the  taxable year, 


I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the due date for such installment
                        shall be January 15 of  the following taxable year, 


I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
the amount of such installment shall
                        be equal to the  required annual payment determined under subsection (d)(1)(B) by  substituting
                        “662/3 percent” for “90 percent” and without regard to  subparagraph (C) of subsection (d)(1), and 


I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
subsection (h) shall be applied— 

I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1)(D)(i)  — 
                        
                        
by substituting “March 1” for “January
                           31”, and 


I.R.C. § 6654(i)(1)(D)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
by treating the required installment
                           described in  subparagraph (A) of
                           this paragraph as the 4th required installment. 




I.R.C. § 6654(i)(2) Farmer Or Fisherman Defined — 
                  
                  
An  individual is a farmer or fisherman for any taxable
                     year if— 

I.R.C. § 6654(i)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the individual's gross income from
                        farming or fishing  (including oyster farming) for the taxable year
                        is at least 662/3 percent  of the total gross income from all sources for
                        the taxable year, or 


I.R.C. § 6654(i)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such individual's gross income from
                        farming or fishing  (including oyster farming) shown on the return
                        of the individual for the  preceding taxable year is at least 662/3 percent
                        of the total gross  income from all sources shown on such return.
I.R.C. § 6654(j) Special Rules For Nonresident Aliens — 
               
               
In  the case of a nonresident alien described in section 6072(c): 

I.R.C. § 6654(j)(1) Payable In 3 Installments — 
                  
                  
There  shall be 3 required installments for the taxable
                     year.

I.R.C. § 6654(k) Fiscal Years And Short Years


I.R.C. § 6654(k)(1) Fiscal Years — 
                  
                  
In  applying this section to a taxable year beginning
                     on any date other than  January 1, there shall be substituted, for
                     the months specified in this  section, the months which correspond
                     thereto. 


I.R.C. § 6654(k)(2) Short Taxable Year — 
                  
                  
This  section shall be applied to taxable years of less
                     than 12 months in  accordance with regulations prescribed by the Secretary.
I.R.C. § 6654(l) Estates And Trusts


I.R.C. § 6654(l)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Except  as otherwise provided in this subsection, this
                     section shall apply to any  estate or trust. 


I.R.C. § 6654(l)(2) Exception For Estates And Certain Trusts — 
                  
                  
With  respect to any taxable year ending before the
                     date 2 years after the date  of the decedent's death, this section
                     shall not apply to— 

I.R.C. § 6654(l)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the estate of such decedent, or 


I.R.C. § 6654(l)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any trust— 

I.R.C. § 6654(l)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
all of which was treated (under
                           subpart E of part I of  subchapter J of chapter 1) as owned by the
                           decedent, and 


I.R.C. § 6654(l)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
to which the residue of the decedent's
                           estate will pass  under his will (or, if no will is admitted to probate,
                           which is the trust  primarily responsible for paying debts, taxes,
                           and expenses of  administration). 




I.R.C. § 6654(l)(3) Exception For Charitable Trusts And Private Foundations — 
                  
                  
This  section shall not apply to any trust which is
                     subject to the tax imposed  by section 511 or
                     which is a private foundation. 


I.R.C. § 6654(l)(4) Special Rule For Annualizations — 
                  
                  
In  the case of any estate or trust to which this section
                     applies, subsection  (d)(2)(B)(i) shall
                     be applied by  substituting “ending before the date 1 month before
                     the due date for the  installment” for “ending before the due date
                     for the installment”.
I.R.C. § 6654(m) Special Rule For Medicare Tax — 
               
               
For purposes of this section, the
                  tax imposed under section 3101(b)(2) (to
                  the extent not withheld) shall be treated as a tax imposed under chapter
                  2.
I.R.C. § 6654(n) Regulations — 
               
               
The  Secretary shall prescribe such
                  regulations as may be necessary to carry  out the purposes of this
                  section.
