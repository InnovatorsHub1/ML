Sec. 6671. Rules For Application Of Assessable Penalties

I.R.C. § 6671(a) Penalty Assessed As Tax — 
               
               
The  penalties and liabilities provided by this subchapter shall be paid upon  notice
                  and demand by the Secretary, and shall be assessed and collected in  the same manner
                  as taxes. Except as otherwise provided, any reference in  this title to “tax” imposed
                  by this title shall be deemed also to refer to  the penalties and liabilities provided
                  by this subchapter.
I.R.C. § 6671(b) Person Defined — 
               
               
The  term “person”, as used in this subchapter, includes an officer or employee  of
                  a corporation, or a member or employee of a partnership, who as such  officer, employee,
                  or member is under a duty to perform the act in respect  of which the violation occurs.
