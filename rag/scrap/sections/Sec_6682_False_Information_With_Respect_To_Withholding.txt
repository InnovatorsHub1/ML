Sec. 6682. False Information With Respect To Withholding

I.R.C. § 6682(a) Civil Penalty — 
               
               
In  addition to any criminal penalty provided by law, if— 

I.R.C. § 6682(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any individual makes a statement under section 3402 or
                     section 3406 which results in a decrease in the amounts deducted and  withheld under chapter 24,
                     and 


I.R.C. § 6682(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
as of the time such statement was made, there was no  reasonable basis for such statement, 


such individual shall pay a penalty
                     of $500 for such statement.
I.R.C. § 6682(b) Exception — 
               
               
The  Secretary may waive (in whole or in part) the penalty imposed under  subsection
                  (a) if the taxes imposed with respect to the individual under  subtitle A for the
                  taxable year are equal to or less than the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 6682(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the credits against such taxes allowed by part IV of  subchapter A of chapter 1, and 


I.R.C. § 6682(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the payments of estimated tax which are considered  payments on account of such taxes.
I.R.C. § 6682(c) Deficiency Procedures Not To Apply — 
               
               
Subchapter  B of chapter 63 (relating to deficiency procedures for income, estate,
                  gift, and certain excise taxes) shall not apply in respect to the  assessment or collection
                  of any penalty imposed by subsection (a).
