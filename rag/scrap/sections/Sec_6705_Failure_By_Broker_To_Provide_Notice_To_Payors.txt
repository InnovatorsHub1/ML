Sec. 6705. Failure By Broker To Provide Notice To Payors

I.R.C. § 6705(a) In General — 
               
               
Any  person required under section 3406(d)(2)(B) to provide notice to any payor  who willfully fails to provide such notice to such
                  payor shall pay a  penalty of $500 for each such failure.
I.R.C. § 6705(b) Penalty In Addition To Other Penalties — 
               
               
Any  penalty imposed by this section shall be in addition to any other penalty  provided
                  by law. 

(Added Pub. L. 98-67, title I, 104(c)(1), Aug. 5, 1983,  97 Stat. 379.)
