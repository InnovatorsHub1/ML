Sec. 6712. Failure To Disclose Treaty-Based Return Positions

I.R.C. § 6712(a) General Rule — 
               
               
If  a taxpayer fails to meet the requirements of section 6114, there is hereby  imposed a penalty equal to $1,000 ($10,000 in the case of a C corporation)
                  on each such failure.
I.R.C. § 6712(b) Authority To Waive — 
               
               
The  Secretary may waive all or any part of the penalty provided by this  section
                  on a showing by the taxpayer that there was reasonable cause for  the failure and
                  that the taxpayer acted in good faith.
I.R.C. § 6712(c) Penalty In Addition To Other Penalties — 
               
               
The  penalty imposed by this section shall be in addition to any other penalty  imposed
                  by law. 

(Added Pub. L. 100-647, title I, Sec. 1012(aa)(5)(B),  Nov. 10, 1988, 102 Stat. 3532.)
