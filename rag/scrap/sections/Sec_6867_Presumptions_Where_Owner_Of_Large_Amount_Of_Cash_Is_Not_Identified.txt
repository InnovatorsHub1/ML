Sec. 6867. Presumptions Where Owner Of Large Amount Of Cash Is Not Identified

I.R.C. § 6867(a) General Rule — 
               
               
If  the individual who is in physical possession of cash in excess of $10,000  does
                  not claim such cash-- 

I.R.C. § 6867(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
as  his, or 


I.R.C. § 6867(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
as  belonging to another person whose identity the Secretary can readily  ascertain
                     and who acknowledges ownership of such cash, then, for purposes  of sections 6851 and 6861, it shall be presumed that such cash represents  gross income of a single individual
                     for the taxable year in which the  possession occurs, and that the collection of tax
                     will be jeopardized by  delay.
I.R.C. § 6867(b) Rules For Assessing — 
               
               
In  the case of any assessment resulting from the application of subsection  (a)-- 

I.R.C. § 6867(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  entire amount of the cash shall be treated as taxable income for the  taxable
                     year in which the possession occurs, 


I.R.C. § 6867(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
such  income shall be treated as taxable at the highest rate of tax specified in 
                     section 1, and 


I.R.C. § 6867(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
except  as provided in subsection (c), the possessor of the cash shall be treated
                     (solely with respect to such cash) as the taxpayer for purposes of  chapters 63 and
                     64 and section 7429(a)(1).
I.R.C. § 6867(c) Effect Of Later Substitution Of True Owner — 
               
               
If,  after an assessment resulting from the application of subsection (a), such  assessment
                  is abated and replaced by an assessment against the owner of  the cash, such later
                  assessment shall be treated for purposes of all laws  relating to lien, levy and collection
                  as relating back to the date of the  original assessment.
I.R.C. § 6867(d) Definitions — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section-- 

I.R.C. § 6867(d)(1) Cash — 
                  
                  
The  term “cash” includes any cash equivalent. 


I.R.C. § 6867(d)(2) Cash Equivalent — 
                  
                  
The  term “cash equivalent” means-- 

I.R.C. § 6867(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
foreign  currency, 


I.R.C. § 6867(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any  bearer obligation, and 


I.R.C. § 6867(d)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
any  medium of exchange which-- 

I.R.C. § 6867(d)(2)(C)(i)  — 
                        
                        
is of  a type which has been frequently used in illegal activities, and 


I.R.C. § 6867(d)(2)(C)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
is  specified as a cash equivalent for purposes of this part in regulations  prescribed
                           by the Secretary. 




I.R.C. § 6867(d)(3) Value Of Cash Equivalent — 
                  
                  
Any  cash equivalent shall be taken into account-- 

I.R.C. § 6867(d)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of a bearer obligation, at its face amount,  and 


I.R.C. § 6867(d)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of any other cash equivalent, at its fair  market value. 

(Added Pub. L. 97-248, title III, 330(a), Sept. 3, 1982,  96 Stat. 619, and amended Pub. L. 100-647, title I, 1001(a)(1), Nov. 10,  1988, 102 Stat. 3349.)
