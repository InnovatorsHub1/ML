Sec. 6873. Unpaid Claims

I.R.C. § 6873(a) General Rule — 
               
               
Any  portion of a claim for taxes allowed in a receivership proceeding which is  unpaid
                  shall be paid by the taxpayer upon notice and demand from the  Secretary after the
                  termination of such proceeding.
I.R.C. § 6873(b) Cross References


I.R.C. § 6873(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
For suspension of running of period of limitations on  collection, see section 6503(b). 


I.R.C. § 6873(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
For extension of time for payment, see section 6161(c).
