Sec. 7217. Prohibition On Executive Branch Influence Over Taxpayer Audits And Other Investigations

I.R.C. § 7217(a) Prohibition. -- — 
               
               
It  shall be unlawful for any applicable person to request, directly or  indirectly,
                  any officer or employee of the Internal Revenue Service to  conduct or terminate an
                  audit or other investigation of any particular  taxpayer with respect to the tax liability
                  of such taxpayer.
I.R.C. § 7217(b) Reporting Requirement. -- — 
               
               
Any  officer or employee of the Internal Revenue Service receiving any request  prohibited
                  by subsection (a) shall report the receipt of such request to  the Treasury Inspector General for Tax
                  Administration.
I.R.C. § 7217(c) Exceptions. -- — 
               
               
Subsection (a) shall not apply to any written request made-- 

I.R.C. § 7217(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
to an applicable person by or on behalf of the taxpayer  and forwarded by such applicable
                     person to the Internal Revenue  Service; 


I.R.C. § 7217(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
by an applicable person for disclosure of return or  return information under section
                     6103 if such request is made in
                     accordance with the requirements of such section; or 


I.R.C. § 7217(c)(3)  — 
                  
                  
by the Secretary of the Treasury as a consequence of  the implementation of a change
                     in tax policy.
I.R.C. § 7217(d) Penalty. -- — 
               
               
Any  person who willfully violates subsection (a) or fails to report under
                  subsection (b) shall be punished upon conviction by a fine in any amount  not exceeding
                  $5,000, or imprisonment of not more than 5 years, or both,  together with the costs
                  of prosecution.
I.R.C. § 7217(e) Applicable Person. -- — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term  “applicable person” means-- 

I.R.C. § 7217(e)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the President, the Vice President, any employee of the  executive office of the President,
                     and any employee of the executive  office of the Vice President; and 


I.R.C. § 7217(e)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any individual (other than the Attorney General of the  United States) serving in
                     a position specified in section 5312 of title 5,  United States Code. 

(Added Pub. L. 105-206, title I, Sec. 1105(a), July 22,  1998, 112 Stat 685.)
