Sec. 7275. Penalty For Offenses Relating To Certain Airline Tickets And Advertising

I.R.C. § 7275(a) Tickets — 
               
               
In  the case of transportation by air all of which is taxable transportation  (as
                  defined in section 4262), the ticket for such transportation shall  show the total of-- 

I.R.C. § 7275(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  amount paid for such transportation, and 


I.R.C. § 7275(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  taxes imposed by subsections (a) and (b) of section 4261.
I.R.C. § 7275(b) Advertising — 
               
               
In  the case of transportation by air all of which is taxable transportation  (as
                  defined in section 4262) or would be taxable transportation if section 4262 did not include subsection (b) thereof, any advertising made by or on
                  behalf of any person furnishing such transportation (or offering to  arrange such
                  transportation) which states the cost of such transportation
                  shall-- 

I.R.C. § 7275(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
state  such cost as the total of (A)
                     the amount to be paid for such  transportation, and (B) the taxes imposed by sections
                     4261(a), (b), and (c), and 


I.R.C. § 7275(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
if  any such advertising states separately the amount to be paid for such  transportation
                     or the amount of such taxes, state such total at  least as prominently as the more
                     prominently stated of the amount to be  paid for such transportation or the amount
                     of such taxes and shall  describe such taxes substantially as: “user taxes to pay
                     for airport  construction and airway safety and operations.”
I.R.C. § 7275(c) Non-Tax Charges


I.R.C. § 7275(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In the case of transportation by air for which disclosure on the ticket or advertising
                     for such transportation of the amounts paid for passenger taxes is required by subsection
                     (a)(2) or (b)(1)(B), if such amounts are separately disclosed, it shall be unlawful
                     for the disclosure of such amounts to include any amounts not attributable to such
                     taxes. 


I.R.C. § 7275(c)(2) Inclusion In Transportation Cost — 
                  
                  
Nothing in this subsection shall prohibit the inclusion of amounts not attributable
                     to the taxes imposed by subsection (a),
                     (b), or (c) of section 4261 in the disclosure of the amount paid for transportation as required by subsection
                     (a)(1) or (b)(1)(A), or in a separate disclosure of amounts not attributable to such
                     taxes.
I.R.C. § 7275(d) Penalty — 
               
               
Any  person who violates any provision of subsection (a), (b), or (c) is, for each  violation, guilty of a misdemeanor, and upon conviction thereof shall
                  be  fined not more than $100.
