Sec. 7324. Special Disposition Of Perishable Goods

I.R.C. § 7324(1) Application For Examination — 
               
               
The  owner thereof, or the United States marshal of the district, may apply to  the
                  Secretary to examine it; and
I.R.C. § 7324(2) Appraisal — 
               
               
If,  in the opinion of the Secretary, it shall be necessary that such property  should
                  be sold to prevent such waste or expense, the Secretary shall  appraise the same;
                  and thereupon
I.R.C. § 7324(3) Return To Owner Under Bond — 
               
               
The  owner shall have such property returned to him upon giving bond in an  amount
                  equal to such appraised value to abide the final order, decree, or  judgment of the
                  court having cognizance of the case, and to pay the amount  of said appraised value
                  to the Secretary, the United States marshal, or  otherwise, as may be ordered and
                  directed by the court, which bond shall  be filed by the Secretary with the United
                  States attorney for the district  in which the proceedings in rem authorized in section
                  7323 may be  commenced.
I.R.C. § 7324(4) Sale In Absence Of Bond


I.R.C. § 7324(4)(A) Order To Sell — 
                  
                  
If such owner shall neglect or refuse to give such bond, the Secretary shall  issue
                     to any Treasury officer or employee or to the United States marshal  an order to sell
                     the same. 


I.R.C. § 7324(4)(B) Manner Of Sale — 
                  
                  
Such Treasury officer or employee or the marshal shall as soon as practicable  make
                     public sale of such property in accordance with such regulations as  may be prescribed
                     by the Secretary. 


I.R.C. § 7324(4)(C) Disposition Of Proceeds — 
                  
                  
The proceeds of the sale, after deducting the reasonable costs of the seizure  and
                     sale, shall be paid to the court to abide its final order, decree, or  judgment.
I.R.C. § 7324(5) Form Of Bond And Sureties — 
               
               
For provisions relating to form and sureties on bonds, see section 7101.
