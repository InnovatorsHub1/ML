Sec. 7326. Disposal Of Forfeited Or Abandoned Property In Special Cases [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 7326(a) Coin-Operated Gaming Devices — 
               
               
Any  coin-operated gaming device as defined in section 4462 upon which a tax is  imposed by section 4461 and which has been forfeited under any provision  of this title shall be destroyed,
                  or otherwise disposed of, in such manner  as may be prescribed by the Secretary.
I.R.C. § 7326(b) Firearms — 
               
               
For  provisions relating to disposal of forfeited firearms, see section 5872(b).
