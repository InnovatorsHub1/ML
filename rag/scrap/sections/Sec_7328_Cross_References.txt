Sec. 7328. Cross References

I.R.C. § 7328(1)  — 
               
               
For the issuance of certificates of probable cause  relieving officers making seizures
                  of responsibility for damages, see 28  U. S. C. 2465.
I.R.C. § 7328(2)  — 
               
               
For provisions relating to forfeitures generally in  connection with alcohol taxes,
                  see chapter 51.
I.R.C. § 7328(3)  — 
               
               
For provisions relating to forfeitures generally in  connection with tobacco taxes,
                  see chapter 52.
I.R.C. § 7328(4)  — 
               
               
For provisions relating to forfeitures generally in  connection with taxes on certain
                  firearms, see chapter 53. 

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 871, 7329; renumbered  7328, Oct. 4, 1976, Pub. L. 94-455, title XIX, 1904(b)(8)(H)(i), 90 Stat.  1816.)
