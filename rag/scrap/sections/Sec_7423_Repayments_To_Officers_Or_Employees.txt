Sec. 7423. Repayments To Officers Or Employees

I.R.C. § 7423(1) Collections Recovered — 
               
               
To  any officer or employee of the United States the full amount of such sums  of
                  money as may be recovered against him in any court, for any internal  revenue taxes
                  collected by him, with the cost and expense of suit; also
I.R.C. § 7423(2) Damages And Costs — 
               
               
All  damages and costs recovered against any officer or employee of the United  States
                  in any suit brought against him by reason of anything done in the  due performance
                  of his official duty under this title. 

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 877; Oct. 4, 1976,  Pub. L. 94-455, title XIX, 1906(b)(13)(A), 90 Stat. 1834.)
