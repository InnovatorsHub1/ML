Sec. 7434. Civil Damages For Fraudulent Filing Of Information Returns

I.R.C. § 7434(a) In General — 
               
               
If  any person willfully files a fraudulent  information return with respect  to payments
                  purported to be made to any  other person, such other person  may bring a civil action
                  for damages  against the person so filing such  return.
I.R.C. § 7434(b) Damages — 
               
               
In  any action brought under subsection (a), upon a  finding of liability on  the part of the defendant, the defendant shall
                  be liable to the plaintiff  in an amount equal to the greater of $5,000  or the sum
                  of-- 

I.R.C. § 7434(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any  actual damages sustained by the plaintiff as a  proximate result of the  filing
                     of the fraudulent information  return (including any costs  attributable to resolving
                     deficiencies asserted as a result of such  filing), 


I.R.C. § 7434(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  costs of the action, and 


I.R.C. § 7434(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
in  the court's discretion, reasonable attorneys'  fees.
I.R.C. § 7434(c) Period For Bringing Action — 
               
               
Notwithstanding  any other  provision of law, an action to enforce the liability created
                  under this  section may be brought without regard to the amount in  controversy and
                  may be brought only within the later of-- 

I.R.C. § 7434(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
6  years after the date of the filing of the  fraudulent information return,  or 


I.R.C. § 7434(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
1  year after the date such fraudulent information  return would have been  discovered
                     by exercise of reasonable
                     care.
I.R.C. § 7434(d) Copy Of Complaint Filed With IRS — 
               
               
Any  person bringing an  action under subsection (a) shall provide a copy of
                  the complaint to  the Internal Revenue Service upon the filing of such  complaint
                  with the  court.
I.R.C. § 7434(e) Finding Of Court To Include Correct Amount Of Payment — 
               
               
The   decision of the court awarding damages in an action brought under subsection
                  (a) shall include a finding of the correct amount which  should  have been reported in
                  the information return.
I.R.C. § 7434(f) Information Return — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term  “information return” means any statement
                  described in section 6724(d)(1)(A).
