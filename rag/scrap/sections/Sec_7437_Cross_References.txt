Sec. 7437. Cross References

I.R.C. § 7437(1)  — 
               
               
For determination of amount of any tax, additions to  tax, etc., in title 11 cases,
                  see section 505 of title 11 of the United  States Code.
I.R.C. § 7437(2)  — 
               
               
For exclusion of tax liability from discharge in cases  under title 11 of the United
                  States Code, see section 523 of such title  11.
I.R.C. § 7437(3)  — 
               
               
For recognition of tax liens in cases under title 11 of  the United States Code, see
                  sections 545 and 724 of such title  11.
I.R.C. § 7437(4)  — 
               
               
For collection of taxes in connection with plans for  individuals with regular income
                  in cases under title 11 of the United  States Code, see section 1328 of such title
                  11.
I.R.C. § 7437(5)  — 
               
               
For provisions permitting the United States to be made  party defendant in a proceeding
                  in a State court for the foreclosure of a  lien upon real estate where the United
                  States may have claim upon the  premises involved, see section 2410 of Title 28 of
                  the United States  Code.
I.R.C. § 7437(6)  — 
               
               
For priority of lien of the United States in case of  insolvency, see section 3713(a)
                  of title 31, United States  Code.
I.R.C. § 7437(7)  — 
               
               
For interest on judgments for overpayments, see section  2411(a) of Title 28 of the
                  United States Code.
I.R.C. § 7437(8)  — 
               
               
For review of a Tax Court decision, see section 7482.
I.R.C. § 7437(9)  — 
               
               
For statute prohibiting suits to replevy property taken  under revenue laws, see section
                  2463 of Title 28 of the United States Code.
