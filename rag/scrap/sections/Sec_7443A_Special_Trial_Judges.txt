Sec. 7443A. Special Trial Judges

I.R.C. § 7443A(a) Appointment — 
               
               
The  chief judge may, from time to time, appoint special trial judges who shall  proceed
                  under such rules and regulations as may be promulgated by the Tax  Court.
I.R.C. § 7443A(b) Proceedings Which May Be Assigned To Special Trial Judges — 
               
               
The  chief judge may assign-- 

I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
any  declaratory judgment proceeding, 


I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
any  proceeding under section 7463, 


I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(3)  — 
                  
                  
any  proceeding where neither the amount of the deficiency placed in dispute  (within
                     the meaning of section 7463) nor the amount of any claimed
                     overpayment exceeds $50,000, 


I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(4)  — 
                  
                  
any proceeding under section 6320 or 6330, 


I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(5)  — 
                  
                  
any  proceeding under section 7436(c), 


I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(6)  — 
                  
                  
any  proceeding under section 7623(b)(4), and 


I.R.C. § 7443A(b)(7)  — 
                  
                  
any  other proceeding which the chief judge may designate, to be heard by the  special
                     trial judges of the court.
I.R.C. § 7443A(c) Authority To Make Court Decision — 
               
               
The  court may authorize a special trial judge to make the decision of the  court
                  with respect to any proceeding described in paragraph (1), (2), (3), (4), (5), or (6) of subsection (b), subject to such conditions and review  as the court may provide.
I.R.C. § 7443A(d) Salary — 
               
               
Each  special trial judge shall receive salary-- 

I.R.C. § 7443A(d)(1)  — 
                  
                  
at a  rate equal to 90 percent of the rate for judges of the Tax Court, and 


I.R.C. § 7443A(d)(2)  — 
                  
                  
in  the same installments as such judges.
I.R.C. § 7443A(e) Expenses For Travel And Subsistence — 
               
               
Subsection (d) of section 7443 shall apply to special trial judges subject to such  rules and regulations as may
                  be promulgated by the Tax Court.
