Sec. 7465. Provisions Of Special Application To Transferees

I.R.C. § 7465(1)  — 
               
               
For rules of burden of proof in transferee proceedings,  see section 6902(a).
I.R.C. § 7465(2)  — 
               
               
For authority of Tax Court to prescribe rules by which a  transferee of property of
                  a taxpayer shall be entitled to examine books,  records and other evidence, see section
                  6902(b). 

(Aug. 16, 1954, ch. 736, 68A Stat. 888, 7463; renumbered  7464, Dec. 30, 1969, Pub. L. 91-172, title IX, 957(a), 83 Stat. 733;  renumbered 7465, Dec. 24, 1980, Pub. L. 96-589, 6(c)(1), 94 Stat. 3407.)
