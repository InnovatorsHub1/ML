Sec. 7477. Declaratory Judgments Relating To Value Of Certain Gifts

I.R.C. § 7477(a)  Creation Of Remedy.-- — 
               
               
In  a case of an actual controversy involving a determination by the Secretary  of
                  the value of any gift shown on the return of tax imposed by chapter 12  or disclosed
                  on such return or in any statement attached to such return,  upon the filing of an
                  appropriate pleading, the Tax Court may make a  declaration of the value of such gift.
                  Any such declaration shall have the  force and effect of a decision of the Tax Court
                  and shall be reviewable as  such.
I.R.C. § 7477(b)  Limitations.--


I.R.C. § 7477(b)(1)  Petitioner.-- — 
                  
                  
A  pleading may be filed under this section only by the donor. 


I.R.C. § 7477(b)(2)  Exhaustion Of Administrative Remedies.-- — 
                  
                  
The  court shall not issue a declaratory judgment or decree under this section  in
                     any proceeding unless it determines that the petitioner has exhausted  all available
                     administrative remedies within the Internal Revenue  Service. 


I.R.C. § 7477(b)(3) Time For Bringing Action.-- — 
                  
                  
If the Secretary sends by certified or registered mail notice of his determination
                     as described in subsection (a) to the petitioner, no proceeding may be initiated under this section unless the pleading
                     is filed before the 91st day after the date of such mailing. 

(Added and amended by Pub. L. 105-34, title V Sec. 506(c)(1) 1997, 111 Stat 788.)
