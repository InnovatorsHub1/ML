Sec. 7610. Fees And Costs For Witnesses

I.R.C. § 7610(a) In General — 
               
               
The  Secretary shall by regulations establish the rates and conditions under  which
                  payment may be made of-- 

I.R.C. § 7610(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
fees  and mileage to persons who are summoned to appear before the Secretary,  and 


I.R.C. § 7610(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
reimbursement  for such costs that are reasonably necessary which have been directly
                     incurred in searching for, reproducing, or transporting books, papers,  records, or
                     other data required to be produced by summons.
I.R.C. § 7610(b) Exceptions — 
               
               
No  payment may be made under paragraph (2) of subsection (a) if-- 

I.R.C. § 7610(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the  person with respect to whose liability the summons is issued has a  proprietary
                     interest in the books, papers, records or other data required  to be produced, or 


I.R.C. § 7610(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the  person summoned is the person with respect to whose liability the summons  is
                     issued or an officer, employee, agent, accountant, or attorney of such  person who,
                     at the time the summons is served, is acting as such.
I.R.C. § 7610(c) Summons To Which Section Applies — 
               
               
This  section applies with respect to any summons authorized under section 6420(e)(2), 6421(g)(2), 6427(j)(2), or 7602.
