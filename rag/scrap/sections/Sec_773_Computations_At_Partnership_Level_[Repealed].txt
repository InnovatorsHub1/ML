Sec. 773. Computations At Partnership Level [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 773(a) General Rule — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 114-74, Sec. 1101(b)(1),
                           repealed Sec. 773 generally effective for returns filed for partnership
                           taxable years beginning after December 31, 2017; however, Act Sec.
                           1101(g)(4) provides that partnerships may elect the amendments made
                           by Act Sec. 1101 (other than the election under Code Sec. 6221(b))
                           to apply to any return of the partnership filed for partnership taxable
                           years beginning after Nov. 2, 2015, and before Jan. 1, 2018.




I.R.C. § 773(a)(1) Taxable Income — 
                  
                  
The  taxable income of an electing large partnership
                     shall be computed in the  same manner as in the case of an individual
                     except that— 

I.R.C. § 773(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  items described in section 772(a) shall be separately stated,
                        and 


I.R.C. § 773(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  modifications of subsection (b)
                        shall apply. 



I.R.C. § 773(a)(2) Elections — 
                  
                  
All  elections affecting the computation of the taxable
                     income of an electing  large partnership or the computation of any
                     credit of an electing large  partnership shall be made by the partnership;
                     except that the election  under section 901,
                     and any election under section 108,
                     shall be made by  each partner separately. 


I.R.C. § 773(a)(3) Limitations, Etc.


I.R.C. § 773(a)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Except  as provided in subparagraph (B), all limitations
                        and other provisions  affecting the computation of the taxable income
                        of an electing large  partnership or the computation of any credit
                        of an electing large  partnership shall be applied at the partnership
                        level (and not at the  partner level). 


I.R.C. § 773(a)(3)(B)  Certain Limitations Applied At Partner Level — 
                     
                     
The  following provisions shall be applied at the partner
                        level (and not at the  partnership level): 

I.R.C. § 773(a)(3)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
Section 68 (relating
                           to overall limitation on itemized  deductions). 


I.R.C. § 773(a)(3)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
Sections 49 and 465 (relating to at risk limitations). 


I.R.C. § 773(a)(3)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
Section 469 (relating to limitation on
                           passive activity losses and  credits). 


I.R.C. § 773(a)(3)(B)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
Any other provision specified in
                           regulations. 




I.R.C. § 773(a)(4) Coordination With Other Provisions — 
                  
                  
Paragraphs  (2) and (3) shall apply notwithstanding
                     any other provision of this  chapter other than this part.
I.R.C. § 773(b)  Modifications To Determination Of Taxable Income — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 114-74, Sec. 1101(b)(1),
                           repealed Sec. 773 generally effective for returns filed for partnership
                           taxable years beginning after December 31, 2017; however, Act Sec.
                           1101(g)(4) provides that partnerships may elect the amendments made
                           by Act Sec. 1101 (other than the election under Code Sec. 6221(b))
                           to apply to any return of the partnership filed for partnership taxable
                           years beginning after Nov. 2, 2015, and before Jan. 1, 2018.




In  determining the taxable income
                     of an electing large partnership—


I.R.C. § 773(b)(1)  Certain Deductions Not Allowed — 
                  
                  
The  following deductions shall not be allowed: 

I.R.C. § 773(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
The  deduction for personal exemptions
                        provided in section 151. 


I.R.C. § 773(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
The  net operating loss deduction provided
                        in section 172. 


I.R.C. § 773(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
The  additional itemized deductions
                        for individuals provided in part VII of  subchapter B (other than
                        section 212 thereof). 



I.R.C. § 773(b)(2) Charitable Deductions — 
                  
                  
In  determining the amount allowable under section 170, the limitation of  section 170(b)(2) shall apply. 


I.R.C. § 773(b)(3) Coordination With Section 67 — 
                  
                  
In lieu of applying section 67, 70 percent  of the amount of the
                     miscellaneous itemized deductions shall be  disallowed.
I.R.C. § 773(c) Special Rules For Income From Discharge Of Indebtedness — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 114-74, Sec. 1101(b)(1),
                           repealed Sec. 773 generally effective for returns filed for partnership
                           taxable years beginning after December 31, 2017; however, Act Sec.
                           1101(g)(4) provides that partnerships may elect the amendments made
                           by Act Sec. 1101 (other than the election under Code Sec. 6221(b))
                           to apply to any return of the partnership filed for partnership taxable
                           years beginning after Nov. 2, 2015, and before Jan. 1, 2018.




If  an electing large partnership
                     has income from the discharge of any  indebtedness—


I.R.C. § 773(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
such  income shall be excluded in determining
                     the amounts referred to in section  772(a),
                     and 


I.R.C. § 773(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
in  determining the income tax of any
                     partner of such partnership— 

I.R.C. § 773(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such  income shall be treated as an
                        item required to be separately taken into  account under section 772(a), and 


I.R.C. § 773(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the  provisions of section 108 shall be applied without regard
                        to this part.
