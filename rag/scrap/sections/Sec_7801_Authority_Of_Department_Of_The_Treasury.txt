Sec. 7801. Authority Of Department Of The Treasury

I.R.C. § 7801(a) Powers And Duties Of Secretary


I.R.C. § 7801(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Except as otherwise expressly provided by law, the administration
                     and  enforcement of this title shall be performed by or under the
                     supervision  of the Secretary of the Treasury. 


I.R.C. § 7801(a)(2) Administration And Enforcement Of Certain Provisions By Attorney
                     General


I.R.C. § 7801(a)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  administration and enforcement of the following
                        provisions of this title  shall be performed  by or under the supervision
                        of the Attorney General;  and the term “Secretary” or “Secretary
                        of the Treasury”  shall, when  applied to those provisions,
                        mean the Attorney General; and the term  “internal revenue officer”
                        shall, when applied to those provisions, mean  any officer of the
                        Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives   so designated
                        by the Attorney General: 

I.R.C. § 7801(a)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
Chapter 53. 


I.R.C. § 7801(a)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
Chapters 61 through 80, to the
                           extent such chapters   relate to the enforcement and administration
                           of the provisions referred to in clause (i). 



I.R.C. § 7801(a)(2)(B) Use Of Existing Rulings And Interpretations — 
                     
                     
Nothing  in the Homeland Security Act of 2002 alters
                        or repeals the rulings and interpretations of the  Bureau of Alcohol,
                        Tobacco, and Firearms in effect on the effective date of such Act,
                        which concern  the provisions of  this title referred to in subparagraph (A). The Attorney General
                        shall   consult with the Secretary to achieve uniformity and consistency
                        in  administering provisions  under chapter 53 of title 26, United
                        States  Code.
I.R.C. § 7801(b) [Repealed. Pub. L. 97-258, Sec. 5(b)]
I.R.C. § 7801(c) Functions Of Department Of Justice Unaffected — 
               
               
Nothing  in this section or section 301(f) of title
                  31 shall be considered to  affect the duties, powers, or functions
                  imposed upon, or vested in, the  Department of Justice, or any officer
                  thereof, by law existing on May 10,  1934.
