Sec. 833. Treatment Of Blue Cross And Blue Shield Organizations, Etc.

I.R.C. § 833(a) General Rule — 
               
               
In  the case of any organization to which this section applies— 

I.R.C. § 833(a)(1) Treated As Stock Company — 
                  
                  
Such  organization shall be taxable under this part in the same manner as if it  were
                     a stock insurance company. 


I.R.C. § 833(a)(2) Special Deduction Allowed — 
                  
                  
The  deduction determined under subsection (b) for any taxable year shall be  allowed. 


I.R.C. § 833(a)(3) Reductions In Unearned Premium Reserves Not To Apply — 
                  
                  
Subparagraph  (B) of paragraph (4) of section 832(b) shall be applied by substituting
                     “100 percent” for “80 percent”, and subparagraph (C) of such paragraph
                     (4)  shall not apply.
I.R.C. § 833(b) Amount Of Deduction


I.R.C. § 833(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
Except  as provided in paragraph (2), the deduction determined under this  subsection
                     for any taxable year is the excess
                     (if any) of— 

I.R.C. § 833(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
25 percent of the sum of— 

I.R.C. § 833(b)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the claims incurred during the taxable year and  liabilities incurred during the taxable
                           year under cost-plus contracts,  and 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the expenses incurred during the taxable year in  connection with the administration,
                           adjustment, or settlement of claims or  in connection with the administration of cost-plus
                           contracts,  over 



I.R.C. § 833(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
the adjusted surplus as of the beginning of the taxable  year. 



I.R.C. § 833(b)(2) Limitation — 
                  
                  
The  deduction determined under paragraph (1) for any taxable year shall not  exceed
                     taxable income for such taxable year
                     (determined without regard to  such deduction). 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3) Adjusted Surplus — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this subsection— 

I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  adjusted surplus as of the beginning of any taxable year is an amount  equal
                        to the adjusted surplus as of the beginning of the preceding taxable  year— 

I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
increased by the amount of any adjusted taxable income  for such preceding taxable
                           year, or 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
decreased by the amount of any adjusted net operating  loss for such preceding taxable
                           year. 



I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(B) Special Rule — 
                     
                     
The  adjusted surplus as of the beginning of the organization's 1st taxable  year
                        beginning after December 31, 1986, shall be its surplus as of such  time. For purposes
                        of the preceding sentence and subsection (c)(3)(C), the  term “surplus” means the
                        excess of the total assets over total liabilities  as shown on the annual statement. 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(C) Adjusted Taxable Income — 
                     
                     
The  term “adjusted taxable income” means taxable income determined— 

I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(C)(i)  — 
                        
                        
without regard to the deduction determined under this  subsection, 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(C)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
without regard to any carryforward or carryback to such  taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(C)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
by increasing gross income by an amount equal to the net  exempt income for the taxable
                           year. 



I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(D) Adjusted Net Operating Loss — 
                     
                     
The  term “adjusted net operating loss” means the net operating loss for any  taxable
                        year determined with the adjustments set forth in subparagraph  (C). 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(E) Net Exempt Income — 
                     
                     
The  term “net exempt income” means— 

I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(E)(i)  — 
                        
                        
any tax-exempt interest received or accrued during the  taxable year, reduced by any
                           amount (not otherwise deductible) which would  have been allowable as a deduction
                           for the taxable year if such interest  were not tax-exempt, and 


I.R.C. § 833(b)(3)(E)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the aggregate amount allowed as a deduction for the  taxable year under sections 243 and 245. 


The amount determined under clause (ii) shall be reduced
                           by the amount of any decrease in deductions allowable for the taxable year  by reason
                           of section 832(b)(5)(B) to the extent such decrease is  attributable to deductions under sections 243 and 245.




I.R.C. § 833(b)(4) Only Health-Related Items Taken Into Account — 
                  
                  
Any  determination under this subsection shall be made by only taking into  account
                     items attributable to the health-related business of the taxpayer.
I.R.C. § 833(c) Organizations To Which Section Applies


I.R.C. § 833(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
This  section shall apply to— 

I.R.C. § 833(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any existing Blue Cross or Blue Shield organization, and 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any other organization meeting the requirements of  paragraph (3). 



I.R.C. § 833(c)(2) Existing Blue Cross Or Blue Shield Organization — 
                  
                  
The  term “existing Blue Cross or Blue Shield organization"
                     means any Blue  Cross or Blue Shield organization if— 

I.R.C. § 833(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
such organization was in existence on August 16, 1986, 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
such organization is determined to be exempt from tax  for its last taxable year beginning
                        before January 1, 1987, and 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
no material change has occurred in the operations of  such organization or in its
                        structure after August 16, 1986, and before  the close of the taxable year. 


To the extent permitted by the Secretary, any successor
                        to an organization meeting the requirements of the preceding sentence, and  any organization
                        resulting from the merger or consolidation of
                        organizations each of which met such requirements, shall be treated as an  existing
                        Blue Cross or Blue Shield organization.



I.R.C. § 833(c)(3) Other Organizations


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
An  organization meets the requirements of this paragraph for any taxable year  if— 

I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
substantially all the activities of such organization  involve the providing of health
                           insurance, 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
at least 10 percent of the health insurance provided by  such organization is provided
                           to individuals and small groups (not taking  into account any medicare supplemental
                           coverage), 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
such organization provides continuous full-year open  enrollment (including conversions)
                           for individuals and small groups, 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
such organization's policies covering individuals  provide full coverage of pre-existing
                           conditions of high-risk individuals  without a price differential (with a reasonable
                           waiting period), and  coverage is provided without regard to age, income, or employment
                           status  of individuals under age 65, 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A)(v)  — 
                        
                        
at least 35 percent of its premiums are determined on a  community rated basis, and 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(A)(vi)  — 
                        
                        
no part of its net earnings inures to the benefit of any  private shareholder or individual. 



I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(B) Small Group Defined — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subparagraph (A), the term “small group"
                        means the lesser of— 

I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
15 individuals, or 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the number of individuals required for a small group  under applicable State law. 



I.R.C. § 833(c)(3)(C) Special Rule For Determining Adjusted Surplus — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of subsection (b), the adjusted surplus of any organization  meeting
                        the requirements of this paragraph as of the beginning of the 1st  taxable year for
                        which it meets such requirements shall be its surplus as  of such time. 



I.R.C. § 833(c)(4) Treatment As Existing Blue Cross Or Blue Shield Organization


I.R.C. § 833(c)(4)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
Paragraph  (2) shall be applied to an organization described in subparagraph (B) as
                        if it were a Blue Cross or Blue Shield organization. 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(4)(B) Applicable Organization — 
                     
                     
An  organization is described in this subparagraph if it— 

I.R.C. § 833(c)(4)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
is organized under, and governed by, State laws which  are specifically and exclusively
                           applicable to not- for-profit health  insurance or health service type organizations,
                           and 


I.R.C. § 833(c)(4)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
is not a Blue Cross or Blue Shield organization or  health maintenance organization. 




I.R.C. § 833(c)(5) Nonapplication Of Section In Case Of Low Medical Loss Ratio — 
                  
                  
Notwithstanding the preceding paragraphs, paragraphs
                     (2) and (3) of subsection (a) shall not apply to any organization unless such organization's
                     percentage of total premium revenue expended on reimbursement for clinical services
                     and for activities that improve health  care quality provided to enrollees under its
                     policies during such taxable year (as reported under section 2718 of the Public Health
                     Service Act) is not less than 85 percent.
