Sec. 859. Adoption Of Annual Accounting Period

I.R.C. § 859(a) General Rule — 
               
               
For purposes of this subtitle— 

I.R.C. § 859(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
a real estate investment trust shall not change to any accounting period other than
                     the calendar year, and 


I.R.C. § 859(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
a corporation, trust, or association may not elect to be a real estate investment
                     trust for any taxable year beginning after October 4, 1976, unless its accounting
                     period is the calendar year. 


Paragraph (2) shall not apply to a corporation, trust, or association which was considered
                     to be a real estate investment trust for any taxable year beginning on or before October
                     4, 1976.
I.R.C. § 859(b) Change Of Accounting Period Without Approval — 
               
               
Notwithstanding section
                  442
                  , an entity which has not engaged in any active trade or business may change its accounting
                  period to a calendar year without the approval of the Secretary if such change is
                  in connection with an election under section
                  856(c)
                  .
