Sec. 85. Unemployment Compensation

I.R.C. § 85(a) General Rule — 
               
               
In  the case of an individual, gross income includes unemployment  compensation.
I.R.C. § 85(b) Unemployment Compensation Defined — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section, the term “unemployment compensation” means any  amount
                  received under a law of the United States or of a State which is in  the nature of
                  unemployment compensation.
I.R.C. § 85(c) Special Rule For 2020


I.R.C. § 85(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In the case of any taxable year beginning in 2020, if the adjusted gross income of
                     the taxpayer for such taxable year is less than $150,000, the gross income of such
                     taxpayer shall not include so much of the unemployment compensation received by such
                     taxpayer
                     (or, in the case of a joint return, received by each spouse) as does not exceed $10,200. 


I.R.C. § 85(c)(2) Application — 
                  
                  
For purposes of paragraph (1), the adjusted gross income of the taxpayer shall be determined— 

I.R.C. § 85(c)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
after application of sections 86, 135, 137, 219, 221, 222, and 469, and 


I.R.C. § 85(c)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
without regard to this section.
