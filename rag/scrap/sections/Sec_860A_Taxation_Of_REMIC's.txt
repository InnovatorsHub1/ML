Sec. 860A. Taxation Of REMIC's

I.R.C. § 860A(a) General Rule — 
               
               
Except  as otherwise provided in this part, a REMIC shall not be subject to  taxation
                  under this subtitle (and shall not be treated as a corporation,  partnership, or trust
                  for purposes of this subtitle).
I.R.C. § 860A(b) Income Taxable To Holders — 
               
               
The  income of any REMIC shall be taxable to the holders of interests in such  REMIC
                  as provided in this part.
