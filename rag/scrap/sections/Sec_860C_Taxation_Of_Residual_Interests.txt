Sec. 860C. Taxation Of Residual Interests

I.R.C. § 860C(a) Pass-Thru Of Income Or Loss


I.R.C. § 860C(a)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
In  determining the tax under this chapter of any holder of a residual  interest in
                     a REMIC, such holder shall take into account his daily portion  of the taxable income
                     or net loss of such REMIC for each day during the  taxable year on which such holder
                     held such interest. 


I.R.C. § 860C(a)(2) Daily Portion — 
                  
                  
The  daily portion referred to in paragraph (1) shall be determined— 

I.R.C. § 860C(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
by allocating to each day in any calendar quarter its  ratable portion of the taxable
                        income (or net loss) for such quarter, and 


I.R.C. § 860C(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
by allocating the amount so allocated to any day among  the holders (on such day)
                        of residual interests in proportion to their  respective holdings on such day.
I.R.C. § 860C(b) Determination Of Taxable Income Or Net Loss — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 860C(b)(1) Taxable Income — 
                  
                  
The  taxable income of a REMIC shall be determined under an accrual method of  accounting
                     and, except as provided in regulations, in the same manner as  in the case of an individual,
                     except that— 

I.R.C. § 860C(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
regular interests in such REMIC
                        (if not otherwise debt  instruments) shall be treated as indebtedness of such REMIC, 


I.R.C. § 860C(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
market discount on any market discount bond shall be  included in gross income for
                        the taxable years to which it is attributable  as determined under the rules of section
                        1276(b)(2) (and sections 1276(a)  and 1277 shall not apply), 


I.R.C. § 860C(b)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
there shall not be taken into account any item of  income, gain, loss, or deduction
                        allocable to a prohibited transaction, 


I.R.C. § 860C(b)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
the deductions referred to in section 703(a)(2) (other  than any deduction under section 212)
                        shall not be allowed, and 


I.R.C. § 860C(b)(1)(E)  — 
                     
                     
the amount of the net income from foreclosure property  (if any) shall be reduced
                        by the amount of the tax imposed by section  860G(c). 



I.R.C. § 860C(b)(2) Net Loss — 
                  
                  
The  net loss of any REMIC is the excess of— 

I.R.C. § 860C(b)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the deductions allowable in computing the taxable income  of such REMIC, over 


I.R.C. § 860C(b)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
its gross income. 


Such amount shall be determined with the modifications
                        set forth in paragraph (1).
I.R.C. § 860C(c) Distributions — 
               
               
Any  distribution by a REMIC— 

I.R.C. § 860C(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
shall not be included in gross income to the extent it  does not exceed the adjusted
                     basis of the interest, and 


I.R.C. § 860C(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
to the extent it exceeds the adjusted basis of the  interest, shall be treated as
                     gain from the sale or exchange of such  interest.
I.R.C. § 860C(d) Basis Rules


I.R.C. § 860C(d)(1) Increase In Basis — 
                  
                  
The  basis of any person's residual interest in a REMIC shall be increased by  the
                     amount of the taxable income of such REMIC taken into account under  subsection (a)
                     by such person with respect to such interest. 


I.R.C. § 860C(d)(2) Decreases In Basis — 
                  
                  
The  basis of any person's residual interest in a REMIC shall be decreased (but  not
                     below zero) by the sum of the following amounts: 

I.R.C. § 860C(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
any distributions to such person with respect to such  interest, and 


I.R.C. § 860C(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any net loss of such REMIC taken into account under  subsection (a) by such person
                        with respect to such interest.
I.R.C. § 860C(e) Special Rules


I.R.C. § 860C(e)(1) Amounts Treated As Ordinary — 
                  
                  
Any  amount taken into account under subsection (a)
                     by any holder of a residual  interest in a REMIC shall be treated as ordinary income
                     or ordinary loss,  as the case may be. 


I.R.C. § 860C(e)(2) Limitation On Losses


I.R.C. § 860C(e)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  amount of the net loss of any REMIC taken into account by a holder under  subsection
                        (a) with respect to any calendar quarter shall not exceed the  adjusted basis of such
                        holder's residual interest in such REMIC as of the  close of such calendar quarter
                        (determined without regard to the  adjustment under subsection (d)(2)(B) for such
                        calendar quarter). 


I.R.C. § 860C(e)(2)(B) Indefinite Carryforward — 
                     
                     
Any  loss disallowed by reason of subparagraph (A) shall be treated as incurred  by
                        the REMIC in the succeeding calendar quarter with respect to such  holder. 



I.R.C. § 860C(e)(3) Cross Reference — 
                  
                  
For  special treatment of income in excess of daily accruals, see section 860E.
