Sec. 860D. REMIC Defined

I.R.C. § 860D(a) General Rule — 
               
               
For  purposes of this title, the terms “real estate mortgage investment  conduit”
                  and “REMIC” mean any entity— 

I.R.C. § 860D(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
to which an election to be treated as a REMIC applies  for the taxable year and all
                     prior taxable years, 


I.R.C. § 860D(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
all of the interests in which are regular interests or  residual interests, 


I.R.C. § 860D(a)(3)  — 
                  
                  
which has 1 (and only 1) class of residual interests  (and all distributions, if any,
                     with respect to such interests are pro  rata), 


I.R.C. § 860D(a)(4)  — 
                  
                  
as of the close of the 3rd month beginning after the  startup day and at all times
                     thereafter, substantially all of the assets  of which consist of qualified mortgages
                     and permitted investments, 


I.R.C. § 860D(a)(5)  — 
                  
                  
which has a taxable year which is a calendar year, and 


I.R.C. § 860D(a)(6)  — 
                  
                  
with respect to which there are reasonable arrangements  designed to ensure that— 

I.R.C. § 860D(a)(6)(A)  — 
                     
                     
residual interests in such entity are not held by  disqualified organizations (as
                        defined in section 860E(e)(5)), and 


I.R.C. § 860D(a)(6)(B)  — 
                     
                     
information necessary for the application of section  860E(e) will be made available by the entity. 


In the case of a qualified liquidation (as defined in
                        section 860F(a)(4)(A)), paragraph (4) shall not apply during the  liquidation period (as defined in section
                        860F(a)(4)(B)).
I.R.C. § 860D(b) Election


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
An  entity (otherwise meeting the requirements of subsection
                     (a)) may elect to  be treated as a REMIC for its 1st taxable year. Such an election
                     shall be  made on its return for such 1st taxable year. Except as provided in  paragraph
                     (2), such an election shall apply to the taxable year for which  made and all subsequent
                     taxable years. 


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2) Termination


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
If  any entity ceases to be a REMIC at any time during the taxable year, such  entity
                        shall not be treated as a REMIC for such taxable year or any  succeeding taxable year. 


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2)(B) Inadvertent Terminations — 
                     
                     
If— 

I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
an entity ceases to be a REMIC, 


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
the Secretary determines that such cessation was  inadvertent, 


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2)(B)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
no later than a reasonable time after the discovery of  the event resulting in such
                           cessation, steps are taken so that such entity  is once more a REMIC, and 


I.R.C. § 860D(b)(2)(B)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
such entity, and each person holding an interest in such  entity at any time during
                           the period specified pursuant to this  subsection, agrees to make such adjustments
                           (consistent with the treatment  of such entity as a REMIC or a C corporation)
                           as may be required by the  Secretary with respect to such period, then, notwithstanding
                           such  terminating event, such entity shall be treated as continuing to be a  REMIC
                           (or such cessation shall be disregarded for purposes of subparagraph  (A)) whichever
                           the Secretary determines to be appropriate.
