Sec. 860I. Gain Recognition On Contributions To A FASIT And In Other Cases [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 860I(a) Treatment Of Property Acquired By FASIT


I.R.C. § 860I(a)(1) Property Acquired From Holder Of Ownership Interest Or Related Person — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




If property is sold or contributed to a FASIT by the  holder of the ownership interest
                        in such FASIT
                        (or by a related person)  gain (if any) shall be recognized to such holder (or person)
                        in an amount  equal to the excess (if any) of such property's value under subsection
                        (d)  on the date of such sale or contribution over its adjusted basis on such  date.



I.R.C. § 860I(a)(2) Property Acquired Other Than From Holder Of Ownership Interest Or Related Person — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




Property which is acquired by a FASIT other than in a  transaction to which paragraph
                        (1) applies shall be treated—


I.R.C. § 860I(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
as having been acquired by the holder of the ownership  interest in the FASIT for
                        an amount equal to the FASIT's cost of acquiring  such property, and 


I.R.C. § 860I(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
as having been sold by such holder to the FASIT at its  value under subsection (d)
                        on such  date.
I.R.C. § 860I(b) Gain Recognition On Property Outside FASIT Which Supports Regular Interests — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                           the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




If property held by the holder of the ownership interest  in a FASIT (or by any person
                     related to such holder) supports any regular  interest in such FASIT—


I.R.C. § 860I(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
gain shall be recognized to such holder (or person) in  the same manner as if such
                     holder (or person)
                     had sold such property at  its value under subsection (d) on the earliest date such
                     property supports  such an interest, and 


I.R.C. § 860I(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
such property shall be treated as held by such FASIT  for purposes of this part.
I.R.C. § 860I(c) Deferral Of Gain Recognition — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                           the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




The Secretary may prescribe regulations which—


I.R.C. § 860I(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
provide that gain otherwise recognized under subsection  (a) or (b) shall not be recognized
                     before the earliest date on which such  property supports any regular interest in
                     such FASIT or any indebtedness  of the holder of the ownership interest
                     (or of any person related to such  holder), and 


I.R.C. § 860I(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
provide such adjustments to the other provisions of  this part to the extent appropriate
                     in the context of the treatment  provided under paragraph (1).
I.R.C. § 860I(d) Valuation — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 860I(d)(1) In General — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




The value of any property under this subsection shall  be—


I.R.C. § 860I(d)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of a debt instrument which is not traded on  an established securities
                        market, the sum of the present values of the  reasonably expected payments under such
                        instrument determined (in the  manner provided by regulations prescribed by the Secretary)-- 

I.R.C. § 860I(d)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
as of the date of the event resulting in the gain  recognition under this section,
                           and 


I.R.C. § 860I(d)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
by using a discount rate equal to 120 percent of the  applicable Federal rate (as
                           defined in section 1274(d)), or such other  discount rate specified in such regulations, compounded semiannually,
                           and 



I.R.C. § 860I(d)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
in the case of any other property, its fair market  value. 



I.R.C. § 860I(d)(2) Special Rule For Revolving Loan Accounts — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




For purposes of paragraph (1)—


I.R.C. § 860I(d)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
each extension of credit (other than the accrual of  interest) on a revolving loan
                        account shall be treated as a separate debt  instrument, and 


I.R.C. § 860I(d)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
payments on such extensions of credit having  substantially the same terms shall be
                        applied to such extensions beginning  with the earliest such extension.
I.R.C. § 860I(e) Special Rules

I.R.C. § 860I(e)(2) Basis Adjustments — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860I, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
