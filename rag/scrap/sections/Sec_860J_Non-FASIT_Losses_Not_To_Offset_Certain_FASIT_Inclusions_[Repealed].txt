Sec. 860J. Non-FASIT Losses Not To Offset Certain FASIT Inclusions [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 860J(a) In General — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860J, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                           the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




The taxable income of the holder of the ownership  interest or any high-yield interest
                     in a FASIT for any taxable year shall  in no event be less than the sum of—


I.R.C. § 860J(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
such holder's taxable income determined solely with  respect to such interests (including
                     gains and losses from sales and  exchanges of such interests), and 


I.R.C. § 860J(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the excess inclusion (if any) under section 860E(a)(1) 
                     for such taxable year.
I.R.C. § 860J(b) Coordination With Section 172 — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860J, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                           the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




Any increase in the taxable income of any holder of the  ownership interest or a high-yield
                     interest in a FASIT for any taxable  year by reason of subsection (a) shall be disregarded—


I.R.C. § 860J(b)(1)  — 
                  
                  
in determining under section 172 the amount of any net  operating loss for such taxable year, and 


I.R.C. § 860J(b)(2)  — 
                  
                  
in determining taxable income for such taxable year for  purposes of the 2nd sentence
                     of section  172(b)(2).
I.R.C. § 860J(c) Coordination With Minimum Tax — 
               
               




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860J, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                           the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




For purposes of part VI of subchapter A of this chapter—


I.R.C. § 860J(c)(1)  — 
                  
                  
the reference in section 55(b)(2) to taxable income
                     shall be treated as a reference to taxable income determined without
                     regard to this section, 


I.R.C. § 860J(c)(2)  — 
                  
                  
the alternative minimum taxable income of any holder of  the ownership interest or
                     a high-yield interest in a FASIT for any taxable  year shall in no event be less than
                     such holder's taxable income  determined solely with respect to such interests, and 


I.R.C. § 860J(c)(3)  — 
                  
                  
any increase in taxable income under this section shall  be disregarded for purposes
                     of computing the alternative tax net operating  loss deduction.
