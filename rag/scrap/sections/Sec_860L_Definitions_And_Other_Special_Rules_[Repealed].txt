Sec. 860L. Definitions And Other Special Rules [Repealed]

I.R.C. § 860L(a)  FASIT


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(1)  In General — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




For purposes of this title, the terms “financial asset  securitization investment
                        trust”
                        and “FASIT” mean any entity—


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
for which an election to be treated as a FASIT applies  for the taxable year, 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
all of the interests in which are regular interests or  the ownership interest, 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(1)(C)  — 
                     
                     
which has only 1 ownership interest and such ownership  interest is held directly
                        by an eligible corporation, 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
as of the close of the 3rd month beginning after the  day of its formation and at
                        all times thereafter, substantially all of the  assets of which (including assets
                        treated as held by the entity under  section 860I(b)(2)) consist of permitted assets, and 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(1)(E)  — 
                     
                     
which is not described in section 851(a). 

A rule similar to the rule of the  last sentence of section 860D(a) shall apply for purposes of this  paragraph.




I.R.C. § 860L(a)(2)  Eligible Corporation — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




For purposes of paragraph (1)(C), the term “eligible  corporation” means any domestic
                        C corporation other than—


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(2)(A)  — 
                     
                     
a corporation which is exempt from, or is not subject  to, tax under this chapter, 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(2)(B)  — 
                     
                     
an entity described in section 851(a) or  856(a), 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(2)(C)  — 
                     
                     
a REMIC, and 


I.R.C. § 860L(a)(2)(D)  — 
                     
                     
an organization to which part I of subchapter T  applies. 



I.R.C. § 860L(a)(3)  Election — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




An entity (otherwise meeting the requirements of  paragraph (1)) may elect to be treated
                        as a FASIT. Except as provided in  paragraph (5), such an election shall apply to
                        the taxable year for which  made and all subsequent taxable years unless revoked with
                        the consent of  the Secretary.



I.R.C. § 860L(a)(4)  Termination — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




If any entity ceases to be a FASIT at any time during  the taxable year, such entity
                        shall not be treated as a FASIT after the  date of such cessation.



I.R.C. § 860L(a)(5)  Inadvertent Terminations, Etc — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




Rules similar to the rules of section 860D(b)(2)(B)  shall apply to inadvertent failures to qualify or remain qualified as a
                        FASIT.



I.R.C. § 860L(a)(6)  Permitted Assets Not Treated As Interest In FASIT — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




Except as provided in regulations prescribed by the  Secretary, any asset which is
                        a permitted asset at the time acquired by a  FASIT shall not be treated at any time
                        as an interest in such  FASIT.

For purposes of this part—


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)  Regular Interest


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(A)  In General — 
                     
                     




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                                 the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




The term “regular interest”
                           means any interest which is  issued by a FASIT on or after the startup date with fixed
                           terms and which  is designated as a regular interest if—


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
such interest unconditionally entitles the holder to  receive a specified principal
                           amount (or other similar
                           amount), 


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
interest payments (or other similar amounts), if any,  with respect to such interest
                           are determined based on a fixed rate, or,  except as otherwise provided by the Secretary,
                           at a variable rate  permitted under section 860G(a)(1)(B)(i), 


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
such interest does not have a stated maturity  (including options to renew) greater
                           than 30 years (or such longer period  as may be permitted by regulations), 


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(A)(iv)  — 
                        
                        
the issue price of such interest does not exceed 125  percent of its stated principal
                           amount, and 


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(A)(v)  — 
                        
                        
the yield to maturity on such interest is less than the  sum determined under section
                           163(i)(1)(B) with respect to such interest. 


An interest shall not fail to meet
                           the requirements of clause (i) merely because the timing (but not the  amount) of
                           the principal payments (or other similar amounts)
                           may be  contingent on the extent that payments on debt instruments held by the  FASIT
                           are made in advance of anticipated payments and on the amount of  income from permitted
                           assets.



I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(B)  High-Yield Interests


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(B)(i)  In General — 
                        
                        




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                                    the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].

                              includes any high-yield  interest.



I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(B)(ii)  High-Yield Interest — 
                        
                        




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                                    the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




The term ‘high-yield interest’ means any interest which  would be described in subparagraph
                              (A) but for—


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(B)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
failing to meet the requirements of one or more of  clauses (i), (iv), or (v) thereof,
                              or 


I.R.C. § 860L(b)(1)(B)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
failing to meet the requirement of clause (ii) thereof  but only if interest payments
                              (or other similar amounts), if any, with  respect to such interest consist of a specified
                              portion of the interest  payments on permitted assets and such portion does not vary
                              during the  period such interest is  outstanding. 





I.R.C. § 860L(b)(2)  Ownership Interest — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on

The term ‘ownership interest’ means the interest issued  by a FASIT after the startup
                        day which is designated as an ownership  interest and which is not a regular interest.
I.R.C. § 860L(c)  Permitted Assets — 
               
               




Editor's Note:

I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)  In General — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




The term “permitted asset”
                        means--


I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
cash or cash equivalents, 


I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any debt instrument (as defined in section 1275(a)(1))
                        under which interest payments (or other similar amounts), if any, at or  before maturity
                        meet the requirements applicable under clause
                        (i) or (ii)  of section 860G(a)(1)(B),

foreclosure property, 


I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(D)  — 
                     
                     
any asset— 

I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(D)(i)  —

                           of credit, insurance, guarantee against payment  defaults, or other similar instrument
                           permitted by the Secretary,  and 


I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(D)(ii)  —

                           with being the obligor on interests  issued by the FASIT, 



I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(E)  —

                        described in subparagraph (D), 


I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(F)  — 
                     
                     
any regular interest in another FASIT, and 


I.R.C. § 860L(c)(1)(G)  — 
                     
                     
any regular interest in a  REMIC.

Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on

                        shall not include any debt  instrument issued by the holder of the ownership interest
                        in the FASIT or  by any person related to such holder or any direct or indirect interest
                        in  such a debt instrument. The preceding sentence shall not apply to cash  equivalents
                        and to any other investment specified in regulations  prescribed by the Secretary.

I.R.C. § 860L(e)  Tax On Prohibited Transactions


I.R.C. § 860L(e)(1)  In General — 
                  
                  




Editor's Note: 
Pub. L. 108-357, Sec. 835(a), repealed Sec. 860L, effective January 1, 2005, except for FASITs in existence on
                              the date of the enactment of this Act [Enacted: Oct. 22, 2004].




There is hereby imposed for each taxable year of a FASIT  a tax equal to 100 percent
                        of the net income derived from prohibited  transactions. Such tax shall be paid by
                        the holder of the ownership  interest in the FASIT.

                        transaction” means—

                        (3), the receipt of any  income derived from any asset that is not a permitted asset,
