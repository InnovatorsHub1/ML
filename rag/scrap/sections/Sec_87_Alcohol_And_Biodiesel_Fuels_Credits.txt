Sec. 87. Alcohol And Biodiesel Fuels Credits

I.R.C. § 87(1)  — 
               
               
the amount of the alcohol fuel credit determined with respect to the taxpayer for
                  the taxable year under section 40(a),
I.R.C. § 87(2)  — 
               
               
the biodiesel fuels credit determined with respect to the taxpayer for the taxable
                  year under section 40A(a), and
I.R.C. § 87(3)  — 
               
               
the sustainable aviation fuel credit determined with respect to the taxpayer for the
                  taxable year under section 40B(a).
