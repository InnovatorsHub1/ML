Sec. 9701. Definitions Of General Applicability

I.R.C. § 9701(a) Plans And Funds — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter— 

I.R.C. § 9701(a)(1) UMWA Benefit Plan


I.R.C. § 9701(a)(1)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  term “UMWA Benefit Plan” means a plan— 

I.R.C. § 9701(a)(1)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
which  is described in section 404(c), or a continuation thereof;
                           and 


I.R.C. § 9701(a)(1)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
which  provides health benefits to retirees and beneficiaries of the industry  which
                           maintained the 1950 UMWA Pension Plan. 



I.R.C. § 9701(a)(1)(B) 1950 UMWA Benefit Plan — 
                     
                     
The  term “1950 UMWA Benefit Plan” means a UMWA Benefit Plan, participation in which
                        is substantially limited to individuals who retired before 1976. 


I.R.C. § 9701(a)(1)(C) 1974 UMWA Benefit Plan — 
                     
                     
The  term “1974 UMWA Benefit Plan” means a UMWA Benefit Plan, participation in  which
                        is substantially limited to individuals who retired on or after  January 1, 1976. 



I.R.C. § 9701(a)(2) 1950 UMWA Pension Plan — 
                  
                  
The  term “1950 UMWA Pension Plan” means a pension plan described in section  404(c) (or a continuation thereof), participation in which is  substantially limited to
                     individuals who retired before 1976. 


I.R.C. § 9701(a)(3) 1974 UMWA Pension Plan — 
                  
                  
The  term “1974 UMWA Pension Plan” means a pension plan described in section  404(c) (or a continuation thereof), participation in which is  substantially limited to
                     individuals who retired in 1976 and thereafter. 


I.R.C. § 9701(a)(4) 1992 UMWA Benefit Plan — 
                  
                  
The  term “1992 UMWA Benefit Plan” means the plan referred to in section 9712. 


I.R.C. § 9701(a)(5) Combined Fund — 
                  
                  
The  term “Combined Fund” means the United Mine Workers of America Combined  Benefit
                     Fund established under section 9702.
I.R.C. § 9701(b) Agreements — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 9701(b)(1) Coal Wage Agreement — 
                  
                  
The  term “coal wage agreement” means— 

I.R.C. § 9701(b)(1)(A)  — 
                     
                     
the  National Bituminous Coal Wage Agreement, or 


I.R.C. § 9701(b)(1)(B)  — 
                     
                     
any  other agreement entered into between an employer in the coal industry and  the
                        United Mine Workers of America that required or requires one or both  of the following: 

I.R.C. § 9701(b)(1)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
the  provision of health benefits to retirees of such employer, eligibility for  which
                           is based on years of service credited under a plan established by  the settlors and
                           described in section 404(c) or a continuation of such  plan; or 


I.R.C. § 9701(b)(1)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
contributions  to the 1950 UMWA Benefit Plan or the 1974 UMWA Benefit Plan, or any
                           predecessor thereof. 




I.R.C. § 9701(b)(2) Settlors — 
                  
                  
The  term “settlors” means the United Mine Workers of America and the  Bituminous
                     Coal Operators' Association, Inc. (referred to in this chapter  as the “BCOA”). 


I.R.C. § 9701(b)(3) National Bituminous Coal Wage Agreement — 
                  
                  
The  term “National Bituminous Coal Wage Agreement"
                     means a collective  bargaining agreement negotiated by the BCOA and the United Mine
                     Workers of  America.
I.R.C. § 9701(c) Terms Relating To Operators — 
               
               
For  purposes of this section— 

I.R.C. § 9701(c)(1) Signatory Operator — 
                  
                  
The  term “signatory operator” means a person which is or was a signatory to a  coal
                     wage agreement. 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(2) Related Persons


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
A  person shall be considered to be a related person to a signatory operator  if that
                        person is— 

I.R.C. § 9701(c)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
a member of the controlled group of corporations (within the meaning of  section 52(a)) which includes such signatory operator; 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
a trade or business which is under common control (as determined under  section 52(b)) with such signatory operator;
                           or 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(2)(A)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
any  other person who is identified as having a partnership interest or joint  venture
                           with a signatory operator in a business within the coal industry, but only if such
                           business employed eligible beneficiaries, except that  this clause shall not apply
                           to a person whose only interest is as a  limited partner. 


A related person shall also include a successor in 
                           interest of any person described in clause (i), (ii), or (iii).



I.R.C. § 9701(c)(2)(B) Time For Determination — 
                     
                     
The  relationships described in clauses (i), (ii), and (iii) of subparagraph  (A) shall be determined as of July 20, 1992, except that if, on July 20,  1992, a signatory
                        operator is no longer in business, the relationships  shall be determined as of the
                        time immediately before such operator ceased  to be in business. 



I.R.C. § 9701(c)(3) 1988 Agreement Operator — 
                  
                  
The  term “1988 agreement operator” means— 

I.R.C. § 9701(c)(3)(A)  — 
                     
                     
a  signatory operator which was a signatory to the 1988 National Bituminous  Coal
                        Wage Agreement, 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(3)(B)  — 
                     
                     
an  employer in the coal industry which was a signatory to an agreement  containing
                        pension and health care contribution and benefit provisions  which are the same as
                        those contained in the 1988 National Bituminous Coal  Wage Agreement, or 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(3)(C)  — 
                     
                     
an  employer from which contributions were actually received after 1987 and  before
                        July 20, 1992, by the 1950 UMWA Benefit Plan or the 1974 UMWA  Benefit Plan in connection
                        with employment in the coal industry during the  period covered by the 1988 National
                        Bituminous Coal Wage Agreement. 



I.R.C. § 9701(c)(4) Last Signatory Operator — 
                  
                  
The  term “last signatory operator” means, with respect to a coal industry  retiree,
                     a signatory operator which was the most recent coal industry  employer of such retiree. 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(5) Assigned Operator — 
                  
                  
The  term “assigned operator” means, with respect to an eligible beneficiary  defined
                     in section 9703(f), the signatory operator to which liability  under subchapter B with respect to the
                     beneficiary is assigned under  section 9706. 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(6) Operators Of Dependent Beneficiaries — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this chapter, the signatory operator, last signatory operator,  or
                     assigned operator of any eligible beneficiary under this chapter who is  a coal industry
                     retiree shall be considered to be the signatory operator,  last signatory operator,
                     or assigned operator with respect to any other  individual who is an eligible beneficiary
                     under this chapter by reason of  a relationship to the retiree. 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(7) Business — 
                  
                  
For  purposes of this chapter, a person shall be considered to be in business  if
                     such person conducts or derives revenue from any business activity,  whether or not
                     in the coal industry. 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(8) Successor In Interest



I.R.C. § 9701(c)(A) Safe Harbor — 
                  
                  
The  term “successor in interest” shall not include any person who— 

I.R.C. § 9701(c)(A)(i)  — 
                     
                     
is  an unrelated person to an eligible seller described in subparagraph (C);
                        and 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(A)(ii)  — 
                     
                     
purchases for fair market value assets, or all of the stock, of a related  person
                        to such seller, in a bona fide, arm's-length sale. 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(A)(B) Unrelated Person — 
                     
                     
The  term “unrelated person” means a purchaser who does not bear a relationship  to
                        the eligible seller described in section 267(b). 


I.R.C. § 9701(c)(A)(C) Eligible Seller — 
                     
                     
For  purposes of this paragraph, the term “eligible seller” means an assigned  operator
                        described in section 9704(j)(2) or a related person to such  assigned operator.
I.R.C. § 9701(d) Enactment Date — 
               
               
For  purposes of this chapter, the term “enactment date"
                  means the date of the  enactment of this chapter.
