Sec. 9703. Plan Benefits

I.R.C. § 9703(a) In General — 
               
               
Each  eligible beneficiary of the Combined Fund shall receive— 

I.R.C. § 9703(a)(1)  — 
                  
                  
health  benefits described in subsection (b), and 


I.R.C. § 9703(a)(2)  — 
                  
                  
in  the case of an eligible beneficiary described in subsection (f)(1), death benefits coverage described in subsection (c).
I.R.C. § 9703(b) Health Benefits


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  trustees of the Combined Fund shall provide health care benefits to each  eligible
                     beneficiary by enrolling the beneficiary in a health care  services plan which undertakes
                     to provide such benefits on a prepaid risk  basis. The trustees shall utilize all
                     available plan resources to ensure  that, consistent with paragraph (2), coverage under the managed care  system shall to the maximum extent feasible be
                     substantially the same as  (and subject to the same limitations of) coverage provided
                     under the 1950  UMWA Benefit Plan and the 1974 UMWA Benefit Plan as of January 1,
                     1992. 


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2) Plan Payment Rates


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(A) In General — 
                     
                     
The  trustees of the Combined Fund shall negotiate payment rates with the  health
                        care services plans described in paragraph (1) for each plan year  which are in amounts which— 

I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(A)(i)  — 
                        
                        
vary  as necessary to ensure that beneficiaries in different geographic areas  have
                           access to a uniform level of health benefits; and 


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(A)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
result  in aggregate payments for such plan year from the Combined Fund which do 
                           not exceed the total premium payments required to be paid to the Combined  Fund under
                           section 9704(a) for the plan year, adjusted as provided in subparagraphs (B) and (C). 



I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(B) Reductions — 
                     
                     
The  amount determined under subparagraph (A)(ii) for any plan year shall be  reduced— 

I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(B)(i)  — 
                        
                        
by  the aggregate death benefit premiums determined under section 9704(c) for
                           the plan year, and 


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(B)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
by  the amount reserved for plan administration under subsection (d). 



I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(C) Increases — 
                     
                     
The  amount determined under subparagraph (A)(ii) shall be increased— 

I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(C)(i)  — 
                        
                        
by  any reduction in the total premium payments required to be paid under  section
                           9704(a) by reason of transfers described in section 9705, 


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(C)(ii)  — 
                        
                        
by  any carryover to the plan year from any preceding plan year which— 

I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(C)(ii)(I)  — 
                           
                           
is  derived from amounts described in section 9704(e)(3)(B)(i), and 


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(C)(ii)(II)  — 
                           
                           
the  trustees elect to use to pay benefits for the current plan year, and 



I.R.C. § 9703(b)(2)(C)(iii)  — 
                        
                        
any  interest earned by the Combined Fund which the trustees elect to use to  pay
                           benefits for the current plan year. 




I.R.C. § 9703(b)(3) Qualified Providers — 
                  
                  
The  trustees of the Combined Fund shall not enter into an agreement under  paragraph
                     (1) with any provider of services which is of a type which is  required to be certified
                     by the Secretary of Health and Human Services  when providing services under title
                     XVIII of the Social Security Act  unless the provider is so certified. 


I.R.C. § 9703(b)(4) Effective Date — 
                  
                  
Benefits  shall be provided under paragraph (1) on and after February 1, 1993.
I.R.C. § 9703(c) Death Benefits Coverage


I.R.C. § 9703(c)(1) In General — 
                  
                  
The  trustees of the Combined Fund shall provide death benefits coverage to  each
                     eligible beneficiary described in subsection (f)(1) which is  identical to the benefits provided under the 1950 UMWA Pension Plan or
                     1974 UMWA Pension Plan, whichever is applicable, on July 20, 1992. Such  coverage
                     shall be provided on and after February 1, 1993. 


I.R.C. § 9703(c)(2) Termination Of Coverage — 
                  
                  
The  1950 UMWA Pension Plan and the 1974 UMWA Pension Plan shall each be  amended
                     to provide that death benefits coverage shall not be provided to  eligible beneficiaries
                     on and after February 1, 1993. This paragraph shall  not prohibit such plans from
                     subsequently providing death benefits not  described in paragraph (1).
I.R.C. § 9703(d) Reserves For Administration — 
               
               
The  trustees of the Combined Fund may reserve for each plan year, for use in  payment
                  of the administrative costs of the Combined Fund, an amount not to  exceed 5 percent
                  of the premiums to be paid to the Combined Fund under  section 9704(a) during the plan year.
I.R.C. § 9703(e) Limitation On Enrollment — 
               
               
The  Combined Fund shall not enroll any individual who is not receiving  benefits
                  under the 1950 UMWA Benefit Plan or the 1974 UMWA Benefit Plan as  of July 20, 1992.
I.R.C. § 9703(f) Eligible Beneficiary — 
               
               
For  purposes of this subchapter, the term “eligible beneficiary” means an  individual
                  who— 

I.R.C. § 9703(f)(1)  — 
                  
                  
is a  coal industry retiree who, on July 20, 1992, was eligible to receive, and  receiving,
                     benefits from the 1950 UMWA Benefit Plan or the 1974 UMWA  Benefit Plan, or 


I.R.C. § 9703(f)(2)  — 
                  
                  
on  such date was eligible to receive, and receiving, benefits in either such  plan
                     by reason of a relationship to such retiree.
