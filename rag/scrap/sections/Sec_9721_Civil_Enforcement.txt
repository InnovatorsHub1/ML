Sec. 9721. Civil Enforcement

I.R.C. § 9721(1)  — 
               
               
arising out of an obligation to pay any amount required to be paid by this  chapter;
                  or
I.R.C. § 9721(2)  — 
               
               
arising out of an obligation to pay any amount required by section 402(h)(5)(B)(ii)
